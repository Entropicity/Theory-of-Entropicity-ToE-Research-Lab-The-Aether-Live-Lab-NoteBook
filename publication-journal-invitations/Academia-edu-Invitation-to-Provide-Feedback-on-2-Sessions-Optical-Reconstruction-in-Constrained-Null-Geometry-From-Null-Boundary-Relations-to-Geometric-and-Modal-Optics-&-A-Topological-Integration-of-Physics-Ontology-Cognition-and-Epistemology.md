## Luka Gluvić: Optical Reconstruction in Constrained Null Geometry From Null Boundary Relations to Geometric and Modal Optics

https://www.academia.edu/t/9aVE-Tx5A4Yp-bmYTbV/s/1a01d8b649?source=ai_email 

I have worked toward this paper for a long time. Light has always seemed to me the greatest puzzle of physical reality—not because we cannot measure it with extraordinary precision, but because the single word “light” may conceal several fundamentally different structures.

In Optical Reconstruction in Constrained Null Geometry, I therefore stopped asking how a thing called light travels and asked instead what geometry produces null directions, optical relations, emission, interference, lenses, thermal radiation, and detection.

This has been one of the most demanding parts of the CNG program. I am genuinely delighted to have finally brought it to completion.

And now I hope that someone will read it closely enough to understand what I have tried to show.

## Eduardo D Aydos: Gauge Autopoiesis: A Topological Integration of Physics, Ontology, Cognition, and Epistemology

https://www.academia.edu/t/9aVE-Tx5A4Yp-bmYTbV/s/d68d191692?source=ai_email

The text I am now submitting for discussion is a case study that—acting as a crucial experiment—aims to test the potential of the topological model of "synthesis epistemology" to facilitate a bridging dialogue between philosophy and science; specifically, between metaphysics/ontology and quantum physics. This integrative aspect of epistemological analysis has been a focus of my communicative self-reflection since the development of my doctoral thesis in 1998. However, the support received from Vladimir I. Rogozhin during the publication and discussion of my *Trilogia de Alétheia* (2024), combined with Shan Gao’s invitation to participate in discussions regarding his most recent papers within this academic community, provided the major impetus for me to tackle the challenge of this dialogue at the intersection of their advanced, paradigm-shifting studies. I quickly traversed this bridge, starting with my modest contribution to the discussion of *Failure of Gauge Invariance or Necessity of Quantum Gravity* (Gao, 2026)—which resulted in an initial draft of an epistemic analysis titled *Whole Paradigm, Cosmological Autopoiesis, Gauge Symmetry, and the Triadic Dialectics of Lore: An Integrative Epistemological Analysis of Rogozhin, Maturana, Gao, and Aydos* (2026). I now submit for discussion this expanded, integrative text, which incorporates the research and suggestions developed and received throughout its creation. You are welcome to join this conversation.
