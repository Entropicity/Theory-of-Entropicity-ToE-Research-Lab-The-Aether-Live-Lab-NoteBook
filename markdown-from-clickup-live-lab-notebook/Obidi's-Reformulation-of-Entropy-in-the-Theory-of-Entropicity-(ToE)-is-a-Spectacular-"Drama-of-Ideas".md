By treating entropy as the primary substrate, theories like ToE stop treating this cosmic decay as a "flaw" or a symptom of a dying universe. Instead, decay and dispersion are recognized as the literal metabolism of spacetime. The universe isn't just subject to thermodynamics; it is thermodynamics.
