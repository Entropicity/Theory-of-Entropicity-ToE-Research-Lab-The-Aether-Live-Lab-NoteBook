# The Intellectual Intuition Behind Obidi's Formulation of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) originates from Obidi's attempt to unify several profound but apparently independent developments in information theory, thermodynamics, information geometry, and gravitational physics.

The reasoning may be summarized as follows.

## Step 1. Shannon established that information possesses entropy.

In information theory,

$H=-\sum_i p_i\log p_i$,

defines the entropy associated with a probability distribution.

Entropy is therefore inseparable from information in Shannon's framework.


---

## Step 2. Landauer established that information is physical.

Landauer's principle demonstrates that information processing has unavoidable physical consequences.

Erasing one bit of information requires a minimum energy dissipation

$E_{\min}=k_BT\ln2$.

Information is therefore not merely an abstract mathematical object but is tied to physical processes.


---

## Step 3. Bekenstein and Hawking connected entropy with gravitation.

Black-hole thermodynamics established

$S_{BH}=\frac{k_BA}{4\ell_P^2}$,

revealing a deep relationship between entropy and gravitational geometry.

Entropy is therefore connected with one of the most fundamental geometric objects in General Relativity—the area of horizons.


---

## Step 4. Information possesses geometry.

Information geometry shows that families of probability distributions naturally possess geometric structure through objects such as the Fisher–Rao metric and its quantum analogues.

Thus,

$\text{Information}
\Longrightarrow
\text{Geometry}$.

This is an established mathematical result.


---

## Obidi's Central Inference

The Theory of Entropicity then asks a natural—but highly ambitious—question.

If

* information is entropic (Shannon),

* information is physical (Landauer),

* entropy is intimately related to gravity (Bekenstein–Hawking),

* and information possesses geometry (information geometry),


then might there exist a deeper framework in which these are not separate facts but different manifestations of a single underlying reality?

This is the conceptual starting point of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE).

**Obidi proposes that the missing unifying principle is the Entropic Field.**

Rather than treating entropy as a secondary quantity, ToE elevates entropy to the status of the fundamental ontological field from which information geometry, spacetime geometry, and ultimately gravitation emerge.

## The Guiding Question of ToE

The motivating question behind Obidi's Theory is therefore not

> "Can entropy describe gravity?"



Rather, it is

> "Are the apparently separate discoveries of Shannon, Landauer, Bekenstein, Hawking, Fisher, Rao, Amari, Čencov, and others different projections of a single, deeper entropic structure of reality?"



The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) answers this question by proposing that they are, and by seeking a mathematical framework that unifies these insights through the Entropic Field and the Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP).


---

## One Important Qualification and Clarification 

There is one logical step that must remain clearly identified as Obidi's conjecture rather than an established consequence.

The following are established within their respective domains:

* Shannon: information entropy.

* Landauer: physical cost of information processing.

* Bekenstein–Hawking: entropy–horizon area relation.

* Information geometry: geometric structure of statistical manifolds.


However, the inference

$\boxed{
\text{Entropy has geometry and therefore can generate spacetime geometry and gravity}
}$

is not a theorem of existing physics. It is the novel hypothesis of ToE that the research program of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) seeks to develop and test.
