# What is the Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence (MOC)?

What-is-the-Maxwell-Obidi-Correspondence-(MOC)?.md

The Maxwell-Obidi Reframing (often referred to as the Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence) is a core theoretical framework within the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), an alternative "entropy-first" cosmological model developed by independent researcher John Onimisi Obidi. [1, 2] 

The correspondence establishes a strict mathematical and structural analogy between James Clerk Maxwell’s 1865 derivation of electromagnetism and the equations governing Obidi's proposed universal entropic field. [2, 3] 

## The Core Concept
The **Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence (MOC)** reinterprets standard field theory by asserting that electromagnetic waves are actually entropic phase waves, and the speed of light (c) is fundamentally an entropic speed limit rather than an axiomatic geometric postulate. Instead of light travelling through a vacuum at a hardcoded speed, c is derived as the maximum rate at which the underlying entropic manifold can physically rearrange itself. [3, 4, 5] 

## Structural Echoes: Electromagnetism vs. Entropicity
In classical electromagnetism, the universal speed of light is fixed by the ratio of two vacuum constants: [6] 

* ε₀ (vacuum permittivity / capacity)

* μ₀ (vacuum permeability / resistance)

The Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence mirrors this setup by introducing two fundamental properties of the entropic field: [3, 6] 

### * Entropic Capacity (C₀): 
Measures how much entropy a local region of space can store or resist.

### * Entropic Conductivity (χ₀): 
Measures how freely or readily entropy flows through the field. [6] 

Just as Maxwell mathematically unified electric and magnetic constants to predict the speed of electromagnetic waves ($c = 1/\sqrt{\mu_0\varepsilon_0}$), the Maxwell-Obidi derivation shows that: 

> # The speed of light emerges naturally from the ratio of **entropic stiffness (κ)** and **entropic inertia ($\rho_S$).** [3, 7, 8] 

## Key Physical Implications

   ### 1. Subsuming Classical 
   Electrodynamics: According to papers published on platforms like [Cambridge Open Engage](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6a02cd7ed1922e37d564c347), classical electrodynamics is not a standalone law of nature but a special, localized case of a broader entropic framework. [3, 7] 
   
   ### 2. The Origin of Relativity: 
   Relativistic phenomena like time dilation and Lorentz length contraction are explained as physical distortions or "stiffening" within the entropic field. As an object accelerates, local entropic resistance increases, slowing down internal physical processes. [5] 
   
   ### 3. Connection to Black Hole Thermodynamics: 
   The entropic parameters (κ and $\rho_S$) are mathematically aligned with the **Bekenstein–Hawking–Unruh (BHU)** thermodynamic framework, creating a bridge between local wave propagation and quantum black hole physics. [8]
