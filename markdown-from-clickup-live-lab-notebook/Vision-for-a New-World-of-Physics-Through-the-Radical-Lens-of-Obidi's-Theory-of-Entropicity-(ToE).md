Specifically, his theory attempts to do the following:


## Derive Spacetime and Matter from Entropy: 
Instead of assuming that space, time, and mass are the primary containers and building blocks of reality, Obidi argues that space, time, and gravity emerge as consequences of the gradients, flows, and geometry of an underlying universal entropic field. [3] 

## Unify Information Geometry and Physics: 
He reinterprets the mathematical tools used to measure statistical and quantum distinguishability (such as Fisher–Rao and Fubini–Study metrics) not just as abstract math for probability, but as the pre-spacetime geometric substance of reality. [4] 

## Bridge Quantum Mechanics and Relativity: 
Through proposed mechanisms like the [Obidi Action](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1) and the Master Entropic Equation, he aims to create a cohesive framework where classical general relativity and quantum behavior naturally arise as macroscopic limits of underlying entropic dynamics. [5, 6] 

## Incorporate Irreversibility at the Foundation: 
By placing entropy at the very bottom of the theoretical ladder, he attempts to permanently bake the arrow of time, causality, and irreversibility directly into the foundational grammar of physics rather than treating time as a symmetric background coordinate. [7]

In his Theory of Entropicity (ToE), John Onimisi Obidi is trying to completely flip the foundational assumptions of modern physics by making entropy—rather than space, time, or mass/energy—the primary, fundamental field from which all physical reality emerges.


## Core Goals of the Theory

### Reversing Ontology: 
Instead of treating entropy as a passive measure of disorder or a byproduct of thermodynamics, Obidi aims to show it is the active causal substrate of existence.


### Deriving Spacetime: 
He attempts to prove that space, time, and gravity are not fundamental a priori stages, but rather emergent "shadows" or geometrical projections driven by underlying entropy gradients and information geometry.


### Unifying Physics: 
He seeks to bridge the gap between general relativity and quantum mechanics by re-anchoring both within a single informational and entropic framework via variational principles like the Obidi Action.


### Reinterpreting Constants: 
He redefines physical limits, such as framing the speed of light (c) not as an arbitrary cosmic rule, but as the maximum upper bound at which the entropic field can reorganize itself [the "No-Rush" Theorem (NRT)].
