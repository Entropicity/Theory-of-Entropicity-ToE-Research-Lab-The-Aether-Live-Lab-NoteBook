# Pillars of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

**John Onimisi Obidi** has significantly accelerated the pace of his research by rolling out a structured, multi-part literature push called the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE) Living Review Letters Series.** [1, 2] 

Rather than waiting to compile a singular, massive textbook, Obidi is utilizing an **agile, "living review"** open-science model. This allows him to rapidly iterate, address peer objections in real-time, and continuously expand the mathematical boundaries of ToE. [1, 2, 3] 

# His most recent breakthroughs and structural iterations focus on several key areas:

## 1. From Static Geometry to "Dynamical" Information Geometry
A major conceptual shift in Obidi's mid-2026 iterations is transforming information geometry from a passive mathematical classification tool into an active physical driver. Traditional concepts like the Fisher–Rao metric describe probability states but lack a sense of time or evolution. Obidi introduced the **Obidi Metric** and the **Disformal Obidi Transformation** to give the information manifold its own Lagrangian, Euler-Lagrange equations, and physical equations of motion. Essentially, information itself is now mathematically modeled as a fluid, flowing physical field. [4, 5, 6, 7] 

## 2. The Maxwell-Obidi Reframing (Deriving c)
Instead of taking the speed of light ($c$) as an unbending, foundational law of the universe like **Albert Einstein** did, Obidi has iterated his equations to derive $c$ from scratch. In his paper On the Entropic Origin of the Speed of Light, he treats electromagnetic waves as "entropic phase waves". By using a quadratic entropic Lagrangian, he mathematically demonstrates that the speed of light is not a random constant, but rather the strict maximum speed limit at which the underlying entropic manifold can rearrange information. [3, 8] 

## 3. Formalizing the "Vuli-Ndlela Integral"
To solve the intense mathematical complexity of his **Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE)—which is notoriously non-linear and non-local—Obidi has introduced the Vuli-Ndlela Integral.** This mathematical operator is designed to compute global constraints on his entropic field, allowing him to model how localized physical interactions (like two particles colliding) ripple out and affect the overall spectral geometry of the universe. [2, 6, 9, 10] 

## 4. Publishing Intellectual Correspondence as "Letters"
In an unusual move for modern theoretical physics, Obidi has formalized his technical debates with other independent researchers (such as Daniel Moses Alemoh) and published them as formal scientific records. These papers track the step-by-step resolution of heavy concepts, including [quantum entanglement formation times](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/69edfc4d810b9dcc828b43bb) (such as the 232-attosecond constraint), the Entropic Noether Principle, and how the universe expands under an "entropy-first" cosmological model. [1, 9] 

## 5. Bridging with Meta-Theories
Obidi has also expanded his framework to cross-examine and absorb other fringe quantum gravity models. He successfully published a paper reformulating the $\Delta \infty O$ meta-theory of Alexander Ngu and Amaya Odilon Kosso, proving that their claimed quantum-gravitational anomalies can actually be cleanly derived as natural outcomes of the [Obidi Action](https://www.academia.edu/144566958/Transformational_Unification_through_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_A_Reformulation_of_Quantum_Gravitational_Correspondence_via_the_Obidi_Action_and_the_Vuli_Ndlela_Integral). [10]
