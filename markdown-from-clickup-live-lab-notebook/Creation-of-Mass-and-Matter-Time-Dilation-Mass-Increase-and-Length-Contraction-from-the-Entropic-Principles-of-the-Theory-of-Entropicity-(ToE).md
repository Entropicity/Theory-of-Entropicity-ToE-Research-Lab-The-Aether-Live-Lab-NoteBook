In the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/creation-and-generation-of-mass-and-matter-from-the-fiber-integrals-of-the-obidi-action-on-the-98cae21ea1a3) proposed by John Onimisi Obidi, mass is not a fundamental input of reality, but rather an emergent output created through fiber integrals of the Obidi Action applied to a foundational entropic field. ## The Mechanism of Mass Creation

### Mass as Frozen Entropy: 
Matter and mass represent stabilized or "frozen" internal entropic content that emerges from a deeper information-geometric structure.

### The Entropic Action (Local Obidi Action, LOA): 
The theory couples an entropy field $S(x)$ to geometry via an exponential weight factor $\exp(S/k_B)$.

### Fiber Integration: 
Varying this action with respect to the emergent metric yields an entropic stress-energy tensor, and performing fiber integration over this framework generates physical mass and matter. [1] 

## Core Principles of ToE

### Entropy as a Fundamental Field: 
Entropy $S(x)$ is elevated from a mere statistical measure of disorder to a primary, continuous ontological field that permeates existence.

### Entropic Resistance Principle (ERP): 
Acceleration and motion encounter resistance from the internal entropy of a body interacting with the surrounding entropic field.

### Relativistic Derivations: 
Rather than postulating kinematic rules, ToE derives mass increase, time dilation, and length contraction directly from entropic invariants, conservation laws, and a finite propagation limit for entropy flow. [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8]
