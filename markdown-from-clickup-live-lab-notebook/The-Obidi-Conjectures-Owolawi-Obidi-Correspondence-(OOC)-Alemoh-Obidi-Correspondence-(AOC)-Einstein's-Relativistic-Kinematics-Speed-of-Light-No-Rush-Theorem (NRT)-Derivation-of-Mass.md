# What are the Obidi Conjectures?

The Obidi Conjectures are the three foundational, axiomatic claims of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) , a theoretical framework proposed by independent researcher John Onimisi Obidi. The theory radically proposes that entropy is the fundamental, primitive field of the universe, from which all matter, energy, and even spacetime itself emerge, rather than being a derived statistical measure.

📜 The Three Core Conjectures

The framework is built on three interlocking claims:

· First Conjecture (The Obidi Conjecture): Asserts that entropy is a genuine, dynamical physical field, S(x). This field is the primary entity of existence, and everything else—matter, forces, and spacetime—is secondary and emergent from its dynamics.

· Second Conjecture (The Obidi Correspondence Principle, OCP): States that all physical laws and interactions (gravity, quantum mechanics, etc.) must be derivable from the entropic field. It also requires that any new formulation must reproduce established theories (like Einstein's equations) in the appropriate limits.

· Third Conjecture (The Obidi Equivalence Principle, OEP): Proposes that physical spacetime is not fundamental but an emergent geometry extruded from a deeper "entropic-informational manifold" (using structures like Fisher–Rao geometry). What we perceive as spacetime curvature is a macroscopic shadow of this underlying information organization.

The Entropic Obidi Action induces a probability distribution over these fiber states. Obidi then uses fiber integrals to extract macroscopic quantities from this distribution.

The crucial step is the second central moment (or covariance) of this distribution, which measures the magnitude and correlations of internal entropic fluctuations.

⚖️ From Fluctuations to Mass

· Uncontracted Moment → Stress-Energy: The full, uncontracted second moment integral yields the precursor of the spacetime stress-energy tensor (the right-hand side of Einstein’s equations).

In essence, mass is the invariant “size” of the internal entropic fluctuations when projected into spacetime.

This reverses the standard view. Rather than matter existing independently and curving spacetime, the fundamental entropic field generates mass-energy first, which then shapes emergent geometry. Mass is described as “frozen entropy”—stabilized internal entropic content that appears as a particle.

# How did Obidi derive Einstein's Relativistic Kinematics of time dilation, mass increase and length contraction 

Within Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity, Einstein’s relativistic kinematics are not derived from the postulates of a fixed spacetime geometry. Instead, they are reconstructed as thermodynamic consequences of a finite “entropic budget”—the maximum rate at which the fundamental entropic field can process or update a system’s information.

⚙️ The Core Mechanism: The Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP)

The derivation rests on the Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP). When an object moves, the entropic field must continually “recompute” its state. This consumes a portion of the system’s total entropic update capacity. The faster the object moves, the more capacity is diverted to maintaining its translation, leaving less available for internal processes like timekeeping or sustaining spatial extent.

📐 The Entropic Lorentz Factor

Mathematically, if a system has a total entropic capacity saturated at rate c, moving at speed v allocates a fraction v/c to translation. The residual capacity available for internal processes is proportional to \sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}. This directly yields the entropic Lorentz factor:

\gamma_e = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}

Each relativistic effect then follows as a simple consequence of this budget depletion.

🔍 The Three Kinematic Effects

· Time Dilation: A moving system’s internal clock runs on the residual entropic budget. Since less capacity is available for internal reconfiguration, its proper time interval expands: \Delta t' = \gamma_e \Delta t.

· Length Contraction: Spatial extent is interpreted as entropic distinguishability along the direction of motion. As the budget for sustaining this distinguishability depletes, the measured length contracts: L' = L/\gamma_e.

· Relativistic Mass Increase: Mass is understood as entropic resistance to reconfiguration. As velocity approaches c, the entire budget is consumed by translation, leaving no capacity to reconfigure the internal state. This manifests as an effective inertial mass that diverges: m' = \gamma_e m_0.

⚠️ Status of the Derivation

Obidi’s own monograph acknowledges that this is currently a reconstruction: it recovers the Lorentz factor by identifying the entropic budget fraction with its algebraic form, but the quadratic structure (1 - v^2/c^2) is imposed by the budget-allocation ansatz rather than independently derived from the deeper Master Entropic Equation (MEE).
