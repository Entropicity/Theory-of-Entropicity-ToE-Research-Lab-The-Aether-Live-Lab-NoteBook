# The Works and Legacy of John Onimisi Obidi: His Scientific Contributions 

The-Works-and-Legacy-of-John-Onimisi-Obidi-His-Scientific-Contributions.md

[John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g2_n9fsg&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi) is an independent researcher best known as the creator and pioneer of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). His academic papers and monographs treat entropy not merely as a measure of disorder, but as the fundamental physical field (S(x)) from which spacetime geometry, matter, gravity, and quantum probabilities emerge. [1](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Obidi-4), [2](https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-obidi-transformation-and-the-obidi-metric-in-modern-theoretical-physics-from-innovations-of-the-b9219bd51149), [4](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55)

His core foundational works, heavily hosted across open research repositories like Cambridge Open Engage and OSF, include: [1](https://osf.io/d7pky/overview), [2](https://client.prod.orp.cambridge.org/engage/coe/search-dashboard?authors=John%20Onimisi%20%20Obidi)
