## Core Contributions & The Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Obidi's work centers on flipping the traditional hierarchy of physics by positioning the entropic field—rather than matter or predefined geometric spacetime—as the primary substrate from which the physical universe emerges. His theoretical architecture includes several core components: [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nagorska_media-attachment-activity-7508506721657724928-YLFe), [2](https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605)

### • The Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE) & Obidi Action: 
The foundational variational centerpieces of ToE. The Obidi Action aims to achieve for the entropic field what the Einstein–Hilbert Action achieves for spacetime curvature, deriving relativity, quantum mechanics, and thermodynamic constraints as entropic inevitabilities. [1](https://www.scilit.com/scholars/29520323), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0ddd9c97504c)

### • Information Geometry to Spacetime: 
His work leverages information geometry (incorporating elements like the Fisher–Rao metric, Fubini–Study metric, and Amari–Čencov α-connections) to show how stable four-dimensional spacetime and gravitational dynamics mathematically emerge from an underlying informational-entropic framework. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p7JYV4GOdaY), [2](https://www.scilit.com/scholars/29520323)

### • Reimagining the Cosmological Constant (Λ): 
Within ToE, the cosmological constant (associated with dark energy) is derived fundamentally from the coarse-grained variance of entropy gradients, explaining background cosmic acceleration without the need for fine-tuned or ad-hoc fields. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpUk5pH739w)

### • Causality & The Speed of Light: 
Under this framework, the speed of light (c) is reinterpreted not just as a velocity constant for photons, but as the absolute maximum rate at which the universal entropic field can reorganize itself. [1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPCN21jA9lk), [2](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311)

## How Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) Pushes the Extreme

### • From Metric to Substrate: 
In classical and quantum physics, you must first define a spacetime background or a quantum Hilbert space before you can calculate entropy. ToE flips this entirely. The entropic field is the canvas. Spacetime geometry and physical matter are ripples formed by the flow, gradients, and curvature of this primary field. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-spectral-action-principle-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-application-of-the-araki-d5a25f14a139), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/a-brief-critical-review-of-john-onimisi-obidis-recent-paper-on-the-conceptual-and-mathematical-22ff55999854)

### • The Inversion of Information: 
In standard modern physics (such as Wheeler's "It from bit"), information is often considered the fundamental building block. Obidi introduces a stark structural asymmetry: entropy is the fundamental field, and information is simply the geometric structure induced by it. [1](https://johnobidi.substack.com/p/authors-preface-and-methodological-ef9)

### • The Obidi Curvature Invariant $\(\ln 2\)$: 
Instead of viewing $\ln 2\)$ as just a mathematical artifact of binary bits, ToE derives it via the Spectral Obidi Action as a literal physical constant. It represents the minimal geometric curvature threshold required for two distinct states to be physically distinguishable in the universe.
