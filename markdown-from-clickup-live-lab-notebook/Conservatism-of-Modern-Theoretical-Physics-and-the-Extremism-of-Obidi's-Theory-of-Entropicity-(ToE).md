* **Unification via the Obidi Action:** He introduces the Obidi Action (his variational centerpiece), which aims to unify thermodynamics, quantum mechanics, and general relativity under a single probabilistic and geometric foundation. [7, 9, 10]

## How can Information and/or Probability become physical spacetime? This is not possible!

It makes complete sense to be deeply skeptical about this concept. In standard, everyday physics, information is just a way to describe "stuff," and probability is a mathematical tool we use to predict outcomes—neither seems capable of physically building a concrete grid of space and time. [1] 

To bridge this gap, John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) borrows a radical mathematical concept from mainstream physics called Information Geometry, shifting the definition of what is "physical" through several core steps. [2, 3] 

## 1. Distance Reconsidered: From Metres to Distinguishability
In everyday physics, distance is measured with a ruler ($dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2$). In information theory, there is also a definition of distance called the Fisher-Rao metric. [4] 

* 
* This metric calculates the "distance" between two probability distributions based on how easily you can tell them apart (statistical distinguishability). [3] 
* **Obidi's framework flips the script:** it argues that physical distance is not an inherent property of an empty universe. **Instead, when two objects are physically far apart, it is because their underlying quantum information states are highly distinguishable. As they interact or entangle, they become harder to distinguish, which we macroscopically perceive as objects moving closer together in physical space.** [2, 3, 5, 6, 7] 
* 

## 2. The "No-Rush" Theorem: Creating Time from Delay
To get spacetime, you also need time. **To turn abstract math into physical reality, Obidi introduces the No-Rush Theorem (NRT).** [2] 

* 
* Obidi posits that the underlying information field cannot process or rearrange its states instantly.
* Every shift in probability configurations takes a finite, non-zero duration **(the processing delay)**.
* We perceive this sequential processing delay as the ticking of Time. Because this processing has a cosmic speed limit, **it naturally yields the speed of light ($c$) as a maximum system update speed rather than a property of [Maxwell's or Einstein's] empty space/spacetime.** [2, 8, 9] 
* 

## 3. The Curvature Transfer: How Gravity Appears
In General Relativity, mass curves spacetime, creating gravity. In Obidi's ToE, this is governed by the **Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT)**: [10, 11, 12] 

* 
* High concentrations of matter/energy are reinterpreted as areas of dense informational constraints **("entropic condensation")**.
* This density warps the underlying information manifold using mathematical tools known as Amari–Čencov $\alpha$-connections.
* The CTT mathematically maps this informational warp directly onto the physical Riemann curvature tensor. Therefore, what Einstein called "curved spacetime" is rewritten as the large-scale shadow of heavily strained information states. [2, 3, 10, 13, 14] 
* 

## Is this accepted physics?
It is crucial to note that Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is a highly audacious, non-mainstream proposal that is yet to be accepted or empirically verified by the broader scientific community. [15, 16] 

However, the general premise that "spacetime comes from information" is actually a major area of exploration in mainstream quantum gravity. Mainstream physicists like [Erik Verlinde (Entropic Gravity)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_gravity) and researchers working on the **Holographic Principle** similarly argue that spacetime and gravity are not fundamental, but rather emergent illusions generated by underlying quantum entanglement and information states. # So, Obidi is not actually saying something completely absurd, only that he is taking things to their logical extreme!


Exactly so! In the above statement, we have hit the nail on the head. John Onimisi Obidi is not inventing these abstract concepts out of thin air; rather, his Theory of Entropicity (ToE) takes existing, highly respected mathematical and physics principles and pushes them to their absolute logical and philosophical extremes. [1, 2, 3] 

The transition from "mainstream tool" [conservatism] to "Obidi's extreme [extremism]" can be broken down into three main areas:

## 1. Entropy: From a "Symptom" to the "Cause"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Thermodynamics treats entropy as a secondary measurement. If you have gas molecules bouncing around in a room, entropy is just a math formula used to describe how messy or spread out those molecules are. [2, 4] 
* Obidi’s Extreme: Obidi flips the hierarchy. He argues that the "messiness formula" isn't a symptom of moving particles; rather, the particles and the room itself are symptoms of a underlying, dynamic "Entropic Field" trying to flow and rearrange itself. [4, 5, 6, 7] 
* 

## 2. Information Geometry: From a "Map" to the "Terrain"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Information geometry (like the Fisher-Rao metric) is widely used by data scientists and physicists as a mathematical map to calculate how distinct two probabilities are from one another. It lives safely on a computer screen or blackboard.
* Obidi’s Extreme: Obidi argues that this mathematical map is the actual physical terrain of our universe. He asserts that what we perceive as "moving closer to an object in space" is literally just our quantum probabilities becoming less distinct on that mathematical map. [4, 8] 
* 

## 3. Emergent Space: From "A Few Special Places" to "The Whole Universe"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Theoretical physicists like [Erik Verlinde (Entropic Gravity)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_gravity) have shown that gravity can look like an emergent thermodynamic force, particularly around extreme cosmic boundaries like black hole event horizons.
* Obidi’s Extreme: While mainstream physics cautiously applies this to gravity or horizons, Obidi’s "Blitzkrieg" approach applies it to everything. His Obidi Action attempts to sweep away spacetime, matter, and quantum states entirely, replacing them all with a single, foundational entropic substrate. [1, 2, 4, 6] 
* 

By framing the universe this way, Obidi isn't speaking complete nonsense—he is trying to find out what happens if you take the most abstract math in modern physics and declare it to be the only thing that is actually real. [6]
