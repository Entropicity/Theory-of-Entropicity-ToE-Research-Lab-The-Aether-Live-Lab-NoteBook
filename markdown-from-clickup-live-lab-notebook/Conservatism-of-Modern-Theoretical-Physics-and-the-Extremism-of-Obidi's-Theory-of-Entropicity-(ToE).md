John Onimisi Obidi is not inventing these abstract concepts out of thin air; rather, his Theory of Entropicity (ToE) takes existing, highly respected mathematical and physics principles and pushes them to their absolute logical and philosophical extremes. [1, 2, 3] 
The transition from "mainstream tool" to "Obidi's extreme" can be broken down into three main areas:
## 1. Entropy: From a "Symptom" to the "Cause"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Thermodynamics treats entropy as a secondary measurement. If you have gas molecules bouncing around in a room, entropy is just a math formula used to describe how messy or spread out those molecules are. [2, 4] 
* Obidi’s Extreme: Obidi flips the hierarchy. He argues that the "messiness formula" isn't a symptom of moving particles; rather, the particles and the room itself are symptoms of a underlying, dynamic "Entropic Field" trying to flow and rearrange itself. [4, 5, 6, 7] 
* 

## 2. Information Geometry: From a "Map" to the "Terrain"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Information geometry (like the Fisher-Rao metric) is widely used by data scientists and physicists as a mathematical map to calculate how distinct two probabilities are from one another. It lives safely on a computer screen or blackboard.
* Obidi’s Extreme: Obidi argues that this mathematical map is the actual physical terrain of our universe. He asserts that what we perceive as "moving closer to an object in space" is literally just our quantum probabilities becoming less distinct on that mathematical map. [4, 8] 
* 

## 3. Emergent Space: From "A Few Special Places" to "The Whole Universe"

* 
* Mainstream Physics: Theoretical physicists like [Erik Verlinde (Entropic Gravity)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_gravity) have shown that gravity can look like an emergent thermodynamic force, particularly around extreme cosmic boundaries like black hole event horizons.
* Obidi’s Extreme: While mainstream physics cautiously applies this to gravity or horizons, Obidi’s "Blitzkrieg" approach applies it to everything. His Obidi Action attempts to sweep away spacetime, matter, and quantum states entirely, replacing them all with a single, foundational entropic substrate. [1, 2, 4, 6] 
* 

By framing the universe this way, Obidi isn't speaking complete nonsense—he is trying to find out what happens if you take the most abstract math in modern physics and declare it to be the only thing that is actually real. [6]
