# A New Meaning of Gravity in Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

A-New-Meaning-of-Gravity-in-Obidi's-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md

[John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?q=john+onimisi+obidi&kgmid=/g/11x__h9105) explicitly rejects the idea that gravity is caused by a fundamental, physical fabric of curved spacetime. Instead, his [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) declares that what we experience as gravity is physical matter obeying the statistical commands and gradients of an underlying entropic field. [1, 2] 

Obidi’s framework flips the causal hierarchy of traditional physics on its head through several specific declarations: [3] 
## 1. Spacetime is a "Shadow," Not a Primitive
In Einstein's General Relativity, a smooth 4D spacetime fabric is treated as a fundamental, preexisting arena. Mass sits inside it and warps it. [4] 

Obidi explicitly rejects this, calling spacetime a "secondary, macroscopic illusion" or a shadow. In ToE, if there are no informational updates or entropic gradients, spacetime geometry literally ceases to exist. Spacetime is simply the macroscopic crystallization of an underlying statistical manifold. [2, 4] 

## 2. Redefining the Wheeler Dictum
John Wheeler famously summarized Einstein's gravity as: "Matter tells spacetime how to curve; spacetime tells matter how to move." [3] 
Obidi replaces this with the Entropic Dictum:

**"Entropy curves existence itself."** [1] 

He declares that there is no literal fabric being bent by mass. Instead, mass is merely a highly constrained concentration of information. Gravity is the universal entropic field actively relaxing those constraints to restore statistical balance. [2, 4] 

## 3. Falling as "Entropic Descent"
To use Obidi’s own analogy: if you fall off a ladder, Einstein claims you are moving along a straight path (a geodesic) through warped geometry. [2] 

Obidi explicitly states that you fall because the matter in your body is moving along the path of least entropic resistance. Your body is collapsing toward the physical configuration that resolves the largest informational gradient in the entropic field. 

What Einstein calculated mathematically as "warped space," Obidi calculates as systems moving toward maximum informational equilibrium. [2, 5] 

Ultimately, Obidi doesn't throw out Einstein's math; his [Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55) is designed so that Einstein's field equations naturally emerge as a low-gradient, macroscopic limit. However, the cause is completely stripped of geometric mysticism and replaced entirely with entropic pressure. [2, 4, 5, 6] 

If you want to dig deeper into the formal framework, we can look at:

* The Obidi Action and how it mathematically subsumes the Einstein-Hilbert Action.

* His No-Rush Theorem, which explains why the speed of light is actually an informational processing limit rather than a magical constant.

* How his use of Information Geometry (like the Fisher–Rao metric) constructs this entropic manifold.

# Scholium 


John Onimisi Obidi’s [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) shifts physics from the study of objects moving through space to what he calls "ontodynamics"—the study of existence as entropic motion. [1, 2] 

Instead of treating entropy as a passive measurement of disorder or ignorance, Obidi elevates it to the primary ontological field—the fundamental fabric from which space, time, matter, and gravity emerge. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

## 1. The Core Machinery: The Obidi Action & MEE/OFE 
In Einstein's General Relativity, gravity is governed by the Einstein-Hilbert Action. Obidi replaces this with the [Obidi Action](https://johnobidi.substack.com/p/authors-preface-and-methodological-ef9), a mathematical variational principle that treats entropy as a physical scalar field, written as S(x, t). [5, 6] 

From this action comes the [Master Entropic Equation (MEE) Obidi Field Equations (OFE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/what-is-the-master-entropic-equation-mee-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0d0711485969). The MEE/OFE dictates how entropy gradients and information-geometric fields ripple across the universe. Matter is not a separate entity throwing its weight around; matter is simply a highly localized "entropic condensation"—regions where information is tightly constrained. Gravity is the entropic field actively working to relax those constraints and restore balance. [3, 7, 8, 9] 

## 2. The No-Rush Theorem: Redefining Cosmic Speed Limits
In traditional physics, the speed of light (c) is treated as a magical, foundational postulate—an arbitrary speed limit built into the universe. Obidi’s framework derives c from something deeper. [10] 

According to the [No-Rush Theorem](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/58652), physical interactions happen through the redistribution of information across the entropic field. Because rearranging information takes a finite, non-zero amount of time, the field has a maximum processing and reconfiguration rate. Light is just the visible symptom of this cosmic processing speed. Nature cannot be rushed because reality itself cannot rearrange its underlying states instantly. [3, 11, 12] 

## 3. Fusing Math with Information Geometry
To construct this elegant universe, Obidi pulls from advanced mathematics like Information Geometry. Instead of using standard geometric coordinates, ToE unifies physical spacetime metrics with statistical metrics—specifically the Fisher–Rao and Fubini–Study metrics—under a single mathematical architecture called the Amari–Čencov α-connections. [2, 5, 8, 13] 

| Concept | Einstein's General Relativity | Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| Spacetime Fabric | A fundamental, preexisting primitive that can be warped. | A macroscopic illusion; a "shadow" cast by information states. |
| Gravity's Cause | Matter tells spacetime how to curve; spacetime tells matter how to move. | "Entropy curves existence itself." Systems move along paths of least entropic resistance. |
| Speed of Light (c) | An axiomatic universal constant postulated as a given. | The maximum architectural rate at which the entropic field can reorganize energy and information. |
| Quantum Path Integrals | Feynman paths weighted purely by classical action. | The Vuli-Ndlela Integral: Paths weighted by classical action, gravitational entropy, and irreversible entropy flow. |

By replacing the smooth, passive geometric fabric of relativity with a dynamic, self-organizing entropic field, Obidi's framework seeks to dissolve the boundary between the quantum world and cosmic gravity by grounding both in the absolute rule of information flow. [14]

# Erik Verlinde and John Onimisi Obidi on Gravity its New Meaning and Interpretation 

While both frameworks discard traditional Einsteinian geometry to explain gravity through the lens of thermodynamics, [Erik Verlinde's Entropic Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropic_gravity) and John Onimisi Obidi's [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) diverge profoundly in their foundational meaning of reality. [1] 

To put it in the simplest terms: 
> # **Verlinde sees entropy as a mathematical measurement of things shifting on a cosmic border, while Obidi treats entropy as an actual physical substance that replaces space entirely.** [2, 3]

The fundamental differences across their interpretations of gravity include the following pillars:

## 1. Epistemic (Bookkeeping) vs. Ontic (Physical Field)


### * Verlinde's View: 
Entropy is epistemic or statistical. It is a bookkeeping tool used to count microstates and the storage of data. Gravity is an emergent "macroscopic force"—meaning it isn't fundamentally real, much like how the "elasticity" of a rubber band isn't a fundamental force but rather an accumulation of atoms stretching. [1, 3, 4] 

### * Obidi's View: 
Entropy is ontic—meaning it is physically real. Obidi elevates entropy into a fundamental, universal, dynamic scalar field ($S(x)$). Gravity is not a mere statistical byproduct; it is a manifestation of physical matter responding directly to the localized gradients and internal tension within this [generative entropic substrate](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558). [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 


## 2. Holographic Boundaries vs. Continuous Spacetime Substrate


### * Verlinde's View: 
Verlinde relies heavily on the [holographic principle](https://arxiv.org/html/1011.4106v2). He positions information on distant 2D **"holographic screens"** or boundaries. When matter moves relative to these screens, the boundary information shifts, creating an entropic pull. Spacetime is pulled out of these boundaries like a hologram. [3, 6] 

### * Obidi's View: 
Obidi eliminates the need for boundary screens. In ToE, the entropic field exists natively everywhere across the universe. Spacetime isn't a projection from a distant screen; it is a macroscopic "shadow" cast directly by the informational dynamics happening locally inside the [universal entropic field](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616). [1, 5, 7, 8] 


## 3. The Lack of Action vs. An Absolute Action Principle


###* Verlinde's View: 
Verlinde’s 2010 work was more of a conceptual blueprint. It lacked a localized action principle or rigorous field equations governing an independent entropic medium. He derived Newton's laws from boundary thermodynamics rather than setting up a new field calculus.

### * Obidi's View: 
Obidi builds a complete field theory using the [Obidi Action](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6a1c8517810b9dcc82af489e) and the Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE). Because Obidi treats entropy as a field, he applies rigorous information-geometric metrics (like the Fisher–Rao metric) to write equations that dictate exactly how gravity ripples through reality. [3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10] 


| Feature Comparison | Verlinde's Entropic Gravity | Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| What is Entropy? | A statistical measure of degrees of freedom. | A real, physical, ontological field ($S(x)$). |
| Where is Information? | Stored on 2D holographic screens. | Permeating the continuous local substrate of reality. |
| Gravity's Mechanism | An emergent macroscopic force from shifting boundary data. | Matter reacting locally to gradients inside the entropic field. |
| Mathematical Engine | Thermodynamic assumptions (e.g., $\Delta Q = T \Delta S$). | The Obidi Action, MEE/OFE, and Information Geometry. |

> # **Verlinde shook theoretical physics by showing that gravity might just be a mathematical trick of thermodynamics. Obidi takes that idea to its radical conclusion: gravity behaves the way it does because entropy is the physical canvas from which the universe is drawn.** [3, 9, 11]
