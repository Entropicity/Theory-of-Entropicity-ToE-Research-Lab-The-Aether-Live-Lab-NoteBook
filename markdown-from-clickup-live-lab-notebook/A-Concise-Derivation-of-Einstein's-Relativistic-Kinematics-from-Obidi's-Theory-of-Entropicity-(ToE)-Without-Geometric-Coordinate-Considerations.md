$$\[

$$\[

To match these claims with physical laws, one could formalize the framework as:

$$\[
d\tau \propto dt\left(1 - \frac{S(v)}{S_{\max}}\right)^{\alpha}
\]$$

If  

$$\[
\frac{S(v)}{S_{\max}} \sim 1 - \frac{1}{\gamma},
\]$$

this recovers relativistic time dilation as a limiting case.

Here, $\(\beta\)$ encodes how spatial distribution interacts with entropic constraint in the moving frame.

$$\[

With a suitable mapping $\(k \sim m_0\)$, the Lorentz-factor dependence could be formally reproduced.

## **4. Caveats and Verification Strategy**

### **Mathematical rigor**

The Lorentz factor $\(\gamma\)$ is precisely known. Any entropic mapping must exactly reproduce $\(\gamma\)$ without free parameters tuned post hoc.

### **Edge cases**

- $\(v \to 0\)$ should yield standard proper time, length, and mass.

### **Cross-validation**

One could attempt to derive EAP predictions for time dilation of fast-moving particles and compare with muon decay experiments.
