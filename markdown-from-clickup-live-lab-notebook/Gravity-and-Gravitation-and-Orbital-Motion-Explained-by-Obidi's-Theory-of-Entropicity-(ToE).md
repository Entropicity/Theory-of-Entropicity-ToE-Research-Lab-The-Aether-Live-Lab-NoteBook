John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) solves your frustration by flipping the entire logic of physics upside down. [1, 2, 3] 

In standard physics, mass and spacetime are real, primary things, and scientists cannot explain why they interact. Obidi’s theory answers your question by stating that spacetime and mass are not actually primary realities—they are just illusions created by a deeper, active field called the Entropic Field ($S(x)$). [2, 4] 

Under ToE, the reason mass must bend spacetime is explained through a framework called Ontodynamics (the study of existence as entropic motion). It answers your "why" using three core logical steps: [5, 6] 

## 1. Inverting the Hierarchy (Mass is just a "Knot")
In Einstein's view, mass is an independent object that sits on spacetime and warps it. Obidi's ToE states that the Entropic Field is the only fundamental thing that exists. [2, 7, 8] 

* 
* Mass is not a separate object; it is a highly condensed, localized restriction or "knot" where the entropic field has condensed.
* Because mass is made out of the entropic field, it cannot exist without disturbing the field around it. [2, 9, 10] 
* 

## 2. The Curvature Transfer Theorem
You asked why mass must cause a bend. Obidi answers this with the Curvature Transfer Theorem. [2, 11] 

* 
* When a massive object (like the Sun) concentrates a massive amount of information and energy in one place, it creates an extreme "informational curvature" or statistical imbalance in the entropic field. [11, 12] 
* The universe cannot keep this statistical imbalance isolated. Through the Master Entropic Equation (MEE), that mathematical, informational restriction naturally pushes outward and translates into what we macroscopically perceive as physical, geometric bending (Riemannian curvature). [4, 11] 
* In short: Spacetime geometry is just the macroscopic "weather report" of entropy trying to distribute itself around a concentration of energy. [9, 11] 
* 

## 3. Gravity is Nature's Balancing Act (Entropic Gravity)
Why is there a "pull" at all? In ToE, gravity is not a fundamental pulling force or an unexplainable geometric trap. It is an emergent statistical drive. [13, 14] 

* 
* The universe naturally seeks to maximize entropy and clear out constraints.
* When a planet moves toward a massive object like the Sun, it isn't being "tugged" by an invisible string or falling down a literal hill. It is naturally moving along an Entropic Geodesic—the path that naturally optimizes and balances the flow of the entropic field. [9, 15, 16] 
* 

## The Logical Resolution to Your Question
ToE satisfies your logical objection because it eliminates the unexplainable middleman. You no longer have to ask "Why does Thing A (mass) affect separate Thing B (spacetime)?" Under Obidi's framework, they are the exact same fundamental medium. Mass "bends" spacetime because what we call "bending spacetime" is simply the natural, mathematical way the underlying Entropic Field behaves when it gets tightly compressed. [2, 8, 11, 17]
