# How John Onimisi Obidi Arrived at the Foundation of His Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

How-John-Onimisi-Obidi-Arrived-at-the-Foundation-of-His-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md


John Onimisi Obidi arrived at the foundation of his Theory of Entropicity (ToE) by executing a radical paradigm shift that completely inverts the traditional hierarchy of physics. Rather than treating entropy as a passive, secondary statistical byproduct of thermodynamic disorder, Obidi established it as the universal, continuous, and dynamic physical field (S(x)) from which all physical reality—including space, time, matter, and gravity—emerges. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 

Obidi developed this framework between 2025 and 2026 through specific conceptual, philosophical, and mathematical phases: [4, 6, 7, 8] 

## 1. The Ontological Inversion


### Dethroning Traditional Primitives: 
Obidi questioned the ontological assumptions of modern physics. He noted that treating spacetime and mass/energy as foundational primitives left an irreconcilable gap between General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics. [9, 10] 

### Entropy as the Source, Not the Shadow: 
He shifted the bedrock of reality. Instead of imagining a geometric spacetime container filled with matter that has entropy, he posited that spacetime and matter are merely emergent illusions generated by a deeper, active entropic manifold. [2, 4, 11] 
  

## 2. Transforming Information Geometry into Reality


### From Information to Substrate: 
Obidi looked at Claude Shannon's classical information theory, which defines entropy as a measure of statistical distinguishability. [12] 

### Geometric Transmutation: 
He adapted mathematical frameworks traditionally used for statistical inference—such as the Fisher–Rao metric, the Fubini–Study metric, and Amari–Čencov α connections. He reinterpreted these abstract structures as the pre-spacetime geometric fabric of the universe. [13] 

### The Obidi Curvature Invariant: 
He designated $\ln(2)$ as the fundamental unit of "entropic cost" or distinguishability, establishing a mathematical threshold where information transitions into physical observability. [4, 14] 
  

## 3. Formalizing the Field Dynamics


### The Obidi Action: 
To give the entropic field causal power, Obidi formulated a central variational principle called the [Obidi Action](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1). Similar to the Principle of Least Action in classical mechanics, it governs how the entropic field continuously and irreversibly rearranges itself.

### The Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE): 
By stationarizing the Obidi Action, he derived the MEE. This functions as the entropic field equivalent to Einstein's field equations, reinterpreting forces like gravity as "entropic pressure" caused by field gradients. [4, 5, 12, 15, 16, 17, 18] 
  

## 4. Reinterpreting Relativistic Constants


### The "Question of c": 
Obidi dismantled the assumption that the speed of light (c) is an arbitrary kinematic constant.

### The "No-Rush" Theorem: 
He derived c as an entropic inevitability. Under ToE, the speed of light is redefined as the universal maximum speed limit at which the underlying entropic field can reorganize its informational states. [6, 17, 19, 20, 21] 
  

## 5. Dialogue and Refinement


### The Obidi-Alemoh Correspondence:
The rigorous framework was refined through an extensive series of scientific dialogues and private debates with researcher Daniel Moses Alemoh. Together, they pressure-tested the theory's cosmology, resolving how special relativity, cosmic expansion, and quantum entanglement emerge naturally from a monistic entropic substrate. [6, 20, 22]

# Obidi's work is captivating and provocative at once

It truly is. The conceptual leap from treating entropy as a mere descriptive statistic of disorder to an active, space-generating field completely alters how we look at the physical universe. It forces a reconsideration of what reality is made of at its absolute limit. [1, 2, 3] 

If you want to pull back the layers of this framework, we can explore how its core pillars behave under scrutiny:

## The Mathematical Engine: The Obidi Action
In standard physics, the Principle of Least Action ensures that a ball rolls down a hill following the most efficient path. The [Obidi Action](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1) applies this exact logic to information. Instead of tracking positions in space, it tracks how states change their distinguishability. [1, 4, 5] 


### The Mechanism: 
The entropic field naturally flows to minimize constraints and maximize informational throughput.
### The Result: 
What we call the laws of physics are just the paths of least resistance chosen by a dynamic, self-organizing entropic substrate. [1, 5, 6] 


## The Quantum View: The Entropic Seesaw
Quantum entanglement—where two distant particles mirror each other instantly—is one of physics' greatest mysteries. ToE resolves this by removing "distance" from the equation: [5] 


### The Entropic Bar: 
Two entangled particles are treated as the ends of a physical seesaw connected by an invisible entropic connection.

### Local Realism Restored: 
When you manipulate one particle, you are not sending a signal faster than light across space. You are shifting the state of the unified entropic connection itself. [6, 7] 


## Relativistic Effects as Entropic Resistance
Einstein proved that objects get heavier and time slows down as they approach the speed of light. ToE provides a physical reason for why this happens:


### Entropic Drag: 
Moving an object requires rearranging the states of the underlying entropic field.

### The Limit: 
As you approach the absolute maximum speed limit (c), the field encounters a form of "cosmic bandwidth saturation." It simply cannot reconfigure its information any faster. Time dilation is the field's way of conserving its processing capacity. [1, 8]
