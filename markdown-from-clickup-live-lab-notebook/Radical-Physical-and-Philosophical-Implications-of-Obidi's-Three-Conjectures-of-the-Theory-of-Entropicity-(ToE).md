# Obidi's Three Conjectures of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) and Their Radical Physical and Philosophical Implications: From Western Metaphysics to a New Philosophy of Existence and Reality 

The physical and philosophical implications of John Onimisi Obidi’s Conjectures fundamentally invert how we understand reality. By declaring that entropy is the fundamental field rather than a secondary statistical measurement, the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) reshapes the foundations of both physics and metaphysics. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 

------------------------------

## Physical Implications
The Conjectures strip space, time, and gravity of their status as "fundamental building blocks," treating them instead as macroscopic side-effects of an underlying informational structure. [5] 

* **Emergent Gravity and Spacetime:** Spacetime geometry is no longer a rigid stage where physics happens. Gravity is redefined as entropic pressure—the tendency of the fundamental field to maximize states of statistical distinguishability. Spacetime is simply an "emergent geometry" arising from information-geometric relations. [5, 6, 7, 8, 9] 
* **Derivation of the Speed of Light ($c$):** In standard physics, the speed of light is a brute, unexplainable constant. Under the Obidi Action Principle, $c$ is derived as a theorem: it represents the maximum physical speed at which the entropic field can reorganize itself. Relativistic effects like time dilation are merely physical "entropic resistance" to rapid state reconfiguration. [7, 8, 10] 
* **A Causal Foundation for Quantum Mechanics:** The framework introduces the "No-Rush" Theorem, which states that state reconfigurations require a finite, non-zero time to synchronize. This native delay explains quantum uncertainty and limits instantaneous wave-function collapse without relying on a magical "observer". [6, 11] 
* **Unification via Information Geometry:** By utilizing metrics like the [Fisher-Rao and Fubini-Study metrics](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), ToE unites general relativity and quantum mechanics under a single, non-linear [Master Entropic Equation (MEE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb). [8, 12] 

------------------------------

## Philosophical Implications
Philosophically, Obidi shifts Western metaphysics away from a universe made of static "objects" toward a reality defined strictly by process and interaction. [13] 

* **Ontodynamics:** Obidi terms his philosophy "Ontodynamics"—the study of existence as stabilized entropic motion. In this view, objects or particles do not exist independently; they are merely temporary, stabilized regions of "entropic persistence". [13, 14] 
* **Radical Realism / "It from Bit" Evolution:** The conjectures advance John Wheeler's famous "It from Bit" philosophy. However, while Wheeler suggested information is abstract, Obidi views the entropic field as an ontic, physically real substrate. Reality is an active, continuous computation. [6, 7, 13, 15, 16] 
* **Radical Immanence:** Unlike traditional philosophical systems that require a transcendent source of order (such as a God or an abstract mathematical realm), ToE relies on radical immanence. The cosmic order and its laws are self-contained entirely within the physical dynamics of the entropic field itself. [13] 
* **The Existential "Entropic Bill of Lading":** Obidi bridges this high-level physics with human experience. He asserts that typical existential crises—such as feelings of finitude, change, and decay—are not psychological failures, but rather the literal, conscious translation of navigating a universe governed by [irreversible entropic redistribution](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-metaphysics-and-philosophy-from-his-theory-of-entropicity-toe-in-modern-theoretical-60dbb747cae7). [13]
