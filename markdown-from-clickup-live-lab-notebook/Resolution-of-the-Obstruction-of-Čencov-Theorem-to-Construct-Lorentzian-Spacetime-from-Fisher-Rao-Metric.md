# Resolution of the Obstruction of Čencov Theorem to Construct Lorentzian Spacetime from the Fisher-Rao Sector of Information Geometry in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

No, the indefinite Lorentzian signature is not strictly implicit within the Fisher-Rao metric itself. Instead, John Onimisi Obidi teaches that standard information geometry contains a fundamental mathematical obstruction that must be actively broken to produce physical spacetime. [1] 

According to his papers, such as **From Information Geometry to Information Gravity**, the relationship between the two metrics is governed by a precise mathematical conflict and its subsequent resolution: [1, 2, 3] 

## The Mathematical Obstruction (Čencov’s Theorem)

In standard information geometry, Čencov’s Theorem proves that the Fisher–Rao metric is the unique metric that maintains Markov invariance under sufficient statistics. However, a strict consequence of Čencov’s Theorem is that the Fisher–Rao metric must remain strictly positive-definite (Riemannian). [1, 4] 

Because it is purely positive-definite, the Fisher–Rao metric alone lacks a temporal dimension, causal cones, or an indefinite signature. It is a static, mathematical landscape of probability distributions without any physical dynamics or a time axis. Therefore, the Lorentzian structure of physical spacetime ($+,-,-,-$) cannot simply be extracted from it natively. [1, 5, 6, 7] 
## The Obidi Transformation as "Čencov Breaking"

To bridge this gap, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) introduces a strong physical postulate coupled with a specific mathematical mechanism known as the Obidi Transformation: [1, 8] 

   1. Relaxing the Invariance: Obidi argues that to generate physical reality, we must relax the rigid constraints of Čencov's theorem.
   2. The Disformal Deformation: He applies an entropy-gradient–driven, rank-one disformal transformation to the Fisher–Rao metric:
   $$g_{\mu\nu} = A(S)g_{\mu\nu}^{\text{Fisher}} + B(S)\nabla_{\mu}S\nabla_{\nu}S$$ 
   3. Injecting the Negative Signature: By defining the behavior of the entropic field gradients ($\nabla_{\mu}S$), this transformation actively warps the positive-definite geometry. It stretches the statistical manifold until a negative eigenvalue emerges along the direction of maximum entropic change. [1, 3, 9] 

## The Physical Result: Time as a Processing Delay
Through this transformation, the negative sign in the Lorentzian metric (which dictates the flow of time) is generated by Obidi's "No-Rush" Theorem (NRT). [9, 10] 

Because the underlying entropic field cannot process or rearrange its information states instantly, every sequential shift requires a non-zero duration. The positive-definite informational metric gains its indefinite temporal dimension precisely because physical time is the macroscopic manifestation of this irreversible, microscopic computational processing delay. [9] 

Thus, Obidi teaches that while the building blocks of distance and distinguishability reside in the Fisher–Rao metric, the indefinite Lorentzian signature is an emergent property. It only appears after the Obidi Action drives the statistical manifold through an "entropic condensation" that breaks Čencov invariance. [1, 3, 5, 11, 12]
