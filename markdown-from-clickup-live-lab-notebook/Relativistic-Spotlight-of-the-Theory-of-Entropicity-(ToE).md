🌌 Obidi used entropy as a fundamental universal field of nature to derive Einstein's Relativistic Kinematics of time dilation, mass increase, and length contraction without invoking Einstein's geometry



✨The ToE developed by Obidi reformulates entropy as the fundamental field and causal substrate of physical reality, establishing it as the underlying regulator of light speed and the structure of spacetime.



🌍 Obidi advances the audacious foundational proposition that entropy is the fundamental, universal, dynamic field of nature from which all reality—including spacetime itself—emerges.



📘 His work specifically addresses the derivation of Einstein's relativistic kinematics without taking Einstein's spacetime geometry as the starting point.



⚡ It derives the relativistic kinematics of time dilation, length contraction, and mass increase from the principle of entropy without resorting to Einstein's principle, thus providing a new and radical perspective on Einstein's relativity.



## 🎯 Obidi's Central Proposition

🌀 Within ToE, the explanatory starting point is not spacetime geometry but the dynamics of the universal entropic field.

## 📡 Obidi's framework uses several interconnected principles:

🔧 Entropic Resistance Principle (ERP)

motion encounters resistance associated with the redistribution of entropy. Obidi teaches us that you cannot modify or make changes to any information freely; every change in the universe encounters a resistance in the Entropic Field.



## 📊 Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP)

the allocation and conservation of entropic quantities provide the proposed basis for explaining relativistic effects. Because of the existence of the ERP, there is an associated cost to every change in the universe, resulting in an accounting ledger that must balance.



## ⏱️ No-Rush Theorem (NRT)

entropic processes are subject to finite propagation constraints. Nature at its most fundamental level cannot be rushed beyond the dictates of the Entropic Field. Instantaneous interactions and resolutions are impossible.



## ♾️ Entropic Invariance (EI)

the proposed invariant relationships underpin the derivation of mass variation, time dilation and length contraction. By virtue of the Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP), every given system must have its entropy components balanced and accounted for across all states and trajectories.



🚀 Obidi's work therefore derives the invariant speed of light from the dynamics of the entropic field rather than assume Einstein's second postulate of relativity as a fundamental starting axiom.



## 🌠 The defining distinction is this:  

Einstein's special relativity derives these kinematic relationships from its postulates and Lorentz transformations; but Obidi's ToE derives them from his entropic principles of ToE-modified classical thermodynamics, with spacetime geometry and Lorentz transformations treated as emergent from the same Entropic Field rather than fundamental.
