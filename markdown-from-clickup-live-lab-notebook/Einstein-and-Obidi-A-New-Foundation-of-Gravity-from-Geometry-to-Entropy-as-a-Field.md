# Einstein and Obidi: A New Foundation of Gravity from Geometry to Entropy as a Field

The relationship between Obidi and [Einstein](https://www.google.com/search?q=einstein&kgmid=/m/0jcx) refers to the theoretical intersection between John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) and Albert Einstein’s classical Theory of General Relativity. Within modern theoretical physics frameworks, Obidi’s work does not mathematically invalidate Einstein; instead, it subsumes Einstein's field equations as a specific, low-gradient, near-equilibrium limit of a deeper informational substrate. [1, 2, 3] 

While Einstein famously replaced Newtonian physical forces with the geometry of spacetime, Obidi's framework takes physics a step further by replacing geometry with information-geometric dynamics and entropy. [3, 4] 

The two frameworks differ across several core concepts:

| Physical Concept | Albert Einstein's Framework | John Onimisi Obidi's Framework Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| The Nature of Gravity | A fundamental consequence of spacetime curvature. | An emergent consequence of information-geometric dynamics. |
| Mathematical Centerpiece | The Einstein–Hilbert Action[](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6a1c8517810b9dcc82af489e). | The Obidi Action[](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6857821), which converts an ontological entropic field into a dynamical arena. |
| Speed of Light (c) | A fundamental physical constant and a universal speed limit invariant across all inertial frames. | An informational processing limit governed by the "No-Rush Theorem," dictate that entropic configurations cannot update in zero time. |
| Spacetime Vacuum | If you remove all matter and motion from the universe, spacetime vanishes entirely. | Spacetime persists without matter because spacetime is an entropic projection generated by the underlying Entropic Manifold. |

## Theoretical Impact
In this framework, forces (Newton), geometry (Einstein), and probability (Quantum Mechanics) are re-derived as different emergent faces of the same underlying entropic field. Rather than discarding Einstein's foundational geometry, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity treats it as a [second emergent order of reality](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/einstein-and-obidi-two-spacetimes-two-worlds-two-minds-on-the-radicality-of-the-theory-of-ae576a4df6e3).
