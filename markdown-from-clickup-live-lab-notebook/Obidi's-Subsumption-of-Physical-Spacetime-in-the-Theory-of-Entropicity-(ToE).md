# Obidi's Subsumption of Physical Spacetime in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

In the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) proposed by theoretical physicist John Onimisi Obidi, the "subsumption of physical spacetime" refers to the concept that four-dimensional pseudo-Riemannian spacetime is not a fundamental entity, but an emergent macroscopic projection arising from an underlying informational-statistical manifold. [1, 2] 
Rather than viewing spacetime as a continuous background container where mass and energy interact, Obidi's framework mathematically demotes spacetime to a "shadow" or secondary byproduct of information-geometric dynamics. [2, 3, 4] 

The structural and mathematical breakdown of how this subsumption is achieved includes:

## 1. The Obidi Action Principle vs. Einstein–Hilbert Action
In General Relativity (GR), the Einstein–Hilbert Action dictates how mass-energy curves the physical fabric of spacetime. The [Theory of Entropicity](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6857821) replaces this with the Obidi Action, which is the variational centerpiece of ToE. [5, 6] 

* The Mechanism: It treats a continuous entropy field S(Λ) as the primary field of existence.
* The Subsumption: Through a rigorous mathematical pipeline, Obidi demonstrates that Einstein's field equations are recovered as a low-gradient, near-equilibrium limit of his entropic field equations. Therefore, gravity is mathematically subsumed as a special case of information geometry rather than a fundamental postulate. [4, 5, 6] 

## 2. Information Geometry as the Substratum
Rather than calculating spatial or temporal distances, ToE calculates distances using information metrics. [3] 

* The Metrics: It builds an informational manifold utilizing the Fisher–Rao metric (which measures classical statistical distinguishability) and the Fubini–Study metric (which measures quantum state distinguishability). [3, 5] 
* Curvature Transfer Theorem: Obidi mathematically proves that the Riemannian curvature of physical spacetime is a pushforward of this information-geometric curvature in its thermodynamic limit. Physical curvature is derived directly from how distinguishable different statistical states are from one another. [1, 3, 7] 

## 3. Reinterpretation of Fundamental Constants
By absorbing spacetime into an entropy field, several foundational ideas in classical and relativistic physics are fundamentally re-framed: [8] 

* The Speed of Light (c): Re-framed not as a spatial velocity limit, but as the absolute maximum speed at which the underlying entropic field can rearrange its information. [8] 
* Time and Uncertainty: Time dilation and quantum uncertainty are derived as secondary macro-byproducts of this finite entropic propagation speed, rather than standalone laws of nature. [8] 
* The Arrow of Time: Formulated through the Vuli–Ndlela Integral, an entropy-weighted path-integral that embeds thermodynamic irreversibility directly into the fabric of the informational geometry. [5, 6] 

Ultimately, Obidi's subsumption argues that just as Minkowski proved space and time are inseparable projections of a single spacetime manifold, spacetime itself is merely a projection of a deeper, foundational reality composed entirely of statistical information and entropy gradients. [2, 4]
