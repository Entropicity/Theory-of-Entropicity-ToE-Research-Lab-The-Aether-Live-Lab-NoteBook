# Reformulation of the Ryu-Takayanagi Formula and the Meaning of Entanglement in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)




In Jonimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE), the reformulation of the [Ryu-Takayanagi (RT) formula](https://adscft.org/black-hole-information/holographic-entropy/ryu-takayanagi-formula/) treats holographic geometry as a consequence—rather than the source—of entropic dynamics. [1] 
Instead of sewing spacetime together using discrete qubits or minimal bulk surfaces, Obidi's framework introduces a continuous entropic field $S(x,t)$. In this model, the classic RT area law is structurally absorbed as a macroscopic, boundary-limited projection of the Master Entropic Equation (MEE). [1] 
## Key Shifts in Obidi's Reformulation

* Causality Reversal: The original RT formula dictates that quantum entanglement patterns determine geometric structure. Obidi’s ToE reverses this, positing that geometry is merely the large-scale "shadow" or consequence of an underlying entropic configuration. [1] 
* Continuous Field Dynamics: It replaces static holographic geometry with an active entropic manifold governed by local informational updates and entropic gradients. [1] 
* Emergent Minimal Surfaces: Minimal surfaces do not fundamentally encode correlations; instead, they appear macroscopically because the entropic field’s internal stiffness forces geometry to behave like an area law at the boundary. [1] 

Ultimately, the reformulation generalizes the linearized gravitational limits of holographic pseudo-entropy into a fully non-linear, time-asymmetric foundation, summarized by Obidi's core axiom: “Entropy curves existence.” [1, 2]
