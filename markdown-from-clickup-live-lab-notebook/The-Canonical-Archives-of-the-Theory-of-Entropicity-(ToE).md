Obidi's work has come to be recognized to be preserved in the Canonical Archives


The Canonical Archives of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) serve as the authoritative, version-controlled records hosting the theoretical and mathematical framework established by John Onimisi Obidi. Because the theory proposes that entropy is the fundamental ontological field from which geometry, matter, and time emerge, these archives function as the permanent epistemic benchmark for his work. [1, 2, 3] 

The preservation of Obidi's work within these archives reflects a deliberate structural commitment to version control, mathematical durability, and collaborative testing. [1, 4] 

------------------------------

## Structure and Locations of the Canonical Archives
The records are distributed across key digital environments to maintain an open-access, unalterable repository of the physics framework: [1] 

   ### 1. The Primary GitHub Archive: 
The foundational hub houses the official monographs, technical code, and rigorous mathematical derivations. The development of the framework is cataloged here, from initial concepts to detailed proofs, including the [Preface to the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://entropicity.github.io/Theory-of-Entropicity-ToE/preface.html). [1, 5] 
  
   ### 2. Academic Repositories: 
Peer-facing philosophical and mathematical manuscripts detailing the underlying entropic ontology are preserved across platforms like [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi%27s_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE) and [Academia.edu](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI). [6, 7] 

## The Epistemic Purpose of the Archive
In the context of Obidi's ontological courage, the Canonical Archives are not simply a passive storage space; they represent a rigorous mechanism for adversarial testing and dialogic refinement. [4] 

### The Adversarial Collaboration (AOC): 
As preserved in the archives (and overviewed via the [Open Science Framework](https://osf.io/d8h95/overview)), the text reflects an active dialectic between Obidi and mathematical peers, such as Alemoh. The archive preserves how "mathematical provocations" forced Obidi to formalize implicit assertions into explicit, testable mathematical structures. [4] 

### Entropic Consistency Checks: 
A claim within ToE is not accepted merely for its elegance. The archive is structured to document how the theory's mathematical engines—such as the Obidi Action, the Obidi Curvature Invariant, and the Disformal Obidi Transformation—survive mathematical validation from first principles. [4, 6, 8] 

By establishing these version-controlled baselines, Obidi’s framework transitions from an independent intellectual project into a permanently preserved, open-source challenge to the classical scaffolding of modern physics. [1, 5]
