## 1. Difference from Newtonian physics

In Newtonian mechanics, the basic picture is:

$$\[
F = ma
\]$$

A body changes its velocity because a force acts on it. For example:

# - gravity pulls an apple downward;
# - friction slows a moving object;
# - an applied push accelerates a cart.

The force is treated as a physical cause that can be calculated independently of the object’s resulting motion.

ToE reverses that explanatory order. It would say that what we call a “force” is not necessarily a fundamental external push. Instead, a body moves because the overall entropic configuration makes one route more natural or less resistant than another. The apparent force is then a consequence of the entropy field’s gradient.

**So, for Newton:**

> # Force causes acceleration.

**For Obidi's ToE:**

> # Entropic structure produces the motion, and force is our description of that entropic tendency.

This is a major conceptual difference. ToE embarks upon providing precise equations that reproduce or replace $\(F=ma\)$, so it doesn't remain a proposed reinterpretation but a genuine replacement for Newtonian mechanics.

## 2. Difference from Einsteinian general relativity

The comparison is subtler because Einsteinian physics already rejects the idea that gravity is simply an ordinary force.

## In general relativity:

- mass and energy determine the curvature of spacetime;

- freely falling objects follow geodesics in that curved spacetime;

- gravity is therefore primarily geometry rather than a Newton-like push.

For example, Earth is not necessarily thought of as “pulling” the Moon through a conventional force. 

Earth changes spacetime geometry, and the Moon follows a geodesic in that geometry.

ToE sounds similar because it also uses the word **geodesic**, but it changes what generates the geometry:

- In general relativity, the relevant structure is the **spacetime metric**, determined by the Einstein field equations.
- In ToE, the relevant structure is an **entropic geometry**, determined by entropy, entropy gradients, or an entropic field of the **Master Entropic Equation (MEE) / Obidi Field Equations (OFE).**

Thus:

> # Einstein: matter-energy curves spacetime, and bodies follow spacetime geodesics.

> # ToE: entropy reorganizes an effective geometry, and bodies follow entropic geodesics.

The ToE proposal is therefore closer in spirit to general relativity than to basic Newtonian “push-and-pull” mechanics, but it is declaring that **entropy is more fundamental than spacetime geometry**.

## 3. Difference from the principle of least action

The passage also says that the **Principle of Least Entropic Resistance** replaces the classical least-action principle.

In standard physics, the path of a system can often be derived from an action:

$$\[
\delta S = 0
\]$$

Strictly speaking, the action is usually **stationary**, not always literally at its minimum. Newtonian mechanics, electromagnetism, general relativity, and quantum theory can all be expressed in action-based mathematical forms.

ToE appears to propose something like:

$$\[
\delta R_{\text{entropic}} = 0
\]$$

where $\(R_{\text{entropic}}\)$ would represent some measure of entropic resistance. The system would select a path according to entropy-related criteria rather than according to the conventional action.

For this to become a physical theory, ToE is working on the following to give the general public a better understanding:

1. what “entropic resistance” mathematically means;
2. what variables generate the entropic geometry;
3. the equation governing that geometry;
4. how the theory recovers Newton’s laws in ordinary conditions;
5. how it recovers general relativity in strong-gravity or relativistic conditions;
6. what new, testable predictions distinguish it from existing physics.

## Bottom line

The passage is not merely saying that entropy affects motion, as in ordinary thermodynamics. It is making the stronger claim that:

> Entropy is the underlying source of geometry, apparent forces, and physical trajectories.

**Newtonian physics says bodies are accelerated by forces.**  

**Einsteinian physics says free bodies follow geodesics of curved spacetime.** 

**Obidi/ToE says bodies follow geodesics of an entropy-generated geometry, while forces and perhaps even geometry itself are emergent from the same Entropic Field (EF).**

That is a substantial philosophical and theoretical proposal and needs experimental evidence to establish it as a successor to Newtonian mechanics or general relativity.
