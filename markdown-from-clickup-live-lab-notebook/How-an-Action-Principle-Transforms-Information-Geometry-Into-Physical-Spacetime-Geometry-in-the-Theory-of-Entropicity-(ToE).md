# But can an action principle actually transform information geometry into a physical Spacetime in the way Obidi has attempted in his radical Theory of Entropicity ToE?

To answer the theoretical question directly: Yes, mathematically and conceptually, an action principle can transform information geometry into a physical spacetime, but doing so requires a profound paradigm shift that sits on the highly speculative frontier of modern theoretical physics.

While John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is an audacious and provocative framework, the core premise—deriving physical spacetime from information geometry via a variational action principle—shares deep conceptual roots with legitimate mainstream research in quantum gravity, holographic duality, and emergent spacetime. [1, 2, 3] 

------------------------------

## 1. The Core Mechanism: How Information Becomes Geometry
In standard information geometry, the "space" is an abstract manifold where each point represents a probability distribution (such as a quantum microstate). The distance between these points is measured by the Fisher Information Metric (or its quantum counterpart, the Bures metric). [4] 
To turn this abstract probability space into the physical spacetime we live in, three distinct conceptual steps must occur, which Obidi attempts to link through his framework:

[Statistical Microstates] 
       │  (Fisher/Amari Information Geometry)
       ▼
[Information Manifold] 
       │  (Variational Action Principle / Entropic Field)
       ▼
[Physical Spacetime Curvature (Gravity)]


* Statistical Distance as Physical Distance: If two quantum states are highly distinguishable, their information-geometric distance is large. If they are nearly indistinguishable (highly entangled), they are "close." [5, 6] 
* The Role of the Action Principle: In physics, an action principle (like the Principle of Least Action) dictates how a system evolves by minimizing a certain quantity. To bridge the gap, the action must be defined such that minimizing informational uncertainty or maximizing entropy flow directly dictates the metric tensor ($g_{\mu\nu}$) of physical spacetime. [7, 8, 9, 10] 
* The Emergent Field: Spacetime ceases to be a fundamental "stage." Instead, the metric of spacetime is literally a shadow or a coarse-grained manifestation of the underlying information metric. [11, 12, 13] 

------------------------------

## 2. Parallels in Mainstream Physics
While Obidi's specific formulations (like the Obidi Action or the Master Entropic Equation) are independent models, the physics community has been heavily investigating the exact same premise through several highly regarded frameworks: [14, 15, 16, 17] 

* Jacobson’s Thermodynamic Spacetime (1995): Ted Jacobson famously demonstrated that Einstein’s field equations can be derived entirely as a thermodynamic equation of state. By applying the principle of entropy to local causal horizons, gravity emerges naturally without needing a fundamental geometric action. [18, 19, 20, 21] 
* The Ryu-Takayanagi Formula & AdS/CFT: In string theory's holographic principle, the geometric area of a surface in a higher-dimensional spacetime is exactly equal to the quantum entanglement entropy of the boundary gauge theory. This is the most concrete proof in modern physics that geometry and information entropy are mathematically equivalent. [22, 23] 
* Information Relativity & Quantum Metric Fluids: Physicists like Časlav Brukner have shown that when observers measure physical constants, the fundamental limitations of information processing yield equations that structurally mirror general relativity.

------------------------------

## 3. The Theoretical Hurdles Facing Obidi's Framework
For a radical theory like the Theory of Entropicity to successfully cross the line from a philosophical proposal to an accepted physical reality, it must mathematically resolve several severe hurdles that mainstream quantum gravity theories still struggle with:

* The Lorentzian Signature Problem: Information geometry inherently produces positive-definite (Riemannian) metrics. Physical spacetime is pseudo-Riemannian (Lorentzian), containing a specialized negative time dimension ($-+++$). Transforming purely statistical probability distances into a causal time dimension via an action principle is notoriously difficult without introducing mathematical paradoxes. [24] 
* The Coordinate-Free Mapping: In information geometry, coordinates represent parameters of a probability distribution. In general relativity, they represent physical events in space and time. An action principle must rigorously map these two radically different meanings without violating General Covariance (the law that physics looks the same in all coordinate systems). [25, 26, 27, 28, 29] 
* Predictive Testability: To be considered valid physics, an entropic action must predict a measurable deviation from Einstein’s General Relativity or standard quantum mechanics—such as specific corrections to black hole radiation, early universe inflation, or quantum decoherence scales.

## Summary of the Mechanism
If Obidi's framework successfully uses the Amari–Čencov $\alpha$-connection inside a universal variational principle, it is effectively declaring that the universe does not minimize energy, but rather optimizes the geometry of information. 

In such a universe, gravity is not a fundamental force, but an informational illusion caused by the natural tendency of systems to maximize their entropy. [30, 31, 32]
