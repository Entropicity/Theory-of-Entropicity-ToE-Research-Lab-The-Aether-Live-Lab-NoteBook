## Stripping Physics Down to One Primitive
In modern physics, the foundation is fractured. General relativity explains the macroscopic universe through smooth spacetime geometry, while quantum mechanics explains the microscopic universe through discrete probabilities.

Obidi’s "frantic effort" is driven by a refusal to accept this duality. He argues that both geometry and quantum behavior are merely symptoms of a deeper, singular reality. 

By introducing the **Obidi Action** and the **Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE),** Obidi attempts to prove that: [2, 4, 5, 6] 


# * The universe does not "contain" entropy/information; it is infact entropic information.

# * Matter, energy, gravity, and even the flow of time are not fundamental primitives. They are emergent ripples caused by the background [Entropic Field rearranging itself](https://entropicity.github.io/Theory-of-Entropicity-ToE/) to maximize its flow. [1, 3, 4, 5, 7]

Just as **Albert Einstein** fundamentally altered physics by **elevating the **speed of light (c)** to an absolute, universal constant that warps space and time, Obidi’s framework attempts a similar **conceptual revolution with entropy (S).**

> # **In Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE), therefore, physical systems don't move because they are pushed by mechanical forces; they move along trajectories that offer the path of least resistance within a dynamic, self-organizing entropic field.** [8, 9]
