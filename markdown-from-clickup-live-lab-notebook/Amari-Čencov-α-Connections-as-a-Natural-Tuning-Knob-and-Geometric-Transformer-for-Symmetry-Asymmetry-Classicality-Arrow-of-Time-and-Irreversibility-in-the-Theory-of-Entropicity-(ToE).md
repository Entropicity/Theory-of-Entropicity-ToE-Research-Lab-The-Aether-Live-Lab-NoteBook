## 1. Dialing down to $\alpha = 0$ (The Relativity Prong)

### The Inversion:

Because $\alpha = 0$ is the exact point where information geometry satisfies the properties of a Levi-Civita connection, Obidi identifies this specific state as the macroscopic, physical spacetime connection used in Einstein's General Relativity. By turning the dial to zero, information curvature directly aligns with gravitational curvature. [2, 6, 7, 8]

If $\alpha = 0$ gives us smooth, reversible Einsteinian spacetime, what happens when the knob is tuned away from zero? Obidi uses $\alpha \neq 0$ to introduce elements that General Relativity cannot naturally account for: [9]

When $\alpha \neq 0$, the geometry introduces a dual affine structure. This creates an inherent mathematical asymmetry. At microscopic scales, the framework utilizes non-zero $\alpha$ values alongside metrics like the Fubini-Study metric to weigh quantum probability states. These are integrated into the Vuli–Ndlela Integral to govern non-instantaneous quantum wave function collapse. [9, 10]
