# A Cursory Introduction to the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

John Onimisi Obidi is an independent scientific researcher, philosopher, and physicist who is the creator of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), a radical and provocative theoretical physics framework that entirely redefines entropy as the fundamental field of physical reality. He is explicitly distinguished from the popular African social media consultant and public speaker of a similar name (John Obidi). [1, 2] 

## The Theory of Entropicity (ToE)
Obidi's work focuses on moving entropy away from being a mere statistical measure of disorder to positioning it as an ontological, dynamic scalar field (S(x)). Within this framework, spacetime, gravity, matter, and motion are not fundamental constants or structures, but rather emergent properties arising from the underlying entropic substrate. [3, 4, 5] 

## Core Pillars of the Framework

* The Obidi Action: A universal variational principle that attempts to unify classical and quantum information geometries. It posits that the mechanical principle of least action is fundamentally an entropic variational principle. [6, 7] 
* Master Entropic Equation (MEE): Also referred to as the Obidi Field Equations (OFE), this acts as the entropic equivalent to Einstein's Field Equations of General Relativity. It describes how entropy gradients couple with geometry and matter. [6, 8] 
* Information Geometry: The framework utilizes structures like the Fisher–Rao metric, Fubini–Study metric, and Amari–Čencov α-connections to transform statistical distinguishability into the physical geometry of spacetime. [4, 6] 
* The "No-Rush" Theorem: A principle stating that state reconfigurations cannot occur instantaneously. It frames the speed of light (c) as the absolute limit of the entropic field's internal reorganization speed. [5] 
* Ontodynamics: A philosophical branch extended from his theory, exploring existence itself as a direct consequence of entropic motion. [9]

# Scholium 

The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is a provocative, novel theoretical physics framework formulated by independent researcher John Onimisi Obidi in early 2025. It proposes a radical inversion of modern physics by treating entropy not as a statistical byproduct of disorder, but as the fundamental, dynamic physical field from which spacetime, gravity, light, and quantum mechanics emerge. [1, 2, 3] 

While it borrows concepts from established fields like entropic gravity and information geometry, it is a highly audacious, self-published framework and is yet to be recognized or accepted by the mainstream physics community. [2, 3] 

------------------------------

## Core Concepts of the Framework
The framework attempts to unify thermodynamics, general relativity, and quantum mechanics under a single entropic principle through several key conceptual assertions: [4, 5, 6, 7] 

* The Ontological Entropic Field: In standard physics, entropy is a secondary property used to describe systems. ToE promotes it to a primary "ontic" scalar field (S(x)) that permeates existence, acting as the ultimate medium of causality. [1, 3, 8, 9] 
* The Speed of Light (c) as an Entropic Reconfiguration Rate: Rather than postulating the speed of light as an arbitrary constant, ToE reinterprets c as the maximum universal rate at which the underlying entropic field can reorganize energy and information. [1, 10] 
* Emergent Gravity and Spacetime: Similar to Erik Verlinde's entropic gravity, ToE argues that gravity is not a fundamental force or fixed geometric bending. Instead, it is an emergent "entropic pressure" caused by gradients in the field, with matter tending to move where entropy is maximized. [3, 10, 11, 12, 13] 
* The "No-Rush Theorem": This principle states that no physical interaction or quantum state synchronization can happen instantaneously. Every process requires a finite, non-zero time interval for the field to redistribute constraints, providing an alternate explanation for causality. [1, 8] 
* The Vuli–Ndlela Integral: ToE introduces an entropy-weighted variation of Feynman's path integral to explicitly build thermodynamic irreversibility and the arrow of time directly into quantum transitions. [7, 14] 

## Mathematical Tools Utilized
The papers associated with the theory attempt to build a formal mathematical architecture by combining physics and information theory: [15] 

* The Obidi Action: A variational principle used to derive the dynamics of the field by optimizing entropic cost and flow. [11, 16] 
* The Master Entropic Equation (MEE): Also referred to as the Obidi Field Equations, these are intended to serve an analogous role to Einstein's field equations by dictating how entropic gradients couple to geometry and matter. [17] 
* Information Geometry: The framework integrates mathematical metrics like the Fisher–Rao and Fubini–Study metrics via the Amari–Čencov alpha-connection to link geometric structures with generalized entropy measures (such as Rényi and Tsallis entropies). [7, 14] 

## Current Scientific Reception
The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is currently categorized as a highly radical or alternative physics. It is hosted on open pre-print repositories and platforms such as [OSF](https://osf.io/d7pky/overview), [Authorea](https://www.authorea.com/doi/10.22541/au.176236357.75572690), and [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558). Because it currently lacks experimental verification, it remains a a philosophical and mathematical exercise and a highly radical theory being further developed to attain the status of an established paradigm of modern physics. [2, 3, 18, 19, 20]
