# John Onimisi Obidi and the Foundational Conjectures of His Theory of Entropicity (ToE)


John-Onimisi-Obidi-and-the-Foundational-Conjectures-of-His-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md

**John Onimisi Obidi** is an independent Nigerian researcher and consultant based in Nairobi who proposed the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** in 2025. He describes himself as a researcher, physicist, philosopher, and humanist.

His work centers on the idea that **entropy is not merely a statistical or thermodynamic quantity but the fundamental field of reality** — the primary substrate from which matter, gravity, spacetime, and all physical laws emerge. His publications (hosted on Google Scholar, Figshare, Medium, and Academia.edu) span quantum gravity, general relativity, quantum field theory, and the Standard Model, all framed through the ToE lens.

His three foundational conjectures form the axiomatic core of the theory:

| Conjecture | Domain | Claim |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | Ontology | Entropy is the fundamental universal field of nature and reality. |
| **Second** | Dynamics | All physical laws and interactions are derivable from the Entropic Field. |
| **Third** | Geometry | Physical spacetime emerges from a deeper entropic-informational manifold. |

Key mathematical constructs in his framework include the **Obidi Action** (a functional governing entropic dynamics), the **Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP)** (requiring ToE to reproduce established physics in appropriate limits), and the **Vuli-Ndlela Integral**. He has published over 15 papers in 2025 alone, with a "Collected Works" volume released in December 2025, and continues to publish through 2026.

It's worth noting that ToE is an **independent, non-peer-reviewed** program — Obidi works as an independent consultant-researcher rather than through a university or institutional affiliation, and the theory has not been validated by the broader physics community.
