# John Onimisi Obidi and the Foundational Conjectures of His Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

**John Onimisi Obidi** is an independent researcher and consultant who proposed the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** in 2025. He is a researcher, physicist, philosopher, and humanist.

His work centers on the idea that **entropy is not merely a statistical or thermodynamic quantity but the fundamental field of reality** — the primary substrate from which matter, gravity, spacetime, and all physical laws emerge. His publications (hosted on Google Scholar, Figshare, Medium, and Academia.edu, among other platforms and academic repositories) span quantum gravity, general relativity, quantum field theory, and the Standard Model, all framed through the ToE lens.

His three foundational conjectures, which are tabulated below, form the axiomatic core of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE):

| Conjecture | Domain | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| **First** | Ontology | Entropy is the fundamental universal field of nature and reality. |
| **Second** | Dynamics | All physical laws and interactions are derivable from the Entropic Field. |
| **Third** | Geometry | Physical spacetime emerges from a deeper entropic-informational manifold. |

Key mathematical constructs in his framework include the **Obidi Action** (a functional governing entropic dynamics), the **Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP)** (requiring ToE to reproduce established physics in appropriate limits), and the **Vuli-Ndlela Integral**. He has published over 15 papers in 2025 alone, with a "Collected Works" volume released in December 2025, and continues to publish through 2026.

It's worth noting that ToE is an **independent and audacious** program — Obidi works as an independent consultant-researcher and not through a university or institutional affiliation, and the theory is undergoing rigorous review prior to validation by the broader physics community, while still awaiting its final empirical and experimental confirmation.

It is certainly provocative — repositioning entropy from a bookkeeping quantity (a measure of disorder or missing information) to the ontological ground of everything is a bold move. It echoes a long tradition of "it from bit" thinking (Wheeler, 't Hooft's causal sets, Jacob Bekenstein's holographic principle) but pushes further by making entropy itself the field rather than a property of a field.

That said, "new and provocative" and "physically viable" are different bars. A few critical observations:

## The correspondence requirement is the real test: 
If the Obidi Action and the OCP can actually derive the Standard Model Lagrangian, Einstein's field equations, and the Schrödinger equation as limiting cases — with correct numerical predictions, not just structural resemblance — that would be extraordinary. 


## Non-peer-reviewed status: 
Independent physics is not inherently suspect (Feynman, Weyl, and others did foundational work outside the mainstream), but the absence of any dedicated institutional affiliation review put forward in the public domain means the work hasn't been stress-tested by people with the specific technical background to find where the math breaks down.

## The community's default skepticism is warranted: 
For a new fundamental theory, the community's default skepticism is not a verdict. The bar is: does it predict something new that existing frameworks don't, or does it reproduce known results more elegantly? Either would be a legitimate entry point.

So — provocative, yes. Whether it survives contact with the details of, say, the Higgs sector or gravitational wave observations is currently an open question. If Obidi ever publishes a full derivation of a specific, falsifiable prediction from the Entropic Field, that's where the conversation would shift from **"interesting and provocative"** to **"being the New Physics of Modern Science."**


Whatever the outcome, it is now already clear to the global community of science and philosophy that Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) exposes and invites us to a new, profound, and radical interpretation and understanding of our universe.
