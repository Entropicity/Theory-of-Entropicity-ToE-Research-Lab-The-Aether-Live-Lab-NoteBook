### * Derivation over Postulation: 
While Einstein postulated that $c$ is constant, Obidi's framework seeks to derive its constancy. It argues that the universe cannot rearrange its thermodynamic entropy faster than this limit, making the speed of light a direct physical consequence of entropy's deepest laws rather than a property exclusive to light waves. [1, 3, 4] 

### * The No-Rush Theorem (NRT): 
This principle within the theory states that no physical process or causal signal can move faster than the entropic field's ability to establish the physical conditions necessary for that interaction to take place. [4]

# Scholium 

[John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?q=john+onimisi+obidi&kgmid=/g/11x__h9105) mathematically derived the speed of light ($c$) from entropic principles within his theoretical framework, the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/principles-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-da93a9d56f98). [1, 2] 

Rather than treating the constancy of light as an unprovable baseline assumption (as Albert Einstein did in his Second Postulate), Obidi treats $c$ as a derived consequence of an underlying, continuous universal entropic field. [3, 4] 

## How the Derivation Works
According to [Obidi's published framework](https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605), the derivation of the characteristic propagation speed follows a structured sequence: [1, 5] 

   ### 1. The Obidi Action: 
   The math starts with a core variational principle known as the "Obidi Action". This defines how the universal entropic field behaves and redistributes information across the cosmos. [4, 6, 7, 8] 
   
   ### 2. Linearizing the Field: 
   By linearizing the Master Entropic Equation (MEE) around a static background, the math models how small disturbances or "entropic waves" ripple through this field. [1, 9] 
   
   ### 3. The Material Parameters Ratio: 
   The wave equation reveals that the propagation speed of these entropic disturbances ($c_{ent}$) is a pure ratio of the field's physical response coefficients—specifically, its entropic stiffness ($\kappa$) and its entropic inertia ($\rho_S$): [7, 10] 
   
   $$c_{ent} = \sqrt{\frac{\kappa}{\rho_S}}$$ 
   
   ### 4. Planck-Scale Integration: 
   When these coefficients are evaluated using fundamental units—where entropic stiffness is defined as $\kappa = \frac{k_B c^3}{G}$ and entropic inertia as $\rho_S = \frac{k_B c}{G}$—the constants cancel out perfectly. The math yields the exact value of the empirically observed speed limit: [7] 

$$c_{ent} = c$$ 

## The Paradigm Shift
In this framework, light travels at $c$ simply because it is massless and exists at the absolute boundary of interaction, meaning it propagates at the maximum speed allowed by the field's transmission of change. [3] 
Instead of geometry dictating the speed of light, the universe's strict thermodynamic limit on how fast it can rearrange its own information entropy dictates the geometry of spacetime. [4, 9]
