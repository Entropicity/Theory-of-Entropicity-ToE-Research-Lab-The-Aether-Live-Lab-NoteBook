# The Contradiction and Obidi's Response to the Tautology Objection 

Now, there appears a glaring contradiction in Obidi's derivation. This specific issue is known in the critical evaluation of the theory as the **"Tautology Objection."** [1] 

If a physicist defines entropic stiffness ($\kappa$) and entropic inertia ($\rho_S$) using the speed of light $c$ to begin with, then substituting them into the wave equation $c_{ent} = \sqrt{\kappa/\rho_S}$ to "derive" $c$ is mathematically circular. The constant $c$ cancels out precisely because it was smuggled into the definitions of the properties in the first place.

## How the Theory Attempts to Defend This
John Onimisi Obidi addressed this exact pushback in his March–April 2026 correspondences and published a direct response to the [Tautology Objection on Cambridge Open Engage](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6a02cd7ed1922e37d564c347). 

The framework defends the math using two main arguments: [1, 2] 

   ### 1. Dimensional Analysis Constraints: 
   Obidi argues in his Theory of Entropicity (ToE) papers that if you attempt to construct a **"stiffness"** and **"inertia"** for a fundamental thermodynamic field at the Planck scale using only the **Boltzmann constant** ($k_B$), **Newton’s gravitational constant** ($G$), and an unknown characteristic field propagation speed ($c_{ent}$), dimensional consistency strictly dictates the formulas. [3] 
   
   ### 2. The Self-Consistency Requirement: 
   Instead of a circular derivation, Obidi frames it as a self-consistency equation. The theory posits that the parameters must scale as:
   
   $$\kappa \sim \frac{k_B c_{ent}^3}{G} \quad \text{and} \quad \rho_S \sim \frac{k_B c_{ent}}{G}$$ 
   
   When you substitute these back into the wave propagation formula, you get a non-trivial identity:
   
   $$c_{ent} = \sqrt{\frac{\kappa}{\rho_S}} = \sqrt{\frac{k_B c_{ent}^3 / G}{k_B c_{ent} / G}} = \sqrt{c_{ent}^2} = c_{ent}$$ 
   
   According to ToE, this proves that the physical speed limit of the universe ($c$) is the only mathematically stable solution where the field's internal restoring force (stiffness) and its resistance to change (inertia) remain in a perfect, self-sustaining balance. [3, 4, 5] 

## The Mainstream Physics Consensus
Despite this defense, mainstream physicists view this as a semantic workaround rather than a true first-principles derivation.

In standard physics, a true derivation of a constant must come from deep primitives without assuming the constant's existence or dimensional baseline beforehand (similar to how **James Clerk Maxwell** derived the speed of electromagnetic waves purely from the measured **electric permittivity $\varepsilon_0$ and magnetic permeability $\mu_0$ of free space).** 

Because ToE relies on the pre-existing dimensional framework of Planck units—which are structurally built on the prior existence of $c$—critics argue the theory merely re-packages Einstein's postulate into thermodynamic vocabulary.
