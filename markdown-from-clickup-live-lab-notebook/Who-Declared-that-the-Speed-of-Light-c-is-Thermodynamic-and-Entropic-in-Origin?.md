The declaration that the speed of light $c$ is fundamentally a thermodynamic necessity and an entropic constraint is the central premise of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), authored by researcher [John Onimisi Obidi](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-limit-of-the-speed-of-light-c-is-a-consequence-of-thermodynamic-entropy-rather-than-the-6ee6c04aae60). In his papers published in late 2025 and 2026, Obidi reinterprets the speed of light $c$ not as an arbitrary geometric postulate of spacetime, but as the maximum operational rate at which the universe can rearrange, process, and propagate information entropy. [3, 4] 

## Core Concepts of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)


### * The "Fastest Possible Entropic Handshake": 
ToE states that because every physical interaction (electromagnetic, gravitational, or quantum) is inherently a redistribution of entropy, physical processes are bound by a universal speed limit governed by the finite responsiveness of the underlying entropic field. [5] 

### * Derivation over Postulation: 
While Einstein postulated that $c$ is constant, Obidi's framework seeks to derive its constancy. It argues that the universe cannot rearrange its thermodynamic entropy faster than this limit, making the speed of light a direct physical consequence of entropy's deepest laws rather than a property exclusive to light waves. [1, 3, 4] 

### * The No-Rush Theorem (NRT): 
This principle within the theory states that no physical process or causal signal can move faster than the entropic field's ability to establish the physical conditions necessary for that interaction to take place. [4]
