# Bernard Carr and John Onimisi Obidi in Modern Theoretical Physics 

Bernard-Carr-and-John-Onimisi-Obidi-in-Modern-Theoretical-Physics.md 

The primary difference between Bernard Carr’s hyperspatial model and John Onimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) lies in what they choose as the most fundamental building block of reality. Professor [Bernard Carr's theory](https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/carr-s-quantum-theory-psi-mental-space) uses a higher-dimensional geometric framework to incorporate consciousness and mental experience directly into physics. In contrast, John Onimisi Obidi's [Theory of Entropicity](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) eliminates spacetime and quantum primitives altogether, asserting that an active, foundational entropic field is the substrate from which all matter, gravity, and time emerge. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

------------------------------

## Bernard Carr's Hyperspatial Model of Consciousness
Bernard Carr, a cosmologist and mathematician trained under Stephen Hawking, proposes that physics must expand its boundaries to accommodate first-person human consciousness. [5, 6, 7, 8] 

* The Core Mechanism: Carr leverages the extra dimensions found in modern physics frameworks like string theory and M-theory. He posits that these higher dimensions serve as a literal "mental space" where transpersonal, psychic, and normal mental experiences occur. [2, 3, 9] 
* The Role of the Brain: Instead of treating the brain as a generator of consciousness, Carr views it as a filter or receiver. The brain narrows down an expansive, higher-dimensional reality into our familiar three dimensions of space and one dimension of time to help us survive. [2, 3, 10] 
* The Goal: To reconcile physics with the anthropic principle and subjective mind, establishing that mind is a fundamental feature of the cosmos rather than an incidental byproduct of matter. [11, 12] 

## John Onimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)
Introduced by John Onimisi Obidi, the [Theory of Entropicity](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616) represents a radical "post-Einsteinian" paradigm shift in thermodynamics and information geometry. [1, 13] 

* The Core Mechanism: Standard physics views entropy as a secondary descriptor—a passive calculation of disorder. Obidi flips this hierarchy, elevating entropy to an ontological scalar field ($S(x)$) that acts as the primary substance of existence. [1, 14, 15] 
* Emergent Spacetime: In Obidi's "ontodynamics," spacetime, matter, and gravity are not fundamental primitives. Gravity emerges as the curvature of the entropic field as it naturally seeks to maximize flow, and matter is simply localized entropic condensation. [1, 4, 16] 
* Key Principles: It introduces the "No-Rush Theorem," which explains that all physical interactions take a non-zero time for the entropic field to reorganize information, physically accounting for the speed of light ($c$) as the maximum reconfiguration rate of this field. [1, 17] 

------------------------------

## Direct Comparison: Carr vs. Obidi

| Dimension | Bernard Carr's Theory | Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Primitive | Higher-dimensional geometry / Universal Mind | The Entropic Scalar Field |
| Primary Focus | Bridging physical matter with consciousness and first-person experiences. | Unifying General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics via information theory. |
| View of Spacetime | Spacetime is real but incomplete; it is wrapped inside a grander, multi-dimensional "mental space". | Spacetime is an illusion; it is an emergent geometric property generated by the flow of entropy. |
| Philosophical Basis | Dual-aspect monism / Idealism (Mind and matter meet in higher dimensions). | Informational Ontodynamics (Existence itself is fundamentally entropic motion). |
