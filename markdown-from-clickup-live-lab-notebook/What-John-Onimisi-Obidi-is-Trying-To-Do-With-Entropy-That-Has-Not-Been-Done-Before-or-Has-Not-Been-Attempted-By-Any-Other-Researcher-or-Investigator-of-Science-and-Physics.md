# What John Onimisi Obidi is Trying To Do With Entropy That Has Not Been Done Before or Has Not Been Attempted By Any Other Researcher or Investigator of Science and Physics

John Onimisi Obidi is attempting a radical conceptual reversal of modern physics through his [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188). 

While physicists historically treat entropy as a secondary, calculated property of matter or information, Obidi postulates that entropy is the fundamental, continuous dynamical field ($S(x)$) from which all of physical reality—including space, time, matter, and gravity—emerges. [1, 2] 

What makes Obidi’s approach entirely unprecedented compared to previous researchers is how he completely flips the ontological hierarchy of physics. [3] 

## The Ultimate Conceptual Reversal
To understand how Obidi's attempt is unique, consider how it fundamentally changes the structural stack of physics: [3] 


### Traditional Physics Stack: $\text{Spacetime} \rightarrow \text{Matter} \rightarrow \text{Information} \rightarrow \text{Entropy}$
(In standard physics, a pre-existing spacetime contains matter. We measure that matter to gather information, and from that information, we compute its entropy/disorder). [3, 4] 

###Obidi's ToE Stack: 
$\text{Entropy} \rightarrow \text{Information} \rightarrow \text{Spacetime} \rightarrow \text{Matter}$
(Entropy is the primary ontological field. It creates the gradients necessary for information, which generates the perception of space and time, ultimately condensing into what we perceive as matter). [3, 4] 


## Unprecedented Pillars of Obidi’s Theory
While prominent physicists like Erik Verlinde (Entropic Gravity) and Ted Jacobson have explored entropy as a driver of gravity, they still treat it as a property emerging from thermodynamic horizons or holographic boundaries. Obidi goes further by eliminating spacetime and classical fields as axiomatic primitives entirely, replacing them with a single entropic substrate. [5] 

His theory introduces several entirely new mathematical and theoretical frameworks:

   ### 1. Entropy as a Real Field ($S(x)$): 
   He elevates entropy from an abstract bookkeeping statistic to a physical, local, and continuous scalar field. He abstracts the famous Einstein-Wheeler dictum ("matter tells spacetime how to curve") to assert that "entropy curves existence itself." [6, 7] 
  
   ### 2. The Obidi Action Principle: 
   Instead of the standard Principle of Least Action, Obidi introduces a universal variational principle split into a Local Obidi Action (LOA) and a Spectral Obidi Action (SOA). It uses a mathematical duality to show how local spikes of order (like a planet forming) are globally and instantaneously counterbalanced by heat dissipation across the broader universe. [7] 
   
   ### 3. Physical Points as "Entropic Events": 
   In standard physics, a point in spacetime is just an empty geometric location. Obidi argues that a point only exists because entropy has created a "locus of distinguishability". If you cannot distinguish one coordinate from another via an entropic gradient, that space literally does not exist. [8] 
   
   ### 4. The "No-Rush" Theorem: 
   This principle dictates that because all physical interactions are governed by the underlying entropic field, nature cannot be rushed; everything must take a finite, non-zero interval of time to occur. [2] 

Ultimately, while other investigations treat the Second Law of Thermodynamics as a statistical trend (things naturally get messy), Obidi is attempting to rewrite the foundational grammar of physics so that the entire universe is a macro-system animated purely by entropy's flux. [9, 10, 11]
