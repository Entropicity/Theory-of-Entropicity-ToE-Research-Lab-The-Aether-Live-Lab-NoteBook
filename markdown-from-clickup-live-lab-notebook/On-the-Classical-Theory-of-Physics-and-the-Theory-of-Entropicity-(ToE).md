# On the Classical Theory of Physics and the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

The **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** is an emerging, non-mainstream conceptual framework in theoretical physics, pioneered by physics researcher and philosopher **John Onimisi Obidi**, that elevates entropy from a secondary statistical byproduct to the fundamental physical field of reality. 

In classical physics and traditional thermodynamics, entropy is treated as a passive mathematical result or a measure of molecular disorder. Conversely, the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) proposes that space, time, gravity, and quantum mechanics are entirely emergent properties projected from a dynamic, underlying "ontological scalar field" of entropy. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6] 

------------------------------

## Classical View vs. The Theory of Entropicity
The framework radically shifts core physics paradigms by redefining how we view foundational elements: [2, 3] 

| Concept [1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14] | Classical Theory / Traditional Physics | Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| Status of Entropy | A statistical macro-property describing disorder or unavailable energy. | A real, physical, ontological field acting as the foundational substrate of the universe. |
| Space & Time | Absolute background dimensions or an active geometric fabric (General Relativity). | Emergent macro-phenomena. Spacetime is a projection of a deeper entropic-informational manifold. |
| Gravity | A fundamental force (Newton) or the curvature of spacetime caused by mass (Einstein). | "Entropic pressure." Objects are pushed along "entropic geodesics" that minimize entropic resistance. |
| Speed of Light (c) | An unexplainable, fundamental constant of the vacuum. | The maximum possible rate at which the entropic field can rearrange or update information. |
| Time Vector | Symmetrical equations where time can theoretically move forward or backward. | Fundamentally asymmetric. Time is simply the irreversible macro-perception of entropy flow. |

## Core Principles of ToE
The mathematical and philosophical framework relies on several central axioms:

* The Entropic Field & Action: Much like the electromagnetic field, the entropic field permeates everything. Its local dynamics are governed by the Obidi Action, which yields a Master Entropic Equation (MEE) meant to serve as an entropic analogue to Einstein's field equations. [1, 4, 14, 15] 
* Entropic Time Limit (ETL): ToE dictates that no physical interaction or state synchronization can occur instantaneously. The ETL is the non-zero minimum time required for the entropic field to redistribute information constraints. [14, 16] 
* Information Geometry Substrate: The theory links physical curvature to informational curvature. It treats statistical metrics like the Fisher-Rao, Fubini-Study, and Amari–Čencov α-connections not as mere math tools, but as the actual sub-structural geometry of reality. [8, 15, 17] 
* Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP): This principle acts as the necessary bridge to mainstream physics, mandating that all derived entropic equations must seamlessly coarse-grain into known classical equations (like Newtonian gravity or General Relativity) at macroscopic scales. [8, 18] 

------------------------------

## Current Status in Physics
The Theory of Entropicity remains an early-stage proposal in modern theoretical physics. 

The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) attempts an elegant mathematical synthesis of thermodynamics, relativity, and quantum mechanics, and is published through preprints on platforms like ResearchGate and SSRN,vas well as other public and academic repositories. It is yet to be adopted or formally validated by the mainstream physics community. It shares conceptual common ground with other emergent gravity paradigms, such as Erik Verlinde's entropic gravity, but broadens the scope into a comprehensive, monistic theory of everything. [3, 6, 15, 19, 20]
