# Perihelion Precession of Planet Mercury of Einstein's General Relativity GR Derived from the Entropic Principles of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

John Onimisi Obidi formulated the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), which treats gravity as an emergent phenomenon driven entirely by an [entropic field](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/on-the-conceptual-and-mathematical-beauty-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-083308c6903c). His framework expands on earlier entropic gravity models to address classical general relativity tests without relying on fixed spacetime curvature. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 

## Overview of Obidi’s Derivation

* **The Paper:** Title is *"The Theory of Entropicity (ToE): An Entropy-Driven Derivation of Mercury's Perihelion Precession Beyond Einstein's Curved Spacetime in General Relativity (GR)".* [3] 
* **Core Hypothesis:** Replaces Einstein's geometric curvature with the Entropic Force-Field Hypothesis (EFFH) [later reformulated as a full theory called the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)]. [3, 4] 
* **The Mechanism:** Introduces higher-order entropy corrections directly into Newton’s gravitational potential. It integrates inputs from the [Unruh effect](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/67e63abe6dde43c9086de9e0), Hawking temperature, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, and the holographic principle. [3] 
* **Mathematical Tools:** Applies the **classical Binet equation** and the **Vis-viva equation** to this **entropy-modified potential to derive a new orbital differential equation.** [3] 
* **The Result:** Successfully **recovers the exact 43 arcseconds per century anomalous perihelion shift of Mercury entirely from thermodynamic and information-geometric dynamics [rather than from Einstein's principle of General Relativity of geometric spacetime curvature]**. [3, 6] 

## Conceptual Distinctions
Unlike **Ted Jacobson** (who derived the Einstein field equations as a local thermodynamic equation of state) or **Erik Verlinde** (who derived Newton's laws from holographic screens), **Obidi** introduces the **Obidi Action**. This variational principle couples an entropic scalar field to the metric. It positions spacetime curvature as a byproduct of a deeper [information-geometric system](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/from-information-geometry-to-information-gravity-information-geometry-as-the-origin-of-einsteins-f64a9c26c1ab) optimizing thermodynamic constraints.
