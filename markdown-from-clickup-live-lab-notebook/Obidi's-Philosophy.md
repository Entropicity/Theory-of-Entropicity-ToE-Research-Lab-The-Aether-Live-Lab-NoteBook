# Obidi's Philosophy 

Obidi's-Philosophy.md

**John Onimisi Obidi’s** philosophy is rooted in his **Theory of Entropicity (ToE),** a framework that redefines entropy not as a measure of disorder, but as the fundamental, dynamic substance and "operating system" of the universe. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/core-tenets-of-obidis-philosophical-ontology-and-the-foundations-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-5fdce397f250)

## Core Tenets of Obidi’s Philosophy

### • Ontological Primacy of Entropy: 
Traditional physics views mass, energy, and spacetime as fundamental, with entropy emerging from them. Obidi flips this hierarchy: entropy is a continuous, active scalar field (S(x)) from which spacetime, matter, gravity, and time emerge as macro-scale ripples or shadows. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/core-tenets-of-obidis-philosophical-ontology-and-the-foundations-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-5fdce397f250), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)

### • Ontodynamics: 
Obidi defines his philosophical and physical framework as ontodynamics—the study of existence itself as stabilized entropic motion, interaction, and negotiation rather than static being. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidi-enters-platos-academy-in-ancient-athens-a-philosophical-dialogue-on-reality-human-fate-1fe09a9a31c1), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-metaphysics-and-philosophy-from-his-theory-of-entropicity-toe-in-modern-theoretical-60dbb747cae7)

### • Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER): 
He replaces the classical Principle of Least Action with PoLER, asserting that physical systems do not move because they are pushed by mechanical forces, but because they naturally follow paths of least resistance within a self-organizing entropic field. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)

### • Information as Physical Reality: 
Expanding on John Archibald Wheeler's "It from Bit" concept, Obidi argues that the universe does not just contain information—it is entropic information, where macroscopic physics is an emergent projection of underlying informational updates. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_how-information-becomes-physical-spacetime-activity-7502485209272168449-3czB), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)

### • Dissolving Classical Divalents: 
The framework attempts to unify General Relativity (macroscopic geometry) and Quantum Mechanics (microscopic probability) by showing that both are merely different expressions or limiting cases of the underlying Entropic Field through principles like the Obidi Correspondence Principle. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/entropy-before-everything-the-radical-rebirth-of-physics-introducing-a-new-foundation-of-reality-46323836fad8), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)
