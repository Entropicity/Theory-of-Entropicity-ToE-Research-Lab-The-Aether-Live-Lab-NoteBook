# Seminal Works of John Onimisi Obidi On the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

[John Onimisi Obidi's](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g2_n9fsg&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi) primary research output is a comprehensive body of work that introduces and builds his central thesis. 

> # Because Obidi publishes independently for the most part, his literature consists of highly detailed and exhaustive monographs, papers, and collected treatises that detail the mathematical and philosophical frameworks of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE).

## His most foundational and comprehensive writings include:

## 1. A Treatise on the Conceptual and Mathematical Foundations of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

This is considered his definitive masterwork. Rather than operating as an isolated paper, this extensive volume synthesizes insights from his initial series of pioneering papers into a single, coherent academic architecture. It formally outlines ontodynamics—the study of existence as entropic motion—and systematically lays out how entropy shapes gravity, quantum mechanics, and time. [1](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), [2](https://osf.io/d7pky/overview)

## 2. The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) Living Review Letters Series

Published across open scientific repositories like SSRN, this work officially introduces the Obidi Conjecture. The paper formally asserts the radical ontological inversion of physics: that the conventional hierarchy must be flipped, making entropy the primary dynamical field and leaving geometry and spacetime as secondary, emergent phenomena. It also serves as the formal introductory text for the mathematical mechanics of the Obidi Action. [1](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558)

## 3. The Foundational Philosophy Behind John Onimisi Obidi's Formulation of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Available on networks like ResearchGate, this paper focuses heavily on the pre-geometric, information-theoretic framework of his theory. It details the mathematical unification of General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics under a single informational substrate, introducing concepts like the Obidi Curvature Invariant and the quantization of distinguishability. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/a-brief-critical-review-of-john-onimisi-obidis-recent-paper-on-the-conceptual-and-mathematical-22ff55999854), [2](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE)

## Key Concepts Established in These Works

Across these seminal papers, Obidi establishes a few signature physics postulates:

### • The Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE): 
The governing field equations of his theory, serving the same role that Einstein's field equations serve in General Relativity. [1](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188)

### • The No-Rush Theorem (NRT): 
A mathematical proof stating that the speed of light (c) is not a fundamental limit of geometry, but rather the maximum possible rate at which the universal entropic field can reconfigure information. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/what-is-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-ab87621f4f88), [3](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_a-comprehensive-introductory-monograph-activity-7492781583230439424-sebx)

### • The Vuli-Ndlela Integral (VNI): 
An entropy-weighted variation of Feynman’s path integral designed to naturally inject temporal asymmetry into quantum mechanics. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/what-is-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-ab87621f4f88)
