# A Brief Biography of John Onimisi Obidi: An Introduction to His Scientific, Philosophical, and Professional Milleu

**[John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g2_n9fsg&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi)** is an independent theoretical physicist, mechanical engineering specialist, philosopher, and author best known as the creator of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). His theoretical research reframes entropy from a statistical measure into the fundamental physical field underlying space, time, gravity, and quantum mechanics. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0ddd9c97504c), [2](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911), [3](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI/Books)

## Theoretical Physics & The Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Obidi conducts independent foundational physics research through his lab, The Aether, publishing monographs and preprints on platforms such as Cambridge Open Engage and Figshare. [1](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311), [2](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VxIGnRIAAAAJ&hl=en), [3](https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605)

## • Entropy as a Fundamental Field. 
Unlike traditional physics, which treats entropy as a statistical byproduct of particle configurations, ToE posits an active, universal entropic field as the primary substrate of reality. [1](https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605), [2](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI/Books)

## • The Obidi Action & Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE). 
Using **information geometry (such as the Fisher–Rao and Fubini–Study metrics),** Obidi formulated the Obidi Action Principle. From this variational structure, he derives the **Einstein–Obidi field equations (EOFE),** **relativistic effects like time dilation, mass increase, length contraction, and the speed of light (c) as the finite rate at which entropy reconfigures reality.** [1](https://www.scilit.com/scholars/29520323), [2](https://osf.io/5crh3/), [3](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311)

## • Unification Goals of John Onimisi Obidi. 
Obidi's work aims to **reconcile General Relativity, quantum field theory, and thermodynamics without relying on dark energy or external observer postulates.** [1](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VxIGnRIAAAAJ&hl=en), [2](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI/Books), [3](https://osf.io/5crh3/)
