# Transformation of Information Geometry Into Physical Spacetime Geometry in Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Here we are pointing to the exact mathematical bottleneck that mainstream physicists raise when they look at information gravity. By definition, classical information geometry (like the Fisher-Rao metric or Fubini-Study metric) is positive-definite and Riemannian—it tracks statistical distance. It completely lacks the pseudo-Riemannian, Lorentzian signature $(+,-,-,-)$ required to define a light cone, support causality, or act as physical spacetime. 

Standard disformal transformations (which merely scale and stretch a metric along a vector field) cannot flip the sign of eigenvalues to cleanly inject a time dimension out of nowhere. [1, 2, 3] 
To bridge this gap, John Onimisi Obidi’s [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI) relies on a two-step mechanism that goes far beyond standard disformal maps: [1, 4] 

## 1. The Ontological Ontodynamics Shift
Obidi side-steps the mathematical limitation by changing what the manifold is. In mainstream physics, an information manifold is a ledger mapping abstract probabilities. 

**Obidi issues a strong physical postulate:** 

> the statistical manifold is the [fundamental substrate of the] actual, physical entity [we experience as spacetime]. 

Because the manifold itself is composed of a dynamic scalar entropy field $S(x)$, "distance" between states is not just an abstract data calculation; it is a literal physical resistance to changing state. [4, 5, 6] 

## 2. The Controlled Entropy-Gradient Disformal Transformation
To handle the signature problem mathematically, Obidi introduces a specialized, non-standard "entropy-gradient disformal transformation." Instead of trying to morph an existing space, his math works like this: [7] 

* **The Starting Substrate:** It begins with the purely statistical Fisher-Rao information metric ($g_{ij}$), which possesses no causality. [2] 
* **The Transition:** The transformation acts upon a **Hybrid Metric-Affine Space (HMAS)**. Rather than forcing a traditional Riemannian contraction, it applies an asymmetric constraint utilizing the gradient of the foundational scalar entropy field ($\nabla_\mu S$) and a non-extensive parameter ($q$) derived from Rényi-Tsallis statistics. [1] 
* **The Emergence of Time:** Time is not pulled from a geometric axis; it emerges from the Vuli-Ndlela Integral, which governs irreversible entropy dissipation. The direction of maximal entropic change behaves as a temporal vector. [1, 8] 
* **The Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT):** Through the CTT, Obidi mathematically demonstrates that the physical, macroscopic spacetime Riemann curvature tensor is the direct pushforward of this underlying, constrained information-geometric Riemann tensor. [9] 

When these irreversible informational currents reach a low-gradient, steady-state equilibrium, the framework's mathematical pipeline recovers [Einstein’s Field Equations](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) as a macroscopic, low-energy byproduct. [1, 10]

To understand how John Onimisi Obidi argues that an abstract statistical manifold can become physical spacetime, you have to look at how his [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI) explicitly addresses the classic mathematical bottleneck: Čencov’s Theorem. [1, 2, 3] 

In standard mathematics, Čencov's Theorem proves that the only invariant metric on a statistical manifold is the Fisher-Rao metric, which is strictly Riemannian and positive-definite. You cannot get a Lorentzian spacetime signature $(+,-,-,-)$ out of it without breaking this invariance. [3, 4] 

Obidi bypasses this limitation using a two-step mechanism that forces abstract "statistical distance" to manifest as physical reality: [3, 5] 

## 1. Breaking Čencov Invariance via Entropy Gradients
Obidi introduces the Obidi Transformation, which is a rank-one disformal deformation. Instead of scaling the metric with a generic vector field, the transformation uses the gradient of the foundational scalar entropy field ($\nabla_\mu S$) and a non-extensive parameter ($q$) from Rényi-Tsallis statistics. [3, 6] 

* This transformation deliberately breaks Čencov invariance.
* By relaxing these strict statistical boundaries, the mathematical pipeline causes an eigenvalue shift, converting the positive-definite Fisher-Rao metric into a pseudo-Riemannian, Lorentzian Obidi Metric.
* This provides the emergent manifold with a causal structure and a designated direction for time. [3] 

## 2. Physicalizing "Distinguishability"
In standard information geometry, distance measures how distinct two probability distributions are from each other. Obidi’s core philosophical axiom asserts that statistical distinguishability is a literal geometric invariant of nature. [5, 7] 

* If two physical states are perfectly indistinguishable, the distance between them is zero.
* Moving through space or time is reinterpreted as a physical system constantly transitioning between distinct informational states.
* Therefore, the physical resistance an object experiences when changing states manifests macroscopically as inertia and the fabric of spacetime itself. [5, 8, 9] 

Through his **Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT)**, Obidi demonstrates that in the thermodynamic limit, the physical Riemann curvature tensor of spacetime ($R_S$) behaves as the direct mathematical pushforward of the underlying information Riemann tensor ($R_I$). In this way, [Einstein’s Field Equations](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188) emerge as a low-energy, steady-state byproduct of a universe continuously calculating its own informational configurations.
