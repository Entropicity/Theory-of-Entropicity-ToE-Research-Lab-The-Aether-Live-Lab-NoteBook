# Obidi's Ingenious Derivation of Einstein's Relativistic Length Contraction from Entropy

Obidi's-Ingenious-Derivation-of-Einstein's-Relativistic-Length-Contraction-from-Entropy.md

John Onimisi Obidi derives length contraction and other relativistic kinematics in his Theory of Entropicity (ToE) by treating entropy as a fundamental dynamic universal field rather than a geometric axiom of spacetime. [1](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/58667), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_derivation-of-the-speed-of-light-in-obidis-activity-7514194030407135232-3D5S)

## Core Concepts of the Derivation

### • Entropy as a Fundamental Field: 
Every object carries an entropy density $\(s\)$ and an entropy flux $\(j\)$. Spacetime geometry and Lorentz transformations are treated as emergent properties of this entropic field rather than foundational assumptions. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_relativistic-spotlight-of-the-theory-of-activity-7514618112412041216-oXWb), [2](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/api-gateway/coe/assets/orp/resource/item/6900d89c113cc7cfff94ef3a/original/the-theory-of-entropicity-to-e-derives-and-explains-mass-increase-time-dilation-and-length-contraction-in-einstein-s-theory-of-relativity-to-r-to-e-applies-logical-entropic-concepts-and-principles-to-verify-einstein-s-relativity.pdf)

### • Entropic Conservation: 
For a closed rod in steady uniform motion, total entropy is conserved and constrained by the relation:

$\(S_{\text{total}} \equiv sL = S_0L_0 = \text{const}\)$. [1](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/api-gateway/coe/assets/orp/resource/item/6900d89c113cc7cfff94ef3a/original/the-theory-of-entropicity-to-e-derives-and-explains-mass-increase-time-dilation-and-length-contraction-in-einstein-s-theory-of-relativity-to-r-to-e-applies-logical-entropic-concepts-and-principles-to-verify-einstein-s-relativity.pdf)

### • The No-Rush Theorem (NRT): 
The entropic field enforces a maximum causal-propagation rate—experienced as the speed of light $\(c\)$—which bounds the entropy flux via an **"entropy cone"**: 

$\(\vert{}j\vert{} \le c e s\)$. [1](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/58667), [2](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/api-gateway/coe/assets/orp/resource/item/6900d89c113cc7cfff94ef3a/original/the-theory-of-entropicity-to-e-derives-and-explains-mass-increase-time-dilation-and-length-contraction-in-einstein-s-theory-of-relativity-to-r-to-e-applies-logical-entropic-concepts-and-principles-to-verify-einstein-s-relativity.pdf)

### • Capacity Constraints: 
As an object's velocity $\(v\)$ increases, the entropic field has to work harder to maintain internal coherence and redistribute entropy. This resistance compresses spatial intervals: 

$\(L' = L / \gamma\)$ 

and **slows internal processes (time dilation)**. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_theory-of-entropicity-toe-activity-7511659255716843520-RWeh), [2](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/58667)

Instead of starting from Einstein’s second postulate or geometric Minkowski space, Obidi reproduces the standard relativistic transformations directly from entropic conservation laws and the Obidi Action Principle. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-principle-of-conservation-of-entropic-flux-opcef-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-ab573d269a75), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_relativistic-spotlight-of-the-theory-of-activity-7514618112412041216-oXWb)
