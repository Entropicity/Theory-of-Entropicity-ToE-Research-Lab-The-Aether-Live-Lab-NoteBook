## The Ontological Leap: From Tool to Reality
In standard physics, entropy is usually treated as an epiphenomenon—a secondary bookkeeping measurement of macroscopic disorder or missing information. Spacetime geometry is the rigid "stage" upon which this physics happens. [2, 4, 5] 
Obidi flips this entirely through what he calls The Ontological Shift: [6] 

   1. Entropy as a Fundamental Field: Entropy is not a secondary calculation; it is a fundamental, universal scalar field ($S(x)$). [6, 7, 8] 
   2. The Manifold is Primitive: Because entropy natively generates all information geometry (the Fisher-Rao metric, Amari's $\alpha$-connections, etc.), the underlying statistical/entropic manifold is the true, fundamental fabric of reality. [5, 6] 
   3. Spacetime is a Macro-Shadow: Physical spacetime and gravity are not fundamental inputs; they are emergent outputs—macroscopic projections or "shadows" generated by the dynamics and gradients of this entropic manifold. [9, 10, 11] 

## The Mathematical Framework: The Obidi Action
Where Amari's information geometry was traditionally a static mathematical tool for tracking probabilities, Obidi makes it physical by giving it dynamics. [2] 
He achieves this via the Obidi Action, a variational principle designed to do for information manifolds what the Einstein-Hilbert action did for spacetime curvature. By varying this action, the framework derives the Master Entropic Equation and the entropic Einstein equations. Through a mathematical mapping called a disformal transformation, the abstract geometric distances of information theory seamlessly translate into the actual, physical curved spacetime of General Relativity. [2, 4, 12, 13] 
In short, the logic flows perfectly: If gravity is geometry, and gravity is thermodynamic (entropy), then geometry must fundamentally be entropic information. [5, 14]
