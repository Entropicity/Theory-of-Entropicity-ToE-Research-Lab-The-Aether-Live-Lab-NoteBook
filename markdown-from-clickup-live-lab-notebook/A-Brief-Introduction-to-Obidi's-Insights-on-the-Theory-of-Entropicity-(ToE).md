# A Brief Introduction to Obidi's Insights on the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

---

### 🔹 1. You’re building a physics framework where **entropy is the fundamental field**
Across multiple gists, ToE asserts that entropy is not just a statistical descriptor but the *primary ontological field* from which spacetime, geometry, and physical laws emerge. This flips the usual hierarchy of physics: instead of geometry shaping entropy, entropy shapes geometry.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 2. PoLER replaces the classical Principle of Least Action
Your Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER) is positioned as a structural replacement for the classical least‑action principle. Instead of minimizing mechanical action, systems minimize **entropic resistance**, selecting “entropic geodesics” rather than geometric curves. This is a radical conceptual shift in how trajectories are defined.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 3. You propose that particles do *not* respond to forces — they follow entropy’s optimization
One of your gists states that particles and bodies do not follow geometric curves or respond to external pushes. Instead, they trace paths that allow entropy to reorganize itself most efficiently. This reframes motion as an informational process rather than a mechanical one.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 4. You’re attempting to derive classical physics from an entropic-first foundation
Your “Challenges Before PoLER” gist acknowledges that deriving classical dynamics (like the harmonic oscillator) from an entropic principle is extremely difficult — requiring new definitions of entropic resistance, entropic curvature, and entropic geodesics. This shows the depth of the reconstruction you’re attempting.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 5. You outline a historical lineage: **de Broglie → Haller → Obidi**
Your DHO Entropic Lineage connects Louis de Broglie’s wave insights and John Haller’s informational physics ideas to your own entropic field theory. This positions ToE within a conceptual evolution of physics that emphasizes information and wave structure.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 6. You contrast your research direction with Edward Witten’s
One gist directly compares your approach with Witten’s:  
- Witten: geometry + strings inside spacetime  
- Obidi: entropy as the field that *creates* spacetime  
This highlights how ToE diverges from mainstream quantum gravity by rejecting geometry as fundamental.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)

---

### 🔹 7. Your work is framed as an attempt to rebuild physics “from the ground up”
Several gists describe your effort as a complete reconstruction of physics — not modifying existing models, but replacing the foundational operating system of nature with an entropic field principle. It’s described as “frantic,” “exhausting,” and deeply ambitious.   [gist.github.com](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity)
