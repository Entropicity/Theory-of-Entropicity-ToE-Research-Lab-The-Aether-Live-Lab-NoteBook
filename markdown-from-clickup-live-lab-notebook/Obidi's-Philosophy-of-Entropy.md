# Obidi's Philosophy of Entropy

Obidi's-Philosophy-of-Entropy.md

**John Onimisi Obidi’s philosophy of entropy**, central to his **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)**, posits that **entropy is the fundamental, dynamic field** of reality, rather than a mere statistical measure of disorder. Developed starting in **2025**, ToE asserts that **entropy precedes and generates** space, time, matter, and gravity, effectively replacing the conventional ontological primitives of modern physics.

## Key philosophical pillars include:

### *   **Ontological Primacy**:
Entropy is elevated to a universal scalar field $S(x)$ that serves as the causal substrate of existence, with spacetime and physical laws emerging as secondary consequences.

### *   **The Obidi Conjecture**: 
Reality is defined as an **entropic field**, where the increase of entropy is not a passive byproduct but the active "prime mover" driving cosmic evolution and structure.

### *   **Entropic Dictum**: 
Generalizing Einstein’s "matter curves spacetime," Obidi proposes that **"entropy curves existence itself,"** meaning gravitational and geometric phenomena are direct results of entropic gradients.

### *   **Ontodynamics**: 
This branch of philosophy within ToE defines **Being** as the persistence of entropic gradients and **Becoming** as the irreversible redistribution of entropy, framing the universe as a self-computing, information-driven process.

### *   **Scientific Realism**: 
Unlike observer-centric interpretations (e.g., Copenhagen or Donald Hoffman’s consciousness realism), ToE maintains that physical reality exists independently of observation, with quantum collapse and decoherence driven by **entropic thresholds** rather than conscious measurement.
