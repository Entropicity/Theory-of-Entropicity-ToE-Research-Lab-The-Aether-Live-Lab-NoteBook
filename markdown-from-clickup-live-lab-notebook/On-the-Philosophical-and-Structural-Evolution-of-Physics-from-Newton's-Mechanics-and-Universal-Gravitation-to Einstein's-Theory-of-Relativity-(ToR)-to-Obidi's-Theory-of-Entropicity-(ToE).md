## How Obidi Challenges Einstein
Einstein's great triumph was proving that space and time are relative to the observer, but he still treated the unified fabric of spacetime as a fundamental entity—the lowest layer of reality.
Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) pushes past this by arguing that spacetime is a macroscopic illusion, much like the surface of a fluid.

* The "Fluid" Analogy: If you look at a river, "waves" and "currents" appear real. However, they are not fundamental entities; they are merely the collective behavior of trillions of H₂O molecules.
* The Entropic View: To Obidi, spacetime is the wave, and entropy is the molecule. Space and time do not possess inherent, standalone existence. Instead, they are thermodynamic, geometric constructions that emerge because physical subsystems are exchanging and processing information.

Where Einstein told Newton, "The stage itself can bend," Obidi tells Einstein, "There is no stage at all—there is only the choreography of information."
