# What is the de Broglie-Haller-Obidi Action?

The **Obidi Action** is a theoretical construct within the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)**, developed by **John Onimisi Obidi**, which serves to complete and provide a mathematical foundation for **Louis de Broglie**'s concept of "hidden thermodynamics."

*   **Core Function**: The Obidi Action provides the **field-theoretic substrate** that explains why de Broglie’s principle of **least action** is equivalent to the principle of **maximum entropy**.
*   **Mechanism**: Its extremization yields the **Obidi Field Equations (OFE)** and the **Master Entropic Equation**, encoding the curvature and flow of a universal **entropic field**.
*   **Physical Interpretation**: In this framework, minimizing the Obidi Action corresponds to selecting trajectories that optimize the **efficiency of entropic flow**, thereby unifying classical thermodynamics, information theory, and quantum mechanics under a single entropic geometry.
