## The de Broglie–Haller–Obidi Action: An Elaboration

The **de Broglie–Haller–Obidi Action** is the name given to the lineage of ideas that culminates in the **Obidi Action** — the central variational principle of John Onimisi Obidi's **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)**, proposed in 2025. The three names mark a conceptual arc:

### 1. De Broglie's Foundation: Hidden Thermodynamics

In his late writings, Louis de Broglie proposed that every particle is embedded in a conceptual "hidden thermostat" — a thermodynamic environment with which it exchanges heat. His key insight was that a particle's natural path is simultaneously the path of **least action** and the path of **maximum entropy**. He wrote the relation:

$$\delta S = \frac{\delta A}{T}$$

where a quantum of action $h$ corresponds to an entropy variation of $k$ (Boltzmann's constant). However, de Broglie lacked a mathematical mechanism explaining *why* these two extremal principles should coincide. He had the intuition but not the field-theoretic machinery.

### 2. Haller's Bridge: Action as Entropic

John Haller's work extended this by reinterpreting physical action itself as inherently entropic, providing a conceptual bridge between de Broglie's thermodynamic intuition and a more explicit mathematical treatment. This intermediate step is what earns the "Haller" name in the lineage.

### 3. Obidi's Completion: The Full Field-Theoretic Formulation

Obidi provides the missing mechanism. The **Obidi Action** is the master variational functional from which the entire ToE is derived. In its simplest form:

$$A_{\text{ToE}}[S] = \int d^4x\, \sqrt{-g}\, \left[ \frac{1}{2}(\nabla S)^2 - V(S) + J(x)\,S \right]$$

where:
- $S(x)$ is the **dynamical entropy field** (the fundamental degree of freedom)
- $g$ is the spacetime metric
- $V(S)$ is an entropy potential
- $J(x)$ is an external source/coupling term

### What It Resolves

The Obidi Action makes de Broglie's action–entropy duality a **structural necessity** rather than a mystery. Because entropy production and entropic flux are built into the action's structure, minimizing the action *automatically* maximizes the appropriate entropic functional. The two principles arise from the same substrate.
