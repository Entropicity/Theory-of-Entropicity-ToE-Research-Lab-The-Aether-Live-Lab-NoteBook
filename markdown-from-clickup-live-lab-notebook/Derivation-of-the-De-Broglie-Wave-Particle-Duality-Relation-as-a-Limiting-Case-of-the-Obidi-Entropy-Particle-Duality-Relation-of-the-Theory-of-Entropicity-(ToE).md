Historically, Louis de Broglie faced a massive contradiction in 1923. A particle's internal periodic frequency transforms via relativistic time dilation ($\nu = \nu_0\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}$), while its quantum energy transforms inversely via Planck's law ($E = h\nu$). [3]
