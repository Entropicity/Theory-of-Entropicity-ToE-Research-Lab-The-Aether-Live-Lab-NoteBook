# What is the Obidi Action of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)?

What-is-the-Obidi-Action-of-the-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE)?.md

The Obidi Action is a central mathematical and variational principle within a theoretical physics framework known as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), developed by John Onimisi Obidi. [1, 2] 

In this theory, the **Obidi Action** plays a role highly analogous to the Principle of Least Action in classical mechanics or the Einstein–Hilbert action in general relativity, but it replaces traditional mass, energy, or spacetime metrics with a fundamental entropy field (S(x)). [1, 2] 

> ### The Obidi Action is the "Mathematical Source Code (MSC)" of the Universe in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE).

## Core Concepts of the Obidi Action
The Obidi Action underpins several radical, paradigm-shifting claims in this specific informational and thermodynamic model of the universe: [2, 3] 

### The Principle of Least Entropic Resistance: 
Instead of physical systems moving along paths defined by classical forces or curved spacetime, the Obidi Action asserts that physical systems evolve along trajectories that minimize entropic resistance. [4] 

### Entropy as the Foundational Field: 
Traditional physics views entropy as a passive, derived property (a measure of disorder or state probability). The Obidi Action treats entropy as an active, continuous, and fundamental field from which space, time, gravity, and matter organically emerge. [2, 5, 6, 7] 

### Derivation of the Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE): 
By applying a variational calculus principle to the Obidi Action, the theory derives the Master Entropic Equation. This field equation is intended to serve as the entropic equivalent to Einstein's field equations in General Relativity. [1, 2, 8, 9] 

### Mass as "Frozen" Entropy: 
According to the model, mass and physical matter are not fundamental inputs. Instead, they are generated as outputs of fiber integrals calculated from the Obidi Action over microscopic entropic fluctuations. [10] 

### Information Geometry: 
The action is mathematically constructed on an information-geometric manifold, synthesizing concepts like the Fisher–Rao metric, Amari–Čencov α-connections, and statistical curvature to translate statistical distinguishability into emergent, physical distance. [5, 8]

# Scholium 

The Obidi Action is a foundational variational principle in the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** developed by **John Onimisi Obidi.** It defines how a universal entropy field evolves, acting in a way similar to the **Principle of Least Action** in **classical mechanics** or the **Einstein-Hilbert action in general relativity.**

## Core Concepts of the Obidi Action

### Entropic Field: 
Treats entropy as the primary building block of reality rather than a byproduct of disorder.

### Least Resistance: 
Dictates that physical systems change along paths of least entropic resistance.

### Master Equation: 
Serves as the mathematical source used to derive the Master Entropic Equation, which governs physical forces.

### Emergent Reality: 
Posits that space, time, gravity, and mass emerge from underlying entropic dynamics.
