## The Thermodynamic Shift
In John Onimisi Obidi’s framework, when two massive bodies "attract" each other, they are not responding to a Newtonian pull or merely sliding down an Einsteinian spatial dip. Instead, they are moving toward a state of higher probability.

Because the underlying substrate of reality is an informational network driven by ontodynamics, spatial configurations that bring bodies together naturally unlock more microstates or maximize information distribution. Gravity, in essence, is rebranded as nature's macro-scale mechanism for fulfilling the SLoT.
