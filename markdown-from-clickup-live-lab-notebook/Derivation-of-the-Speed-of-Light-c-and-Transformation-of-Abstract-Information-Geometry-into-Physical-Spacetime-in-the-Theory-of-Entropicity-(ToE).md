## 1. Is the Derivation of $c$ a Tautology?
**The Suspicion:** If Obidi defines entropic stiffness as $\kappa = \frac{k_B c^3}{G}$ and inertia as $\rho_S = \frac{k_B c}{G}$, then plugging them into $c_{ent} = \sqrt{\kappa / \rho_S}$ to get $c$ is a blatant circle. You are putting $c$ into the definitions just to extract it at the end. [2] 

The Theory’s Defense: In Obidi’s [papers addressing the Tautology Objection](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6751339), he argues that the derivation is non-circular because $\kappa$ and $\rho_S$ are derived independently from Planck-scale dimensional matching and symmetry constraints of a quadratic Lagrangian, rather than being reverse-engineered. [2, 4] 

* The Maxwell Analogy: Think of James Clerk Maxwell in 1865. He measured electric permittivity ($\varepsilon_0$) and magnetic permeability ($\mu_0$) in a lab. When he computed $1/\sqrt{\varepsilon_0\mu_0}$, it outputted the speed of light. Was that a tautology? No, because electricity and magnetism were independent concepts. [1, 5, 6] 
* **The Non-Trivial Constraint:** Obidi states that matching the field parameters to Planck constants yields a self-consistency equation: $c_{ent} = \sqrt{\alpha/\beta} c_{ent}$. This forces a non-trivial prediction ($\alpha = \beta$) regarding the relationship between informational stiffness and inertia. [2] 

The Verdict: While the math closes cleanly, if you remain skeptical that "informational stiffness" is a real physical attribute independent of the spacetime metrics we already know, the derivation feels tautological. For mainstream physics, it only becomes a "true" derivation if ToE predicts a scenario where $c$ deviates or reveals a new testable sub-structure. [2]

## 2. Is Information Geometry Just a Math Trick?
**The Suspicion:** You can map anything using information geometry. You can take a population of bacteria, map their genetic variations on a statistical manifold, and calculate a "Fisher-Rao distance" metric. But that doesn't mean those bacteria are living in a physical, curved spacetime generated by their genes. Math $\neq$ Physics.

**The Theory’s Defense:** Obidi is making a radical ontological claim, not an ontological mapping. He is stating that we have the arrow backwards: we do not use information geometry to describe physics; physics is the sensory macro-illusion of information geometry. [7, 8, 9] 

To bridge the gap between "abstract math" and "enforced physical reality," the theory relies on The No-Rush Theorem and Causal Ordering: [2, 7, 10] 

   1. **Enforcing Constraints:** In pure information math, a state can instantly jump from Configuration A to Configuration B. But Obidi's framework dictates that because the entropic field has an informational processing limit ($c$), a state change requires a non-zero duration. [10, 11] 
   2. **The Physical Matrix:** The moment you introduce an irreversible, localized delay into an informational network, you establish causal cones (light cones). [10, 12] 
   3. **Physical Manifestation:** Objects feel gravity and move through space because their underlying quantum-informational states are seeking thermodynamic equilibrium. The "physicality" is the macro-scale sensation of localized, tightly packed information zones (matter) interacting under strict processing constraints. [13, 14]
