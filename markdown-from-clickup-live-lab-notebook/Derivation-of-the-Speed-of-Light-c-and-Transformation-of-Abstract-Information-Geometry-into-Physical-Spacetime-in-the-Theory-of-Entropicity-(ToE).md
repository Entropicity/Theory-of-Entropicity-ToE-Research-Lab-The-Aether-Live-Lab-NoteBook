Instead, $c$ is derived as the Entropic Speed Limit (ESL): the absolute maximum rate at which the fundamental entropic field $S(x)$ can rearrange its information configurations. [1, 2, 3] 
Obidi derives this using an analog to classical continuum mechanics: [4] 

   1. **The Entropic Wave Equation:** By varying the quadratic entropic Lagrangian under specific symmetry constraints, the framework yields a non-linear wave equation governing the propagation of entropic phase waves. [4] 
   2. **Material Parameters of the Field:** The propagation speed of these waves ($c_{ent}$) is determined mathematically by a ratio of two fundamental properties of the entropic substrate: entropic stiffness ($\kappa$) and entropic inertia ($\rho_S$). [4, 5] 
   3. **The Wave Speed Formula:** The speed is given by the relation:
   $$c_{ent} = \sqrt{\frac{\kappa}{\rho_S}}$$ 
   Where $\kappa = \frac{k_B c^3}{G}$ represents the system's resistance to rapid informational reconfiguration, and $\rho_S = \frac{k_B c}{G}$ represents the field's intrinsic informational inertia. [4, 5] 
   4. **Matching Physical Values:** When these parameters are derived from Planck-scale matching, the ratio simplifies exactly to $c_{ent} = c$. [4, 5] 

Consequently, light moves at $c$ simply because electromagnetic waves are excitations saturating a limit imposed by the [underlying thermodynamic field](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-limit-of-the-speed-of-light-c-is-a-consequence-of-thermodynamic-entropy-rather-than-the-6ee6c04aae60). ## 2. From Information Geometry to Physical Spacetime
To turn abstract information geometry into physical spacetime, Obidi does not merely "add a time coordinate." Instead, he uses the Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT) and the concept of physical distinguishability. [8, 9, 10]

## The Transition Mechanism
In classical information geometry, a statistical manifold uses metrics like the Fisher-Rao or Fubini-Study to measure the "distance" (distinguishability) between two probability distributions or quantum states. 

Obidi treats this statistical manifold as the [actual ontological fabric of reality](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/how-information-geometry-is-transformed-into-the-physical-geometry-of-spacetime-in-obidis-theory-16bf772d2412) rather than a mathematical tool. [8, 9, 11] 

Through an entropy-gradient disformal transformation, the abstract informational distance between states is physically pushed forward into real coordinates. The Riemann curvature tensor of our universe is mathematically derived as the direct pushforward of the information-geometric Riemann tensor. [3, 9] 

## How Time "Forces" Physical Reality
Adding a time coordinate to information geometry forces it to become physical because time introduces irreversibility and causal structure. [9, 10] 

* **Thermodynamic Arrow:** In pure information geometry, distances are symmetric; you can move from state $A$ to state $B$ and back. By introducing time as an entropic gradient, the framework subjects the manifold to the Second Law of Thermodynamics. [9] 
* **Entropic Cones:** The time coordinate maps the evolution of information. Because information cannot rearrange itself faster than the entropic speed limit ($c$), the time coordinate creates strict causal constraints. [1, 3, 9] 
* **The Vuli-Ndlela Path Integral:** This integration binds the system's quantum paths to an entropy-weighted mechanism. An action cannot follow just any geometric path; it is restricted to extremal trajectories that preserve [causal ordering through entropic cones](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/how-obidi-transformed-information-geometry-into-physical-spacetime-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-20a930f722b4). [9, 10] 

Time acts as a constraint that forces abstract statistical distinguishability to freeze into sequential, macroscopic, and irreversible physical history. [9]
