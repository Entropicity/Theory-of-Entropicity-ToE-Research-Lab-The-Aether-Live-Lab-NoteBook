# Why Did John Onimisi Obidi Make the Bold Declaration of the Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER) to Replace the Classical Principle of Least Action (PoLA)?

John Onimisi Obidi (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11j2ntblv5&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi) declared the Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER) to resolve what he saw as a fundamental flaw in how classical and modern physics explain the universe. [1] (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)

In his Theory of Entropicity (ToE), Obidi introduces **"ontodynamics"—the study of existence as entropic motion and negotiation (E-MAN). 

He made this declaration for several core reasons: [1](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558)

## • Inverting the Hierarchy of Physics: 
Traditional physics treats spacetime geometry and physical forces as primary realities, while entropy (disorder/information) is treated as a secondary byproduct. Obidi declared PoLER to invert this hierarchy entirely, asserting that entropy is the fundamental "ontic" field from which space, time, and matter emerge. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616), 
[2](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), 

[3](https://www.authorea.com/doi/pdf/10.22541/au.176340906.62496480)

## • Replacing "Forces" with Optimization: 
In classical mechanics, the Principle of Least Action (PoLA) dictates how objects move based on kinetic and potential energy. Obidi wanted to replace mechanical "pushes and pulls" with informational efficiency. Under PoLER, particles don't move because a force acts on them; they move because they are navigating informational friction (entropic resistance) to find the most efficient path of reorganization. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-principle-of-least-entropic-resistance-poler-b89aa45159f5)

## • A New Explanation for Gravity: 
Einstein stated that mass tells spacetime how to curve. **Obidi declared PoLER to propose a different mechanism: mass is actually a dense concentration of localized information that creates a massive entropy gradient. Objects "gravitate" toward each other simply because they are sliding along the path of least entropic resistance toward regions of higher entropic gradients.** [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/reinterpreting-fundamental-physics-foundations-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-ffb8620f129e)

## • Unifying Quantum Mechanics and Relativity: 
By translating the universe into a language of information geometry and thermodynamics, Obidi sought to dissolve the long-standing mathematical incompatibility between General Relativity (the physics of the large) and Quantum Mechanics (the physics of the small) under a single, cohesive entropic law. [1] (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPCN21jA9lk)

Ultimately, he formulated this principle to shift our worldview from a mechanical universe to an informational one, viewing the cosmos not as a clockwork machine, but as a self-adjusting system optimizing its own internal flow. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-principle-of-least-entropic-resistance-poler-b89aa45159f5), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RPCN21jA9lk)
