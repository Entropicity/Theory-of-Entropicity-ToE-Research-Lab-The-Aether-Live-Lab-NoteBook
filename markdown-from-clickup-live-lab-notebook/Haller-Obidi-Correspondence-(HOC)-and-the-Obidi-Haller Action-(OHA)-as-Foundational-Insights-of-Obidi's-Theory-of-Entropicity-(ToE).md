# Haller-Obidi Correspondence (HOC) and the Obidi-Haller Action (OHA) as Foundational Insights of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) 


Haller-Obidi-Correspondence-(HOC)-and-the-Obidi-Haller Action-(OHA)-as-Foundational-Insights-of-Obidi's-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md


The Obidi-Haller Action and the Haller-Obidi Correspondence are foundational theoretical frameworks within the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)—a physics paradigm pioneered by researcher [John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Obidi-4) that proposes entropy, rather than spacetime, is the fundamental substrate of reality. Together, these concepts establish a mathematical bridge showing that the classical mechanical "action" of a particle is fundamentally equivalent to the generation of informational entropy. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

------------------------------

## 1. The Core Entropy–Action Equivalence
The groundwork for this framework was laid in 2015 when physicist John Haller mathematically demonstrated a deep link between information theory and quantum mechanics: [3] 


* The Identity: For a diffusing quantum particle, its self-information (entropy) directly equals its classical action scaled by Planck's constant: $\text{Entropy} \leftrightarrow \text{Action}$. [3] 
* The Implication: Physics does not merely produce entropy; rather, entropy production is the underlying driver that dictates physical trajectories and motion. [3, 4] 
  

## 2. The Haller-Obidi Lagrangian and Action
To make this variational relationship explicit, the theory utilizes the Haller-Obidi Lagrangian ($\mathcal{L}_{HO}$), which governs particle paths through a dual mechanical and informational lens: [4] 
$$\mathcal{L}_{HO} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}\dot{H}$$ 


* mc²: Represents the standard rest mass energy of the particle.
* Ḣ: Represents the total entropy rate (the sum of its conditional entropy and mutual information rates).
* The Informational "Cost": A particle that generates entropy rapidly reduces its effective Lagrangian weight. The universe effectively "pays" for entropy production by reducing the variational weight of that trajectory, meaning every true classical path is simultaneously an entropy-rate extremum.
* The Action: The time integral of this Lagrangian yields the Haller-Obidi Action. [4] 
  

## 3. The Haller-Obidi Correspondence
The Haller-Obidi Correspondence is the formal dictionary that links single-particle mechanics to universal field theories: [1, 5] 


* Scale Reduction: It proves that Haller’s single-particle entropy-action equation is actually a localized, single-particle projection of Obidi's much larger entropic field theory. [1, 5] 
* Field Integration: While Haller looked at a single diffusing particle, Obidi’s Obidi Action promotes entropy to a universal dynamical field (S(x)). When this universal field is mathematically restricted (localized) to a single particle's worldline, it collapses perfectly back into the Haller-Obidi formulation. [3, 5] 
  

## 4. The De Broglie–Haller–Obidi Entropic Lineage
This framework places ToE into a broader historical continuum of physics: [1] 

   1. Louis de Broglie: Originally intuited that a hidden thermodynamic or entropic mechanism must underpin quantum behavior.
   2. John Haller: Provided the precise informational and mathematical formula proving de Broglie's intuition at the single-particle limit.
   3. John Obidi: Elevated this structure to a universal field theory, showing how geometry, gravitational fields, and Einstein's equations naturally emerge from information-geometric tools (like Fisher-Rao metrics). [1, 6]
