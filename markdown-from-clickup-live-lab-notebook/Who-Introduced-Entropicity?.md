# Who Introduced Entropicity?

Who-Introduced-Entropicity?.md

The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) was introduced and formulated by scientific researcher and physicist [John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g2_n9fsg&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi). [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/my-publications-homepage-by-john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-7692279c6921)

He began developing the framework conceptually in early 2024, leading to its first public release in early (precisely 18th February) 2025. The theory serves as an alternative approach to modern physics by proposing that entropy is not merely a statistical measure of disorder, but rather the fundamental field substrate (an "entropic field") from which space, time, gravity, and quantum phenomena emerge. [1](https://entropicity.github.io/Theory-of-Entropicity-ToE/), [2](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6955ae35098cdc781f0f5f36)

(Note: While the foundational concept of entropy itself was famously introduced by German physicist **Rudolf Clausius** in 1865, the specific modern framework known as **"Entropicity"** or the **"Theory of Entropicity (ToE)"** belongs entirely to John Onimisi Obidi.) [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/a-brief-history-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-550a0fef7afe), [2](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/physics/entropy/), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/my-publications-homepage-by-john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-7692279c6921)
