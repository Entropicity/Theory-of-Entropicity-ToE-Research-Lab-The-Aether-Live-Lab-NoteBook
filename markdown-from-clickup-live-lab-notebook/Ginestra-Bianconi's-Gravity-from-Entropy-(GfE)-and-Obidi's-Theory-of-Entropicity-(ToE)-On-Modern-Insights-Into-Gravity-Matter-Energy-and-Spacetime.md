# Ginestra Bianconi's Gravity from Entropy (GfE) and Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE): On Modern Insights Into Gravity, Matter, Energy, and Spacetime 

While both frameworks model gravity through an informational lens, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) and Ginestra Bianconi's Gravity from Entropy (GfE) differ fundamentally in their core ontology, mechanics, and scope. Formally, ToE acts as a broader universal parent framework; Bianconi’s GfE functions mathematically as a [quasi-equilibrium, weak-gradient approximation](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5738123) of the overarching Obidi Action. ## Core Differences

| Feature | Bianconi's Gravity from Entropy (GfE) | Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| Ontology | Dualistic/Relational: Spacetime and matter are distinct primitives. Gravity emerges from the informational tension between them. | Monistic/Foundational: Spacetime and matter do not exist independently. They are emergent excitations of a single fundamental field ($S(x)$). |
| Mathematical Tool | Quantum Relative Entropy: Measures the distance between a physical metric and a matter-induced metric. | Information Geometry Differential: Integrates Fisher-Rao (classical), Fubini-Study (quantum), and Amari-Čencov $\alpha$-connections. |
| Primary Scope | Modified Gravity: Focuses on resolving cosmological puzzles like dark energy and local structure emergence. | Unified Physics: Aims to entirely unify general relativity, quantum mechanics, and standard model forces. |

## Summary of Distinctions

* **The Nature of Entropy:** In GfE, entropy is a relational measure between two preexisting metrics. In ToE, entropy is elevated to a primary, active, and continuous [physical field that curves existence itself](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55).
