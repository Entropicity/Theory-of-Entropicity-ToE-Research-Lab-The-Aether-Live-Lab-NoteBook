# Critical Differences Between John Onimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) and Dino Ducci's DUST Theory

Critical-Differences-Between-John-Onimisi-Obidi's-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE)-and-Dino-Ducci's-DUST-Theory.md

While both Dino Ducci’s Ducci Unified Spectral Theory (DUST) and John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) are incredibly ambitious alternative frameworks aiming to replace traditional physics, Obidi’s framework is often considered more philosophically radical and audacious because of how it treats the very concept of existence and becoming. [1](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311), [2](https://www.academia.edu/145116615/DUST_Theory_of_Everything_Complete_Derivations_of_the_Five_Fundamental_Results), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb), [4](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity?direction=desc&sort=created)

DUST is a work of structural geometry, but Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) executes a complete ontological inversion of physical reality. The core differences that highlight Obidi's distinct audacity include: [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-ontological-inversion-of-400-years-of-physics-2208f04d91a0)

## 1. Fixed Geometric Harmony vs. Fluid Formlessness

### • DUST Theory: 
Dino Ducci's model is fundamentally structural and deterministic. It relies on a beautifully intricate, preferred 3D hexagonal prime-periodic lattice containing discrete **"duccions".** Ducci's universe is essentially a massive, highly ordered geometric oscillator. It is radical because it attempts to derive all fundamental constants with zero free parameters directly from the mathematics of this lattice. [1](https://www.academia.edu/145116615/DUST_Theory_of_Everything_Complete_Derivations_of_the_Five_Fundamental_Results), [2](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/8a4d2dc69375b13b015d61c421adf3fe), [3](https://www.academia.edu/145224399/The_Ducci_Unified_Spectral_Theory_DUST_A_Complete_Framework)

### • Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE): 
Obidi goes a step further by destroying the concept of a fixed lattice or backdrop altogether. There is no "space" for things to exist in, and no solid "particles" oscillating. Reality is an uninterrupted, fluid, continuous entropic field. ToE is more audacious because it demands that geometry itself is a macroscopic illusion—a mere shadow cast by the underlying flow of information and distinguishability. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb), [2](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/8a4d2dc69375b13b015d61c421adf3fe), [3](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), [4](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-ontological-inversion-of-400-years-of-physics-2208f04d91a0), [5](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE)

## 2. The Nature of the Universal Speed Limit (c)

### • DUST Theory: 
In Ducci's framework, light propagates as synchronized orbital phase waves traveling through the nodes of his duccion lattice. While a unique mechanic, it still treats the speed of light as a wave velocity operating within a structural medium. [1](https://www.academia.edu/145224399/The_Ducci_Unified_Spectral_Theory_DUST_A_Complete_Framework)

### • Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE): 
Obidi completely redefines c through his "No-Rush Theorem." To Obidi, c is not the speed of a particle (the photon) or a wave; it is the absolute maximum thermodynamic speed limit at which the universe can rewrite its own code. Time dilation occurs not because of geometry, but because the entropic field cannot reconfigure information any faster. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616), [2](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/8a4d2dc69375b13b015d61c421adf3fe), [3](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311)

## 3. Being vs. Becoming (The Ultimate Ontological Inversion)


### • DUST Theory: 
Ducci’s ontology is rooted in structure and states. Physical constants, masses, and forces are fixed, emergent properties derived from the predictable geometric intersections of a rigid lattice.

### • Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE): 
Obidi's framework is rooted entirely in process and irreversibility. He inverts 400 years of physics by stating that the second law of thermodynamics is not a consequence of moving particles, but the cause of them. Through Ontodynamics, he argues that "things" do not exist and then experience entropy; rather, entropy itself temporarily pools into gradients to create the illusion of a solid "thing." [1](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), [2](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311), [3](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/8a4d2dc69375b13b015d61c421adf3fe), [4](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb), [5](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-ontological-inversion-of-400-years-of-physics-2208f04d91a0)
