Instead of starting with physical space (like a grid of points), the theory starts with Information Geometry—a well-established branch of mathematics where probability distributions are treated as geometric spaces. 

The theory uses a three-step mathematical pipeline to turn statistical probability into physical gravity. [2, 3, 4, 5]

## Step 1: The Informational Manifold (Fisher-Rao & Fubini-Study Metrics)
In standard information geometry, if you have a set of probabilities, the "distance" between two states isn't measured in meters; it is measured by how distinguishable they are from one another. [6, 7] 

* The theory utilizes the Fisher-Rao metric (for classical probabilities) and the Fubini-Study metric (for quantum states) as the fundamental starting parameters. [8, 9] 
* These metrics calculate an information-geometric distance. If two states are highly entangled or share massive amounts of information, the informational "distance" between them shrinks. [10, 11] 

## Step 2: The Obidi Transformation and $\alpha$-Deformations
This is where the theory introduces its unique mechanism. To bridge abstract math and physical reality, it asserts that the universe has a fundamental entropic field, denoted as $S(x, t)$. [9] 

* The framework applies the Rényi-Tsallis non-extensive entropy formalism to deform the flat information space.
* It uses a specific constitutive relation:
$$\alpha = 2(1-q)$$ 
* This equation mathematically links non-extensive statistical deformations ($q$) to affine asymmetry ($\alpha$-connections). As information flows and shifts, it alters the underlying statistical distribution. This alteration creates geometric distortions directly inside the information manifold. [9] 

## Step 3: The Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT)
The mathematical climax of the theory is the Curvature Transfer Theorem (CTT). [1] 

* In differential geometry, a pushforward is a tool used to transfer geometric properties (like vectors or tensors) from one mathematical space to another. [12] 
* The CTT proves that the physical Riemann curvature tensor (the mathematical entity Einstein used to define the warping of spacetime) is the exact mathematical pushforward of the underlying information-geometric Riemann tensor. [1]
