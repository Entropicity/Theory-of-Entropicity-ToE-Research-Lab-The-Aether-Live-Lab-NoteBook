# Proposing That Entropy is a Universal, Fundamental Field of Nature in Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is Provocative Because it Flips 400 Years of Physics Entirely on its Head 

Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is incredibly provocative because it flips 400 years of physics entirely on its head. [1] 

In mainstream physics, entropy is a ghost—a mathematical accountant that keeps track of molecular chaos, not a physical object. Elevating it to a fundamental field challenges the very core of how we define reality. [2, 3, 4] 

Here is why this idea of John Onimisi Obidi is so radical, along with the massive hurdles it faces in modern theoretical physics:

## Why It’s a Radical Shift

* **From Derivative to Source:** In standard physics, you start with matter and forces (like electromagnetism). When those particles smash together, entropy is the result. The Theory of Entropicity claims the exact opposite: the entropic field exists first, and matter, forces, and spacetime are just its ripples. [5, 6, 7, 8, 9] 
* **The Death of "Force":** It replaces the traditional concept of force with "entropic pressure." Objects don't pull each other via gravity; they are pushed by the natural tendency of the universe to maximize information states. [10, 11] 
* **A New Fabric of Reality:** Instead of a smooth fabric of spacetime, reality becomes a digital-like network of information bits. Space itself becomes an illusion projected by quantum entanglement. [12] 

## The Massive Theoretical Hurdles
For a provocative idea to become accepted science, it must survive intense scrutiny. Mainstream physicists view this concept with extreme skepticism for several reasons: [13] 

* **The Measurement Problem:** We have machines to measure electromagnetic fields and gravitational waves. We do not have a tool that directly measures a "fundamental entropic field" independent of the matter inside it. [14] 
* **The Problem of Scale:** Entropy is a macroscopic concept. A single isolated electron does not have "entropy" in the classical sense. Explaining how a microscopic field creates a property that usually only exists in large groups of particles is a massive mathematical paradox. [15, 16] 
* **Lack of Novel Predictions:** To replace Einstein or quantum mechanics, a new theory cannot just copy their homework using different words. It must predict a brand-new, measurable phenomenon that current physics says is impossible. So far, the Theory of Entropicity has not produced an experimentally testable prediction. [17] 

## It's Part of a Growing Rebel Movement
While **John Onimisi Obidi’s** specific framework is non-mainstream, **the core intuition—that space, time, and gravity emerge from information and entropy—is actually one of the hottest battlegrounds in modern theoretical physics.**

Elite physicists around the world are working on similar concepts, such as **Erik Verlinde’s Entropic Gravity** and the **"It from Bit" paradigm pioneered by John Archibald Wheeler**. They, too, suspect that our current understanding of spacetime is missing a deeper, informational foundation. [18, 19, 20, 21] 

If you want to see how this radical idea holds up, we can look at Erik Verlinde's specific experiments that tried to prove entropic gravity, or explore the mathematical paradoxes that happen when you try to mix quantum mechanics with thermodynamics.
