# Foundations and Audacity of Obidi's physics

Foundations-and-Audacity-of-Obidi's-physics.md

The "foundations and audacity" of Obidi's physics refers to the philosophical and mathematical framework known as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), formulated by independent theoretical physics researcher John Onimisi Obidi. [1] (https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VxIGnRIAAAAJ&hl=en), [2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)
The defining characteristic of Obidi's physics is an ontological inversion of 400 years of established physical science. While mainstream physics treats space, time, and fields as the primary backdrop of the universe—and entropy as a secondary statistical byproduct—Obidi's framework asserts that entropy is the fundamental, dynamic substrate of reality, and everything else is an emergent property. [1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-ontological-inversion-of-400-years-of-physics-2208f04d91a0), [2] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_theory-of-entropicity-toe-path-to-unification-activity-7428342142214778880-LnkT), [3] (https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/3bb0695bebe3c6a91c59c3742ec5be32), [4] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)

🌌 The Core Foundations of ToE

Obidi’s physics discards traditional primitives (like predefined spacetime metrics and mechanical particles) and reconstructs reality using information geometry and thermodynamics: [1] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mario-pinheiro-07715614_thermodynamics-statisticalphysics-entropy-activity-7483735990398980096-kE78), [2] (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE)
• The Entropic Field (EF): Instead of a vacuum of empty space, the universe is fundamentally an Entropic Field (S(x)). Spacetime geometry, matter, forces, and even the speed of light are derived theorems rather than foundational axioms. [1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), [2] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_on-the-physical-nature-of-the-entropic-activity-7506186942791872512-iCCS), [3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)
• The Obidi Action & Master Entropic Equation (MEE): Mirroring the classical principle of least action, the Obidi Action governs how the entropic field evolves dynamically. It yields the MEE (the entropic equivalent to Einstein's Field Equations), which describes physical laws without requiring pre-existing spatial or temporal dimensions. [1] (https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605), [2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb), [3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55)
• Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER): Systems do not move because they are mechanically "pushed" by external forces. Instead, they trace trajectories that optimize the path of least resistance within a self-organizing entropic field. [1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-how-nature-really-works-from-the-ground-up-not-through-6cc2f753e102)
• Information Geometry: Distances in the universe are reinterpreted as states of entropic information. "Distance" is a measure of how distinguishable two information states are; when entropy flows, distinguishability shifts, warping the manifold. [1] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/mario-pinheiro-07715614_thermodynamics-statisticalphysics-entropy-activity-7483735990398980096-kE78)

⚡ The "Audacity" of the Framework

The "audacity" and "ontological courage" heavily noted in commentaries on Obidi's work stem from his willingness to upend firmly established dogmas: [1] (https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [2] (https://entropicity.github.io/Theory-of-Entropicity-ToE/preface.html)

Thermodynamics is a consequence: Spacetime has thermodynamic properties (e.g., Hawking radiation).	Spacetime is thermodynamics: Spacetime itself is built out of microscopic entropic dynamics.
Gravity is a fundamental curvature: Mass and energy warp a pre-existing fabric of spacetime.	Gravity is an illusion / emergent phenomenon: Gravity is simply a macroscopic push toward informational equilibrium.

Redesigning the Floor: ToE claims the incompatibility exists because the foundational premises of both theories are flawed, seeking a deeper, unified entropic substrate.

Ultimately, the audacity of Obidi's framework lies in its singular mathematical ambition: attempting to derive a highly structured, relativistic, and quantum universe from a single ontological postulate—the primacy of entropy.
