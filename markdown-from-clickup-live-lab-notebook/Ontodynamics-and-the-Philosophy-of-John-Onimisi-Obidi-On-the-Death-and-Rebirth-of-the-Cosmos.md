# Ontodynamics and the Philosophy of John Onimisi Obidi: On the Death and Rebirth of the Cosmos 

John Onimisi Obidi’s philosophy centers on the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** and a framework called ontodynamics, which **defines existence as entropic motion and continuous informational redistribution and negotiation.** It posits entropy as the fundamental, dynamic causal field of reality [**the Vis Viva Entropica**] rather than a passive measure of disorder. Obidi's philosophical framework spans several core principles:

## Ontodynamics & Entropic Primacy: 
He redefines being as "stabilized entropic persistence" and becoming as "irreversible entropic redistribution". Everything from motion and gravity to time itself emerges from the continuous flow of this active entropic field.

## Probabilistic Cosmos: 
Moving away from classical determinism, his philosophy asserts that the universe operates on "probabilistic entropicity" where the future is fundamentally open, allowing for genuine novelty, creativity, and quantum indeterminacy.

## Obidi Action & The Master Entropic Equation: 
Analogous to variational principles in general relativity, the Obidi Action is the foundational premise dictating how the universe continuously optimizes its entropy flow.

## Death as Transition: 
Rather than ontological annihilation, death is viewed as an **"entropic transition**." The information constituting an entity is never truly destroyed, but rather dissolved and redistributed back into the broader entropic field.

## Core Tenets of Ontodynamics
According to Obidi's writings on [Medium and the Canonical Archives](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/on-obidis-ontodynamics-of-being-and-becoming-in-his-theory-of-entropicity-toe-552720452514), Ontodynamics redefines classical metaphysics through a few core philosophical axioms:

* **The Primacy of Becoming over Being:** Traditional philosophy treats "Being" as static and permanent. Obidi [redefines "Being"](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-philosophical-analysis-of-being-and-becoming-based-on-his-theory-of-entropicity-toe-and-ee68e95fccb5) as stabilized entropic persistence, and "Becoming" as the irreversible, continuous redistribution of the universal entropy field. [2, 6] 
* **Ontological Courage:** Obidi elevates the existential concept of courage—traditionally used to survive dread or meaninglessness—into an epistemic tool. In his philosophy, it represents the intellectual audacity required to overturn inherited scientific assumptions and reconstruct physical primitives. [6] 
* **Death as Transition, Not Annihilation:** Using his philosophical corollaries, Obidi posits that an individual is an "Entropic Particular". Because information cannot be completely destroyed under his axioms, death is viewed as a radical entropic transition—the redistribution of an individual's unique informational trajectory back into the broader universal field. [7] 
* **The Exceeded Predicate (Theology):** In resolving category errors regarding the existence of God, **Obidi introduces a core metaphysical axiom:** ***any predicate applied to God is automatically exceeded by God.*** [8]
