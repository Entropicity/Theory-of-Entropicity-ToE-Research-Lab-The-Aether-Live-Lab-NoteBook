## 2. The De Broglie–Haller–Obidi Entropic Lineage (Physics)

In modern academic literature, the **"Obidi Lineage"** refers to a conceptual lineage in theoretical physics and mathematical information theory formalized by researcher physicist and philosopher John Onimisi Obidi. [8, 9] 

* **The Theory:** This lineage marks the mathematical progression of unified field theories, known specifically as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). [8, 9] 
* **The Progression:** It charts how the physical intuition of Louis de Broglie (who suspected a hidden thermodynamic substrate in quantum mechanics) evolved into John Haller’s 2015 "action-as-entropy" framework, and finally culminated in the Obidi Action.

The Obidi Action attempts a grand unification by treating entropy as the fundamental substrate from which gravity, geometry, and quantum laws emerge. [8, 9]
