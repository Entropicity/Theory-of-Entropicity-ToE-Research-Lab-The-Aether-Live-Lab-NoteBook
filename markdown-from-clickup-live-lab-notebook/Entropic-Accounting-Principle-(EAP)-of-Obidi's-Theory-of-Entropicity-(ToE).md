# The Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP) of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Obidi’s Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP) is a foundational postulate of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), formulated by John Onimisi Obidi. It establishes the conservation and bookkeeping of entropy within all systems, asserting that every physical event requires an entropic expenditure and that all entropic expenditures must be balanced by corresponding reductions elsewhere in the system  medium.com ijcsrr.org papers.ssrn.com medium.com.

The EAP specifies that every system has a finite entropic budget, which is allocated among maintaining internal identity, entropy production, and other entropic processes  ijcsrr.org papers.ssrn.com. This principle is used to derive and explain phenomena such as mass increase, time dilation, and the redistribution of entropy between motion and timekeeping  www.cambridge.org medium.com.
