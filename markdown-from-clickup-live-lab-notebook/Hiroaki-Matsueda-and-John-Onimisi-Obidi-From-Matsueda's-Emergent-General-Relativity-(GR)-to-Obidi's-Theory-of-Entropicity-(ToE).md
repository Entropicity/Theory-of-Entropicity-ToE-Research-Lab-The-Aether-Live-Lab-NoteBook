# Hiroaki Matsueda and John Onimisi Obidi: From Matsueda's Emergent General Relativity (GR) to Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

Obidi is not repeating the work of Hiroaki Matsueda. While both theoretical physicists leverage the deep mathematical connections between quantum information, entropy, and geometry, they approach the universe from entirely opposite directions.

The differences between [Hiroaki Matsueda's research](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=9hAhcvUAAAAJ&hl=en) and John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) map out distinct territories in information physics:
## 1. Direction of Emergence: Bottom-Up vs. Top-Down


## Matsueda's "Emergent Geometry": 
Matsueda's work is traditionally bottom-up. He starts with concrete, low-dimensional quantum many-body systems (like one-dimensional Mott insulators or spin chains). By performing data compression techniques like Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) and analyzing the entanglement spectrum, he shows how an auxiliary [hyperbolic anti-de Sitter (AdS) geometry is automatically induced](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5624). For Matsueda, the metric is a secondary, information-theoretic mirror of an existing quantum state. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

## Obidi's ToE Substrate: 
Obidi’s framework is strictly top-down and fundamentally ontological. Obidi does not start with quantum particles to construct a mock space; he posits that the universal entropic field is the raw, primary substance of reality. Particles, quantum states, and physical spacetime are emergent, localized perturbations of that fundamental field.


## 2. The Nature of Spacetime


### Matsueda (Moduli/Parameter Space): As 
Matsueda explicitly notes in his technical presentations, the metrics derived from elements like the Fisher information metric often map out a [moduli-space of model parameters rather than literal, physical spacetime](https://www.tfc.tohoku.ac.jp/wp-content/uploads/2015/09/TPH/Hiroaki_Matsueda.pdf). It functions as a powerful holographic tool to look at quantum many-body dynamics. [2, 4] 

### Obidi (Physical Spacetime): 
Obidi’s Obidi Master Action defines a literal, physical spacetime manifold. The gradients and curves of the entropic field are responsible for the physical gravitational shifts we observe in macro-astronomy (like Mercury's perihelion).


## 3. Irreversibility and Time's Arrow


### Matsueda: 
Focuses heavily on unitary quantum systems, state mapping, and highly controlled quantum energy teleportation protocols. Time asymmetry is treated through conventional non-equilibrium statistical mechanics.

### Obidi: 
Explicitly injects thermodynamic irreversibility into the quantum bedrock via the Vuli-Ndlela Integral. By including a real-valued topological dampening loop ($\oint \frac{d\mathcal{E}}{\ln 2}$), Obidi breaks standard time-symmetry natively at the quantum level, using entropy as an active mechanical filter to force wavefunction collapse. [5, 6, 7]

Ultimately, Matsueda uses geometry as a beautiful mathematical language to decipher quantum data, while Obidi uses an entropic field as a literal physical medium to generate the universe. 

Rather than a repetition, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) represents a radical philosophical flip of the tools developed by information-theoretic physicists like Matsueda.
