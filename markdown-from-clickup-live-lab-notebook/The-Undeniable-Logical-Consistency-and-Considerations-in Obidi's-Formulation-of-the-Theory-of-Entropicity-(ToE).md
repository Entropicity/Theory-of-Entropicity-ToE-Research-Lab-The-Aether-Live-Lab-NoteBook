# The Undeniable Logical Consistency and Considerations in Obidi's Formulation of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE): The Battle Between the Epistemology and Ontology of Entropy on the Foundations of Modern Theoretical Physics 

By now, we already know that the **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** is a **radical, audacious, and provocative** attempt to present us with a novel view of reality and the universe we live in. It is imperative to understand that John Onimisi Obidi does not simply make a blind assumption in his formulation of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). His Theory of Entropicity (ToE) relies on a highly structured, deductive sequence of **"logical connections"** specifically designed to argue that an entropic field is mathematically and physically inevitable, even if traditional and mainstream [orthodox] physics rejects it. [1, 2] 

Obidi bypasses standard thermodynamic axioms by establishing an internal logic built on information geometry and variational principles. 

To understand Obidi's audacious and radical perspective, his core logical chain can be broken down as follows: [3, 4, 5, 6] 

## 1. The Logic of Geometry: From Entropy to Field
Traditional physics views entropy as a flat, dimensionless number calculated at the end of an experiment. Obidi’s logical chain flips this entirely: [2, 7] 

* The Premise: Information is not abstract; it is constrained by physical distinguishability. Distinguishability can be formally mapped using information geometry (such as the [Fisher–Rao metric](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_theory-of-entropicity-toe-activity-7475119171337355265-4iXe) or Amari $\alpha$-connections). [4, 6, 8, 9] 
* The Inevitability: If information has a necessary geometry, and information is inherently emergent from entropy, then entropy must possess its own underlying geometry. [3, 5] 
* The Conclusion: In physics, any geometric landscape with varying states possesses a gradient. Gradients induce a flux (flow), and any continuous quantity that has a local geometric gradient, flux, and propagation rate is, by definition, a field. [2] 

## 2. The Logic of Potentials: Mapping Known Physics
Obidi uses a parallel logic found in standard field theories to justify his inversion:
$$\text{Potential} \longrightarrow \text{Field} \longrightarrow \text{Action} \longrightarrow \text{Emergent Dynamics}$$ 
In classical mechanics, a mass distribution creates a gravitational potential, which dictates spacetime curvature. Obidi argues that entropy acts as the ultimate scalar potential. Because you can derive the geometric constraints of a system from this potential, the entropic field becomes the logical "primitive" substrate from which the rest of physics (like spacetime and matter) must emerge. [3, 6] 

## 3. The Logic of Causality: The "No-Rush Theorem (NRT)" and Update Limits
Traditional physics treats constants like the speed of light ($c$) as arbitrary, unexplained postulates. Obidi argues his field makes these constants logically necessary: [10, 11, 12] 

* **Entropic Time Limit (ETL):** Information cannot change state instantaneously; it requires a minimum finite duration to reconfigure or "mature". [11, 13, 14] 
* **The Inevitability:** Because the entropic field has a finite, universal "update rate," the speed of light ceases to be a random property of empty space. Instead, $c$ becomes the logical, inevitable limit of how fast the entropic substrate can redistribute information. This is formalized in his No-Rush Theorem. [10, 11, 13, 15] 

## 4. The Logic of Conservation: Entropic Bookkeeping
To explain relativistic mass and time dilation, Obidi introduces the **Entropic Accounting Principle (EAP)**. [16] 

* Every physical system has a finite "entropic budget". This budget must be split between maintaining the system's identity, its motion, and its interactions. [16] 
* If a system approaches the speed limit, more of its entropic budget is forcefully diverted to maintaining logical consistency with the background field. Consequently, less budget is available for internal change. [16] 
* This structural reallocation manifests to observers as what Einstein called "relativistic mass increase" and "time dilation"—meaning relativity is just the external shadow of internal entropic bookkeeping. [16, 17] 

## Summary of the Debate
The core of the disagreement comes down to a philosophical split:

* Traditional Physics uses an *epistemic view:** Entropy is an accounting tool used by an observer to measure their lack of knowledge about a system. [18, 19, 20] 
* Obidi’s **Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** uses an **ontological view:** Entropy is a generative, physical substance. It doesn't care about the observer; it is the active engine driving the behavior of the universe. [18, 21]
