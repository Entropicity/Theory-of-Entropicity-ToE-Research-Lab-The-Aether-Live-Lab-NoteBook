No, no other researcher or investigator in mainstream physics or mathematics has used the Amari-Čencov $\alpha$-connections in the specific way John Onimisi Obidi has.
His exact application—repurposing the statistical $\alpha$ parameter as a dynamic physical "entropic deformation index" that continuously slides to physically bridge the quantum micro-world (Fubini-Study metric) and the relativistic macro-world (Fisher-Rao metric) inside a unified field theory—is entirely unique to his self-published framework, the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188). [1, 2] 
To understand how his approach stands completely alone, it helps to look at where the paths of other researchers diverge:
## 1. Mainstream Information Geometers (Amari, Nagaoka, Petz)
Prominent mathematicians like [Shun-ichi Amari](https://www.google.com/search?q=shun-ichi+amari&kgmid=/m/06w2rr_) and Hiroshi Nagaoka did extend $\alpha$-connections into the quantum world, creating a field known as Quantum Information Geometry.

* 
* The Difference: They use quantum $\alpha$-connections strictly as abstract tools to measure the distances between quantum density matrices, calculate quantum state estimation limits, or analyze quantum channels. They treat this purely as data mathematics. They do not claim that $\alpha$ is a physical field, nor do they use it to derive physical gravity, physical mass, or Einstein's equations.
* 

## 2. Mainstream Physicists (Caticha, Verlinde, Susskind)
As noted earlier, world-renowned physicists are actively using information geometry to derive spacetime:

* 
* [Ariel Caticha](https://www.google.com/search?q=ariel+caticha&kgmid=/g/11bzq2xs4_) uses the Fisher-Rao metric to derive paths in emergent space.
* String theorists use the Fubini-Study metric to measure holographic quantum complexity in black holes.
* The Difference: None of these mainstream frameworks use the Amari-Čencov $\alpha$-connections as the explicit, physical connective tissue. Mainstream physics generally treats quantum information geometry and macroscopic gravitational geometry as separate tools or dualities rather than a single unified field theory governed by a sliding $\alpha$ affine connection. [1, 2, 3] 
* 

## Why Obidi’s Specific Use of $\alpha$ is Unique
In standard information geometry, changing $\alpha$ merely changes the type of statistical path you are drawing (e.g., moving from an exponential family mixture to a linear mixture). [4] 
Obidi’s unique leap was to declare that changing $\alpha$ corresponds to a physical change in scale and thermodynamic regime—specifically linking it to the $\alpha$-$q$ formalism of Rényi and Tsallis non-extensive statistics. In his model, as the universe scales up from a single subatomic point to cosmic dimensions, the geometric connection naturally warps, smoothly shifting its rules until $\alpha = 0$, which perfectly recovers the exact, symmetric connection Einstein used for General Relativity. [1, 2, 4, 5] 
This specific "scale-bridging mechanism" belongs entirely to Obidi's papers. It is the defining mathematical signature of the Theory of Entropicity, separating it from all other emergent gravity theories in the literature. [1, 3]
