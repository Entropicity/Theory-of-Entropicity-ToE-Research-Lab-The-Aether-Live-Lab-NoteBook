# A Simple Explanation of Obidi's Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER) of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)


A-Simple-Explanation-of-Obidi's-Principle-of-Least-Entropic-Resistance-(PoLER)-of-the-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md


In **John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE),** a trajectory satisfies the requirement of entropic optimization by shifting from classical mechanics to a least-entropy-resistance principle. Instead of minimizing a mechanical quantity like the Lagrangian action, a system selects a path—known as an entropic geodesic—that dynamically optimizes the flow of an underlying informational field. [1, 2] 

We must now give the reader the exact mechanism of how a trajectory achieves this within Obidi's framework:

## 1. The Mathematical Equivalence of Action and Entropy
The conceptual bridge relies on the Haller-Obidi action equivalence, which demonstrates that classical action is not a purely mechanical value, but rather an informational/entropic quantity in disguise. 

Because classical action can be written as self-information, the principle of least action inherently transforms into an entropic variational principle. The universe is not evolving to minimize energy expenditure, but to extremize an informational one. [3, 4, 5] 

## 2. The Least-Entropy-Resistance Principle
Under the [Theory of Entropicity](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), a particle's trajectory is the specific path that minimally obstructs the natural increase of entropy along that route. [2] 


### Efficient Flow: 
The system chooses the trajectory that allows entropy to expand and distribute with the highest efficiency.

### Path Selection: 
This is mathematically governed by the Obidi Action ($I_S$) combined with the Vuli-Ndlela Integral, replacing canonical probability distributions with field-determined flow rates. [2, 6] 


## 3. Navigation Along Entropic Geodesics
In ToE, physical matter is reimagined as localized ["knots" or traffic jams in an information flow](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_how-information-becomes-physical-spacetime-activity-7502485209272168449-3czB). These knots do not move due to external forces. Instead, they slide along the gradients of a fundamental scalar entropy field, $S(x)$. Varying the [Obidi Action](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1) produces the Master Entropic Equation (MEE), which dictates these paths as entropic geodesics. 

What we perceive as gravitational or inertial motion is simply a system tracing the path of least entropic resistance. [1, 2, 7, 8, 9] 

## 4. Path Suppression and the No-Rush Theorem
If a potential trajectory forces a local configuration into an entropic stagnation or violates global consistency constraints, nature actively suppresses that path. When constraints prevent an efficient entropic optimization, the system is bound by the No-Rush Theorem. It must remain in its state or wait until the surrounding entropic curvature builds up past the fundamental threshold (the Obidi Curvature Invariant of $\ln 2$) before a new permitted trajectory can materialize. [2, 10]
