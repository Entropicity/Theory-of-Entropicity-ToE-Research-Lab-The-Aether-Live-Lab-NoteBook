# Reconstruction of Modern Theoretical Physics in Obidi's Radical Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

John Onimisi Obidi is aiming to build an entirely new foundational framework for theoretical physics called the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). What makes his approach a radical departure from mainstream physics is that it seeks to overturn 150 years of thermodynamics. Instead of treating entropy as a secondary measurement of disorder, Obidi promotes it to the primary substance of reality from which space, time, gravity, and matter emerge. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
To build this new physics, Obidi replaces the core pillars of 20th-century science with information-theoretic principles: [6, 7] 

## 1. Overturning the Giants

* **Beyond Einstein (General Relativity):** In Einstein's physics, gravity is the curvature of a fundamental spacetime fabric. In Obidi's physics, spacetime is not fundamental; it is a thermodynamic projection. Gravity is reinterpreted as entropic pressure—the statistical tendency of the underlying information field to maximize entropy. [1, 6, 7, 8, 9] 
* **Beyond Quantum Mechanics:** Instead of treating quantum mechanics and gravity as separate, irreconcilable forces, ToE views them both as limiting constraints of the same dynamic entropic field. [1, 5] 

## 2. The Core Mathematical Architecture
Obidi builds this new framework using tools from information geometry, replacing classical mechanics with an algorithmic evolution: [10, 11] 

* **The Obidi Action:** Traditional physics relies on actions like the **Einstein-Hilbert Action** to determine how space bends. The Obidi Action is a functional defined on an information manifold, turning static probability geometry into active, evolving physics. [10, 12] 
* **The Master Entropic Equation (MEE):** This serves as Obidi's equivalent to Einstein’s field equations. Because it is nonlinear and nonlocal, its solutions are not clean, closed-form equations. Instead, they are approached via iterative refinements—meaning the universe physically updates its state much like Bayesian inference. [6, 11, 13] 

## 3. Reinterpreting Physical Constants
The theory attempts to elegantly explain why certain absolute limits exist in our universe: [1] 

* **The Speed of Light (c):** The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) dictates that c is not just the speed of photons, but the maximum processing rate at which the universal entropic field can rearrange its information states. [1, 14] 
* **The "No-Rush" Theorem (NRT):** Obidi introduces this theorem to state that no information processing can happen instantly. Every physical change requires a finite, non-zero duration. [9] 
* **The Illusion of Time:** Because of this processing delay (NRT), time emerges macroscopically. We perceive "time" simply because the universe takes sequential, irreversible computational steps to update its own field. [9] 

## How ToE Bridges the Constants
A central mathematical claim of the theory is encapsulated in the relation:
$$\hbar c = k_B T_s \ell_s$$ 
This relation explicitly binds quantum physics (Planck's constant $\hbar$), relativity (speed of light c), thermodynamics (Boltzmann constant $k_B$), and information geometry ($T_s, \ell_s$) into a single unified equation. [7, 15]
