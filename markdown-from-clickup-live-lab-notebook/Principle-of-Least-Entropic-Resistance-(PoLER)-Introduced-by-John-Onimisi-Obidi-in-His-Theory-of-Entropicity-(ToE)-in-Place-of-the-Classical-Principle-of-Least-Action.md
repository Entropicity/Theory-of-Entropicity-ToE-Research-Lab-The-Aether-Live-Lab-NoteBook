## Key Concepts of PoLER


### The Obidi Action Principle: 
Instead of minimizing a mechanical action functional (the difference between kinetic and potential energy), the framework minimizes an entropic curvature functional. [3] 

### Entropic Geodesics: 
Physical systems and particles do not respond to traditional external forces or move through a pre-existing geometric spacetime. Instead, they follow "entropic geodesics"—the trajectories that minimize cumulative entropic resistance or allow entropy to increase most efficiently along that path. [2, 4, 5] 

### Emergent Physics: 
Within this framework, what we traditionally perceive as mass, energy, and gravitational attraction are redefined as manifestations of informational gradients and thermodynamic resistance within a universal entropy field. [1, 2]
