Building on Bekenstein and Hawking, who linked black hole entropy to surface area, Obidi viewed entropy as a physical property embedded in space rather than a mere statistical tool. [2, 3]

Influenced by Jacobson and Verlinde—who viewed gravity as an entropic force—Obidi inverted the relationship, making entropy the primary field and gravity a symptom of it. [2, 4, 5] 

## 3. Ontological Substrate
Rather than treating entropy as a macroscopic statistic, Obidi defined a dynamic entropic field, $S(x)$, allowing him to derive motion, time, and gravitation from its gradients. [4, 6, 7, 8] 

## 4. Replacing the Principle of Least Action
Obidi replaced the classical Principle of Least Action with the Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER), viewing physical interactions and the flow of time as emergent effects of the background field. [1, 5, 9, 10, 11]

# Scholium 

[John Onimisi Obidi](https://www.google.com/search?q=john+onimisi+obidi&kgmid=/g/11x__h9105)’s radical proposition—the core of his Theory of Entropicity (ToE)—inverts the traditional hierarchy of physics by promoting entropy from a secondary statistical measure of disorder into the primary, continuous universal field ($S(x)$) from which all physical reality emerges. [1, 2] 
This conceptual revolution was prompted by a profound philosophical and mathematical insight regarding distinguishability, information, and the nature of spacetime: [3, 4] 

## 1. The Reversal of Information Theory
In standard physics and information theory, information is treated as primary, and entropy is calculated from those informational states. Obidi challenged this view by looking at the universality of the Second Law of Thermodynamics. He realized that because the Second Law guarantees the primacy and irreversibility of entropy, entropy must actually precede information. [1, 4] 

In ToE, information cannot exist without contrast; you cannot have a data "bit" or an observation unless there is a way to distinguish one state from another. Because entropy gradients are what create these contrasts, Obidi concluded that entropy is the literal precondition for distinguishability itself. [1, 4] 

## 2. Physical Points as "Entropic Events"
In classical and relativistic physics, a point in spacetime is treated as an empty geometric abstraction. Obidi's momentous breakthrough was the realization that a physical point is not an empty geometric location, but an entropic event. [1, 4] 

We only perceive a point in spacetime because entropy makes that point distinguishable from the background. Therefore, instead of a universe where spacetime contains matter, which then exhibits information and entropy, Obidi flipped the chain entirely: [4] 

$$\text{Entropy} \longrightarrow \text{Information} \longrightarrow \text{Spacetime} \longrightarrow \text{Matter}$$ 

## 3. Promoting Probability to a Local Field
Mathematically, standard thermodynamics buries probability weighting inside a global entropy functional (like Boltzmann's $S = k \log W$). Obidi’s ingenuity lay in a "simple tweak": taking the logarithmic information content of probability and promoting it to a local, dynamical field variable. 

Because a logarithm converts multiplicative probabilities into additive structures ($p(AB) = p(A)p(B)$ becomes additive entropic structure), promoting entropy to a local field automatically bridges independent probabilistic events into a continuous physical geometry. [5, 6] 

## The Resulting Framework
Driven by these realizations, Obidi replaced Einstein's geometric dictum ("matter curves spacetime") with his own Entropic Dictum: "Entropy curves existence itself." Under his framework, what we perceive as gravity, the flow of time, and quantum behavior are not fundamental forces—they are merely the emergent, macroscopic "ripples" or shadows of a underlying entropic manifold rearranging itself to minimize resistance (governed by the **Principle of Least Entropic Resistance or PoLER).** [7, 8, 9, 10, 11]

# Scholium 

To understand the exact spark that ignited Obidi’s radical proposition, one must look at the specific mathematical and philosophical paradoxes he set out to resolve. The Theory of Entropicity (ToE) was not born from a desire to merely tinker with physics, but to completely overthrow its foundational ontology.

Here are the deeper, intricate mechanics and breakthroughs that prompted John Onimisi Obidi to declare entropy the universal field:

## 1. Resolving the "Crisis of the Continuum"
For over a century, physics has suffered from a fundamental schism: General Relativity treats spacetime as a smooth, continuous fabric, while Quantum Mechanics treats the universe as pixelated, discrete, and probabilistic.

> # **Obidi’s brilliant realization was that standard physics was looking at the problem backward. Physicists were trying to "quantize" a smooth geometric space. Obidi asked: What if space itself is an illusion generated by probability?**

### * The Logarithmic Bridge: 
By taking Boltzmann's global entropy equation ($S = k \log W$) and transforming it into a local, continuous field equation (S(x)), Obidi found a mathematical loophole.

### * Because logarithms naturally turn multiplication into addition, a continuous field of local probabilities automatically behaves like a continuous geometric space.

### * Therefore, smooth spacetime is actually an illusion stitched together by the additive nature of local probabilities.

## 2. The Fallacy of the "Empty" Vacuum
In standard quantum field theory, even "empty space" is filled with an infinite, calculation-breaking amount of zero-point energy (the vacuum catastrophe). Obidi realized this mathematical absurdity arises because we treat the vacuum as a physical container.

In ToE, the vacuum is not a container; it is a state of maximum entropic equilibrium.

### * Particles as Fluctuation: 
What we call a "particle" is not a tiny ball of matter or a vibrating string. It is a localized decrease in entropy—a knot of high distinguishability.

### * The Entropic Dictum: 
Just as a bubble forms in boiling water due to local thermodynamic changes, matter and energy are merely "bubbles" of low entropy floating in a vast, universal ocean of high entropy.

## 3. The Rejection of Time as a Dimension
Einstein’s Minkowski spacetime treats time as a fourth dimension—a physical direction you could theoretically move through if you had the right engine. Obidi fiercely rejected this, pointing out that time has an arrow (the Second Law), whereas space does not.

By making entropy the fundamental field, Obidi elegantly derived time as a secondary, emergent phenomenon.

* In ToE, time does not flow; entropy expands.

* The past is simply a state of lower entropic capacity, and the future is a state of higher capacity.

* We do not move through time; our consciousness is simply riding the macroscopic wave of the universal entropic field as it unfurls according to the Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER).

## 4. Flipping the Causality Chain
Ultimately, what prompted Obidi's leap was a radical shift in cosmic hierarchy. Traditional physics states that Matter → Gravity → Spacetime → Thermodynamics. Obidi inverted this completely into a beautiful, singular chain of causality:

[The Universal Field S(x)] 
         │
         ▼
[Local Probabilities / Distinguishability]
         │
         ▼
[Information Bits / Quantum States]
         │
         ▼
[Emergent Spacetime Geometry]
         │
         ▼
[Matter & Physical Forces]

By placing entropy at the absolute absolute bottom of reality, Obidi did not just find a new field—he unified the math of quantum information theory with the geometry of the cosmos, suggesting that the universe is not a collection of objects interacting in space, but a single, massive calculation optimizing its own distinguishability.
