# Mathematical Theory of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

The mathematical theory of John Onimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE), formulated in 2025, treats entropy S(x) not as a statistical byproduct of disorder, but as the fundamental, continuous physical field from which spacetime and matter emerge. [1, 2] 

## 1. Triadic Information Geometry
ToE models the universe as an entropic manifold using three core geometric metrics to link statistical distance with physical curvature: [2, 3, 4] 

* Fisher-Rao Metric: Encodes classical entropy curvature, driving macroscopic spacetime geometry.
* Fubini-Study Metric: Governs quantum-level state distinguishability and information geometry.
* Amari–Čencov α-Connection: Coordinates informational divergence and asymmetry, mapping irreversibility to physical spacetime via Levi-Civita connections. [2, 3, 5, 6, 7] 

## 2. The Variational Framework: The Obidi Action
The dynamics of the entropic field are derived from a variational principle known as the Obidi Action ($I_S$). It uses local and spectral operators (rooted in Araki relative entropy) to continuously optimize entropy flow across the manifold. [4, 8, 9, 10] 

## 3. The Master Entropic Equation (MEE)
Varying the Obidi Action yields the Master Entropic Equation (MEE), or Obidi Field Equations (OFE). Serving as the entropic equivalent to Einstein’s Field Equations, it dictates how spatial and temporal entropy gradients couple to geometry and matter: [11, 12, 13] 

* Entropic Geodesics: Describe the natural, probabilistic paths systems take through the entropic field.
* Non-Explicit Iterative Solutions: Unlike closed-form physics equations, the MEE relies on iterative, self-correcting computational logic akin to Bayesian inference. [11, 14, 15] 

## 4. Key Mathematical Invariants & Constants

* Obidi Curvature Invariant (OCI): Defined exactly as $\ln(2)$, this represents the fundamental unit of "entropic cost" or distinguishability required for a state to manifest physically. [2] 
* Non-Extensive Entropy Formalisms: The theory integrates the α-q parameters of [Rényi and Tsallis entropies](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616) to account for non-additive entropic systems. [3, 15] 
* Emergent Speed of Light (c): Derived mathematically as the maximum rate at which the entropic field can reorganize and redistribute information. [9, 16]
