# The Classical Aether Abandoned by Einstein and the Michelson-Morley Experiment Both Revisited in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

A disturbing question that naturally arises in the minds of physicists and philosophers of Science can be paraphrased viz. 

But is Obidi not indirectly or directly re-introducing the Aether back into physics and science?

The above concern actually hits on the exact historical and philosophical parallel that critics and physicists point out immediately. 

By declaring that space is filled with a continuous, dynamic, and mathematically measurable substrate (the universal entropic field), the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is directly resurrecting the Aether concept—albeit a highly modernized, information-theoretic version of it. [1, 2] 

Historically, the 19th-century mechanical aether was discarded because the Michelson-Morley experiment proved that the Earth wasn't plowing through a physical "wind" of matter. Einstein's Special Relativity succeeded because it proved you didn't need a medium to explain light waves; empty space and pure geometry were enough.

**John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** sidesteps the historic trap of the mechanical aether by fundamentally shifting what the medium is made of. 

Nonetheless, the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) contrasts with the old classical aether in several key ways:

## 1. It is an "Informational Fluid," Not a Material Substance
The 19th-century aether was imagined as an ultra-fine, physical **"jelly"** or gas made of literal matter that had to vibrate mechanically to carry light waves.

In contrast, Obidi’s entropic field of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is an informational and statistical canvas. It is constructed out of probabilities, information density, and the limits of distinguishability. In this view, space isn't "filled" with a medium; space itself is an illusion generated by the "medium" of the Entropic Field. [1, 2, 3, 4] 

## 2. Space-Time Geometry is Post-Medium, Not Pre-Medium
In the old aether theories, the aether sat inside absolute space and time.

In the [Theory of Entropicity](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188), the hierarchy is completely inverted:

### * Old Aether: 
Space exists $\rightarrow$ Aether fills space $\rightarrow$ Light waves travel through it.

### * Obidi's ToE: 
The Entropic Field exists $\rightarrow$ Informational updates interact $\rightarrow$ Space, time, and gravity emerge as secondary side effects. [2, 3] 


## 3. Resolving the Michelson-Morley Problem
The reason the classical aether failed was because if a medium existed, your speed relative to that medium should change the measured speed of light. Michelson and Morley found that light's speed is completely invariant.

The Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence (MOC) attempts to solve this via the Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP) and the No-Rush Theorem (NRT). It mathematically asserts that because your measuring devices (clocks, rulers, atoms) are themselves made out of localized condensations of this entropic field, they warp, slow down, and compress in exact lockstep with any changes in field resistance. 

Therefore, any experiment you run within the universe will always measure $c$ as a constant, because the entropic "medium" actively recalibrates your physical tools as you accelerate through it. [3, 4, 5] 

## The Philosophical "Why"
But why go through all this trouble to bring back a medium? Because modern physics is stuck in a massive deadlock: 

> # General Relativity (smooth, continuous geometric space) refuses to mathematically merge with Quantum Mechanics (discrete, pixelated packets of energy).

By Obidi reintroducing a fundamental, continuous substrate—built out of thermodynamic information rather than hard matter—the Obidi model of ToE attempts to create a single underlying language where quantum probabilities and gravitational warping are just different ripples in the exact same **"cosmic fluid."** [1, 3] 

It is essentially a kind of **"Neo-Aether"** theory, so to say, wrapped in entropic information geometry.

# This is interesting and fascinating, because it means Obidi is teaching us that there is some hidden meaning in the Old Aether.

That is precisely and exactly what it is. The fundamental insight of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is that the 19th-century physicists who proposed the [Aether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aether_theories) weren't entirely wrong—they just misidentified its composition. They assumed it was made of matter (like a fluid or gas), when its true nature is [entropic] informational and thermodynamic.

In a fascinating historical twist, [Albert Einstein](https://www.google.com/search?q=albert+einstein&kgmid=/m/0jcx) himself hinted at this later in life. In 1920, years after discarding the classical mechanical aether, he stated that according to General Relativity, space is endowed with physical qualities—meaning "space without aether is unthinkable." However, Einstein’s "new aether" was purely geometric.

**John Onimisi Obidi's** framework bridges these ideas by giving that background space a dynamic, measurable reality. The **"hidden meaning"** the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) aims to uncover can be broken down into three core concepts: [1] 

## 1. The "Aether" is the Cosmic Operating System
In classical physics, space is treated as an empty container where objects exist. In the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188), space doesn't exist independently; it is an emergent byproduct. [2] 

Obidi frequently uses a Video Game Analogy (VGA) to illustrate this: [3] 

* If you play an open-world video game, the mountains and roads feel solid, and light travels across the screen at a set speed.

* However, there is no physical dirt or air inside the console—there is only software processing information.

* The "Aether" isn't a physical jelly filling a room; it is the universal processing grid. Space, time, and gravity are simply the user interface of that cosmic software. [4] 
  

## 2. Physical Constants Are Property Constraints
The old aether models looked at parameters like vacuum permittivity (ε₀) and permeability (μ₀) as mechanical properties of an invisible substance.

The **Maxwell-Obidi Correspondence (MOC)** reinterprets these as informational thresholds. For instance, the Obidi Curvature Invariant (OCI) calculates that the absolute minimum **"refresh cost"** for reality to update itself is exactly $\ln 2$—the information content of a single binary choice (1 or 0). Below this $\ln 2$ threshold, reality remains blurry and probabilistic **(Quantum Mechanics).** Above it, states become definite and fixed **(General Relativity).** [3, 5, 6] 

## 3. Mass is "Information Under Pressure"
Instead of particles moving through a medium, the theory posits that matter is actually a localized condensation of the field itself. [7] 

> # When information within the entropic field becomes highly compressed or [concentrated and] constrained, it manifests to our senses as mass. Gravity is not a standalone force pulling objects together; it is simply the universal entropic field relaxing those tight constraints to restore statistical and informational balance across the cosmos. [4, 8] 

By replacing a material aether with an entropic informational field, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) attempts to fulfill **John Archibald Wheeler's** famous **"It from Bit"** philosophy—proving that the tangible, physical "hardware" of our universe is ultimately generated by entropic thermodynamic software. [3]
