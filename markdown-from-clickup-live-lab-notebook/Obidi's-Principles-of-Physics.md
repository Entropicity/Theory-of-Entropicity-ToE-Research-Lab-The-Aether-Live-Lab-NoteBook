Obidi's principles of physics are centered on **John Onimisi Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE),** a theoretical framework that replaces traditional particle- and spacetime-based physics with a universal, entropy-first foundation.

## Core Principles & Concepts

### • The Obidi Conjecture: 
Reality is not built from empty space, time, or isolated particles; instead, the universe is fundamentally an entropic field (S(x)) from which space, time, matter, and quantum mechanics emerge. 

[1] (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), 

[2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), 

[3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)

### • The Obidi Action: 
A central variational principle (analogous to the **Principle of Least Action in classical mechanics** or the **Einstein-Hilbert action in general relativity)** that dictates how the universal entropic field evolves and flows. It splits into the **Local Obidi Action (LOA)** and **Spectral Obidi Action (SOA).** 

[1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/how-the-self-compensatory-dual-action-principle-of-the-local-obidi-action-loa-and-the-spectral-3f62e9256d1b), 

[2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1), 

[3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), 

[4] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)

### • Master Entropic Equation (MEE) / Obidi Field Equations (OFE): 
The governing field equations of the framework, which play the same role as Einstein's field equations by describing how entropy gradients curve reality and drive interactions. 

[1] (https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188), 

[2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/core-tenets-of-obidis-philosophical-ontology-and-the-foundations-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-5fdce397f250), 

[3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55)

### • Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER): 
Replaces classical least-action paths with a rule stating that physical systems evolve along trajectories that optimize or balance entropic flow. 

[1] (https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-activity-7507415818830450688-ZrX3)

### • Obidi Curvature Invariant (OCI): 
Set mathematically to \(\ln(2)\), representing the fundamental unit of "entropic cost" or the smallest stable distinction between two configurations. 

[1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-obidi-transformation-and-the-obidi-metric-in-modern-theoretical-physics-from-innovations-of-the-b9219bd51149), 

[2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55)

### • The "No-Rush" Theorem (NRT): 
A principle stating that nature cannot be infinitely accelerated; all physical interactions unfold within a finite, non-zero time interval governed by the underlying entropic field. 

[1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55)
