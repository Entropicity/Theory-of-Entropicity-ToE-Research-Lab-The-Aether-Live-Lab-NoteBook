# Mathematical Methods of Shun-ichi Amari's Information Geometry Applied by Obidi in the Formulation of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE): The Obidi Action and the Mathematical Foundations ToE

**John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) explicitly employs Shun-ichi Amari's mathematical methods.** [1, 2] 

Rather than just being a conceptual parallel, ToE directly builds its mathematical engine using Amari's information geometry. While Amari used these geometric tools to analyze how global behaviors emerge from chaotic networks of neurons, Obidi scales this concept up to the entire universe, arguing that physical spacetime, gravity, and quantum mechanics emerge from an underlying informational substrate. [1, 3, 4, 5, 6] 

The specific ways Obidi's ToE uses Amari's methods include:

## 1. The Amari-Chentsov $\alpha$-Connection as a Physical Force

In standard **information geometry**, **Amari’s $\alpha$-connections** are used to measure the asymmetric dualities between different probability distributions. In Obidi's framework, this abstract geometric tool is converted into a physical mechanism. 

Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) utilizes the Amari-Chentsov $\alpha$-connection to define the gauge structure and affine asymmetry of physical reality, mathematically mapping the **arrow of time** and **thermodynamic irreversibility** directly onto the curvature of existence. [3, 7] 

## 2. The Fisher Information Matrix as the Spacetime Metric
Amari heavily expanded on the use of the **Fisher Information Matrix** to define the **Riemannian metric of a data manifold**. 

Obidi's ToE takes this exact mathematical structure and **elevates it into the physical domain.** In ToE, the Fisher-Rao metric (paired with the **quantum Fubini-Study metric**) forms the core of the "Obidi Action Principle," **translating statistical distinguishability into the literal metric tensor of physical spacetime.** [3, 4, 7] 

## 3. Macroscopic Emergence from Microscopic Rules
The exact premise of Amari's Statistical Neurodynamics—proving how macroscopic, stable dynamics emerge from local, random connection rules—is the foundational logic of ToE. 

Obidi’s theory treats our entire universe as an emergent "macro-shadow". Instead of local neurons, ToE looks at local entropic gradients (S(x)). **The Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE)** applies a **variational principle to show how the large-scale geometry of gravity and spacetime emerges from these local informational fluctuations. [2, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12] 

**Ultimately, where Amari used information geometry as an epistemic tool to understand networks and data, Obidi uses it as an ontological field theory to explain the fabric of reality itself.** [2, 4, 7]
