Formulated beginning in early 2025, the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is an ambitious **"entropy-first"** framework that completely upends the traditional metaphysical and ontological foundations of modern physics. Rather than treating entropy as a mere statistical byproduct of moving particles or a measure of macroscopic disorder, Obidi elevates entropy to the primary ontological substrate of reality. ## Core Philosophical Contributions


### 1. "Ontological Courage" and the Triadic ARC

Obidi introduced the concept of Ontological Courage to the philosophy of science. It represents a thinker's willingness to completely abandon entrenched physical "primitives"—such as the axiom that spacetime, gravity, or quantum states are the absolute base layers of reality. Obidi argues that physics has stagnated by retreating into a **"shut up and calculate" ethos.** To break this, he advocates for a **triadic "ARC" (Audacity, Radicality, and Courage)** to challenge foundational metaphysical scaffolding and propose entirely new existential substrates. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_the-world-as-i-see-it-a-brief-philosophical-activity-7502622385297281024-VqFh), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/obidis-inspiring-ontological-courage-philosophy-and-science-in-perspective-from-the-theory-of-fd73d3483ffb)

## 2. Ontodynamics: Existence as Entropic Motion and Negotiation 

Obidi replaces classical kinematics and dynamics with Ontodynamics—the study of existence itself as entropic motion and negotiation. In this philosophical view, the universe is not made of "things" or particles moving through an empty backdrop of space and time. Instead, the universe is a continuous, dynamic entropic field. What we perceive as physical matter, forces, or cosmic geometry are simply secondary projections, gradients, and organized patterns emerging from this deeper entropic manifold. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb), [2](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-world-as-i-see-it-39a0185bff4e), [4](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE)

## 3. The Curvature of Distinguishability

Philosophically reconciling physics with information theory, Obidi posits that reality is fundamentally built on differences. He introduced the Obidi Curvature Invariant $\(\ln 2\)$, which he defines as the "quantum of distinguishability". This invariant represents the minimal, non-zero fold required in the entropic manifold for one state to be meaningfully distinct from another, giving a concrete physical metric to John Archibald Wheeler's classic "It from Bit" philosophy. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_the-structural-vulnerabilities-in-modern-activity-7487364521347584000-XeHQ), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-world-as-i-see-it-39a0185bff4e)

## 4. Radical Inversion of Physical Laws

Through the Obidi Correspondence Principle, he philosophically shifts how scientific laws are validated. Instead of building upward from quantum mechanics to explain thermodynamics, Obidi's framework inverts the hierarchy: all established physical laws (including Einstein's General Relativity and quantum field principles) must be mathematically derived as limiting expressions or secondary boundaries of entropic redistribution. [1](https://johnobidi.substack.com/p/hiroaki-matsueda-and-john-onimisi), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidis-audacious-contributions-to-the-foundations-of-modern-theoretical-physics-df9c286f2aeb)

## Intellectual Legacy

John Onimisi Obidi’s legacy lies in demanding a return to philosophical inquiry within the hard sciences. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_the-world-as-i-see-it-a-brief-philosophical-activity-7502622385297281024-VqFh)

### • Bridging the Objective and Subjective: 
Obidi’s writings on the human condition argue that if entropy is the foundational substrate of reality, human consciousness, memory, and creativity are not cosmic accidents or exceptions to nature, but direct expressions of it. He positions entropy as the ultimate bridge between physical geometry and existential experience. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-world-as-i-see-it-39a0185bff4e), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_the-world-as-i-see-it-a-brief-philosophical-activity-7502622385297281024-VqFh)

### • A Paradigm Shift in Unification: 
In the history of science, humanity has routinely demoted assumed primitives (e.g., heat was once thought to be a physical fluid called "caloric" before being understood as particle motion). Obidi's legacy pushes this historical trajectory to its absolute limit by arguing that spacetime geometry itself is an illusion. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911_the-structural-vulnerabilities-in-modern-activity-7487364521347584000-XeHQ)

### • Framework Interoperability: 
By utilizing tools like the Obidi Master Action and the Vuli-Ndlela Integral, Obidi's work has left a legacy of unification. It attempts to subsume prior fragmented concepts in information geometry, black hole thermodynamics, and entropic gravity into a singular, cohesive field theory. [1](https://ijcsrr.org/comparative-analysis-between-john-onimisi-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-and-waldemar-marek-feldts-feldt-higgs-universal-bridge-f-hub-theory/), [2](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6599558)
