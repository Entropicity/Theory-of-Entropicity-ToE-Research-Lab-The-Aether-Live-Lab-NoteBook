# Mathematical Methods of the Formulation of Spacetime and  Gravity from Shannon Entropy in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

John Onimisi Obidi constructed a physical field from Shannon entropy by treating statistical uncertainty as an ontological substance and applying the tools of information geometry. Instead of viewing Shannon entropy as a passive summary statistic calculated by an observer, Obidi’s [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://theory-of-entropicity-toe.pages.dev/) treats it as a primitive, dynamic field that actively generates physical spacetime. [1, 2, 3] 

The mathematical and conceptual progression from abstract information to a physical field occurs through four major steps:

## 1. The Ontological Shift (Promoting Probability to a Field)
In classical information theory, Claude Shannon defined entropy based on a probability distribution over discrete or continuous data states. Obidi shifts this from an epistemic tool (what we know about a system) to an ontological reality (what the system is made of). [1, 2, 4] 
He maps a continuous probability density $p(x)$ across a differentiable manifold directly to a physical scalar field, $\phi(x)$ (or $S(x)$). He defines this via the field identification:
$$p(x) = e^{-\phi(x)}$$ 

By substituting this into the continuous Shannon entropy formula ($H = -\int p(x) \ln p(x) dx$), the entropy itself stops being a single global number. It becomes a spatial functional where the field value fluctuates from point to point based on the underlying informational density. [1, 5, 6, 7] 

## 2. Constructing the Potential Energy Term
In field theory, an action requires both kinetic (motion) and potential (interaction) terms. To build the potential term, Obidi uses the functional form of the Shannon entropy equation itself. Because $p(x) = e^{-\phi(x)}$, the mathematical structure of Shannon entropy naturally generates the spatial potential energy profile for this new entropic substrate. [5, 8] 

## 3. Extracting Kinetic Energy via the Fisher Metric
To give the field dynamics (kinetic energy), Obidi bridges Shannon entropy with Information Geometry—specifically using the [Fisher Information Metric](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405206960_From_Shannon_Entropy_to_Spacetime_A_Rigorous_Derivation_of_the_Obidi_Action_from_Shannon_Entropy_via_Information_Geometry_in_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_ToE_Living_Review_Letters_Series_ToE_LRLS_-Le). [5] 

* In data science, the Fisher metric measures the "distance" or statistical distinguishability between two probability distributions.
* In ToE, the gradient of the entropic field ($\nabla \phi$) is fed into the Fisher metric, transforming abstract statistical distinguishability into a real, physical Riemannian metric. [2, 4, 5, 9] 

The variation and spatial derivatives of the field act as the kinetic energy term of the system. [5] 

## 4. Formulating the Obidi Action
By marrying the potential term (derived from Shannon entropy) with the kinetic term (derived from Fisher information), Obidi compiles what is known as the proto-Obidi Action. [5] 

When you apply the variational principle (minimizing or stationarizing this action), it yields a nonlinear covariant field equation known as the Master Entropic Equation (MEE). This equation dictates how the entropic field continuously and irreversibly rearranges its internal informational degrees of freedom. [4, 5] 

## The End Result: Curvature Transfer
Through a mechanism Obidi calls the Curvature Transfer Theorem, the informational curvature generated by these Shannon/Fisher field variations maps directly onto physical spacetime geometry. In a coarse-grained macroscopic limit, Einstein's equations of General Relativity emerge not as fundamental laws, but as thermodynamic identities resulting from the gradients of this underlying Shannon-derived field. [5, 10]
