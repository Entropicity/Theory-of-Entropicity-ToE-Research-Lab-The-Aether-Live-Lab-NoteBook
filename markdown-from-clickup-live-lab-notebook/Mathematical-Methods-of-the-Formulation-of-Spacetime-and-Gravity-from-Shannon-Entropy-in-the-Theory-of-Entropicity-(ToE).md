# Mathematical Methods of the Formulation of Spacetime and  Gravity from Shannon Entropy in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

John Onimisi Obidi constructed a physical field from Shannon entropy by treating statistical uncertainty as an ontological substance and applying the tools of information geometry. Instead of viewing Shannon entropy as a passive summary statistic calculated by an observer, Obidi’s [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://theory-of-entropicity-toe.pages.dev/) treats it as a primitive, dynamic field that actively generates physical spacetime. [1, 2, 3] 

The mathematical and conceptual progression from abstract information to a physical field occurs through four major steps:

## 1. The Ontological Shift (Promoting Probability to a Field)
In classical information theory, Claude Shannon defined entropy based on a probability distribution over discrete or continuous data states. Obidi shifts this from an epistemic tool (what we know about a system) to an ontological reality (what the system is made of). [1, 2, 4] 
He maps a continuous probability density $p(x)$ across a differentiable manifold directly to a physical scalar field, $\phi(x)$ (or $S(x)$). He defines this via the field identification:
$$p(x) = e^{-\phi(x)}$$ 

By substituting this into the continuous Shannon entropy formula ($H = -\int p(x) \ln p(x) dx$), the entropy itself stops being a single global number. It becomes a spatial functional where the field value fluctuates from point to point based on the underlying informational density. [1, 5, 6, 7] 

## 2. Constructing the Potential Energy Term
In field theory, an action requires both kinetic (motion) and potential (interaction) terms. To build the potential term, Obidi uses the functional form of the Shannon entropy equation itself. Because $p(x) = e^{-\phi(x)}$, the mathematical structure of Shannon entropy naturally generates the spatial potential energy profile for this new entropic substrate. [5, 8] 

## 3. Extracting Kinetic Energy via the Fisher Metric
To give the field dynamics (kinetic energy), Obidi bridges Shannon entropy with Information Geometry—specifically using the [Fisher Information Metric](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405206960_From_Shannon_Entropy_to_Spacetime_A_Rigorous_Derivation_of_the_Obidi_Action_from_Shannon_Entropy_via_Information_Geometry_in_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_ToE_Living_Review_Letters_Series_ToE_LRLS_-Le). [5] 

* In data science, the Fisher metric measures the "distance" or statistical distinguishability between two probability distributions.
* In ToE, the gradient of the entropic field ($\nabla \phi$) is fed into the Fisher metric, transforming abstract statistical distinguishability into a real, physical Riemannian metric. [2, 4, 5, 9] 

The variation and spatial derivatives of the field act as the kinetic energy term of the system. [5] 

## 4. Formulating the Obidi Action
By marrying the potential term (derived from Shannon entropy) with the kinetic term (derived from Fisher information), Obidi compiles what is known as the proto-Obidi Action. [5] 

When you apply the variational principle (minimizing or stationarizing this action), it yields a nonlinear covariant field equation known as the Master Entropic Equation (MEE). This equation dictates how the entropic field continuously and irreversibly rearranges its internal informational degrees of freedom. [4, 5] 

## The End Result: Curvature Transfer
Through a mechanism Obidi calls the Curvature Transfer Theorem, the informational curvature generated by these Shannon/Fisher field variations maps directly onto physical spacetime geometry. In a coarse-grained macroscopic limit, Einstein's equations of General Relativity (GR) emerge not as fundamental laws, but as thermodynamic identities resulting from the gradients of this underlying Shannon-derived field. [5, 10]

# Scholium 

Dismissing the theory as a "trick" overlooks the deep, rigorous logic that informs Obidi's Leap. To truly judge the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/59188), one must engage with its core premise on its own terms. [1, 2] 

Obidi’s core argument is that modern physics is stuck because it treats space, time, quantum states, and gravity as fundamental, axiomatic "givens". The logic of his leap is built on a specific, structured chain of foundational principles. [3, 4, 5]

## 1. Ontological Primacy: The Ultimate Substrate
The foundation begins with Obidi’s First Conjecture: entropy is not a passive statistical calculation of disorder. It is the primary, active substance of reality—the ontological scalar field $S(x)$. [6, 7] 

* The Logic: If you assume information is the most fundamental thing in the universe (an idea echoed by Wheeler's "It from bit"), then the geometry of that information must be what we perceive as physical reality. Spacetime is not an empty stage; it is the macroscopic "shadow" or projection of this underlying entropic field. [3, 6, 8, 9, 10] 

## 2. The Geometry of Distinguishability
To bridge abstract information and physical force, the theory relies on Information Geometry. [11, 12] 

* The Logic: In physics, a force requires a gradient (a difference between two points). In information theory, the [Fisher Information Metric](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/405206960_From_Shannon_Entropy_to_Spacetime_A_Rigorous_Derivation_of_the_Obidi_Action_from_Shannon_Entropy_via_Information_Geometry_in_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_ToE_Living_Review_Letters_Series_ToE_LRLS_-Le) measures how "distinguishable" two states are. Obidi maps statistical distinguishability directly onto physical distance. If two states are highly indistinguishable, they are geometrically "closer". Gravity, therefore, is reinterpreted as entropic pressure—the natural, statistical tendency for systems to move toward states of higher entropic equilibrium. [8, 12, 13] 

## 3. The "No-Rush" Theorem & The Speed of Light
One of the most elegant conceptual pieces of the foundation is how it handles the speed of light ($c$). In standard physics, $c$ is a brute fact—a constant we measure but cannot explain. [14, 15] 

* The Logic: Through the Entropic Time Limit (ETL), Obidi argues that physical interactions cannot be instantaneous because the entropic field requires a finite time interval to rearrange and synchronize states. Therefore, the speed of light is derived as a property of the field itself: $c = \sqrt{\kappa / \rho_S}$, where $\kappa$ is the entropic stiffness and $\rho_S$ is the entropic inertia of the substrate. Light is just the visible manifestation of the field's maximum reconfiguration speed. [6, 13, 15] 

## 4. The Curvature Transfer Theorem
The final pillar is the mathematical translation. By embedding Shannon entropy into the Obidi Action (the variational principle governing the field), the theory states that informational curvature mathematically translates into physical spacetime curvature. When you look at the universe at a macro scale, Einstein's equations naturally emerge as thermodynamic identities. [8, 9, 12] 

## Engaging with the Paradigm
When viewed through this lens, the theory is an attempt to build a unified framework (Ontodynamics) where quantum mechanics and general relativity are not conflicting laws, but two different scales of the exact same entropic manifold. [3, 15] 

The scientific community's hesitation isn't necessarily a lack of engagement with this logic, but a strict requirement for falsifiability—the theory must make a unique prediction that can be experimentally tested and proven wrong. [16, 17, 18]
