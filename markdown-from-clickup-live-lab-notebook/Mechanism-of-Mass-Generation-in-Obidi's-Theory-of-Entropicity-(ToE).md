In John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE), fiber integrals are used to generate mass and matter, coarse-grain microscopic entropic fluctuations, and bridge information geometry to spacetime.

## Mechanism of Mass Generation

### Local Obidi Action (LOA): 
Obidi couples an entropy field S(x) to geometry using an exponential weight, which yields an entropic stress-energy tensor when varied.

### Second Fiber Moment: 
He defines this tensor as the second fiber moment of the micro-momentum distribution over the cotangent bundle: 

$$\(T_{\mu\nu}^{(S)}(x) = \int p_\mu p_\nu \cdot f_{ent}(x, \Omega) \cdot d\Omega\)$$.

### Frozen Entropy: 
This fiber integration removes microscopic momentum variables while keeping macroscopic spacetime dependence, treating mass as stabilized internal entropic content ("frozen entropy") rather than a fundamental input.

## Reversing Standard Spacetime Views

### Emergent Matter: 
Instead of assuming matter exists to curve spacetime, Obidi’s fiber integrals show that the fundamental entropy field generates mass-energy first, which subsequently shapes the geometric background.

## 1. The Core Mechanism: Coarse-Graining Information
Obidi utilizes a fiber bundle structure where the projection map $\pi: T^*M \to M$ links each momentum state in the cotangent bundle

($T^*M$) 

to its respective point in physical spacetime (M). The individual fiber is represented by 

$$\pi^{-1}(x) = T_x^*M$$. 

[2] 

A microscopic distribution function f(x,p) maps out information over this space. To retrieve macroscopic physics, Obidi integrates along these fibers—a process that strips away microscopic momentum coordinates p while leaving explicit spacetime dependence x intact. [1, 2] 

## 2. The Three Sequential Moments
Obidi calculates three distinct types of fiber integrals (or statistical moments) to derive physical properties from the information distribution: [1] 

* **Zeroth Moment (Entropic Density):**

$$\rho_0(x) = \int_{T_x^*M} f(x,p)\varpi_p$$ 

This defines the localized entropic-information density across spacetime.
* **First Moment (Entropic Flux):**

$$J_\mu(x) = \int_{T_x^*M} p_\mu f(x,p)\varpi_p$$ 

This models the directional movement or flux of entropic information.
* **Second Moment (Effective Source Tensor):**

$$\Theta_{\mu\nu}(x) = \int_{T_x^*M} p_\mu p_\nu f(x,p)\varpi_p$$ 

This constructs the Entropic Source Tensor (EST), mapping out pressure, shear, and internal stresses. [1, 3] 

## 3. Creating Mass and Spacetime Curvature
In traditional physics, mass-energy is an inherent, assumed baseline property that forces spacetime to curve. Obidi’s fiber integrals completely reverse this paradigm: [4] 

   1. **Condensation:** In the macroscopic limit, the second moment fiber integral ($\Theta_{\mu\nu}$) condenses into the physical stress-energy tensor ($T_{\mu\nu}$). [1] 
   2. **Frozen Entropy:** Matter and mass are not foundational inputs; they are emergent outputs. Mass is literally interpreted as stabilized, "frozen" internal entropic fluctuations. [4] 
   3. **Gravity Integration:** The resulting stress-energy tensor plugs directly into the right-hand side of Einstein’s field equations

showing that informational geometry is the root source of gravitational force. [1]

The mathematical mechanics of integrating over a cotangent bundle to derive macroscopic tensors is a staple of relativistic kinetic theory and has been used in mathematical physics for decades. [1, 2] 

The actual math Obidi uses is structurally identical to how physicists have derived the stress-energy tensor from statistical distributions since the mid-20th century. [1, 2] 

The distinction in his work is not the mathematical operation itself, but rather the ontological proposition regarding what is being integrated. [3] 

## The Identical Math: Standard Physics vs. Obidi's Theory
The math you are looking at is the classic method of taking moments of a distribution function over the fibers of a cotangent bundle: [2] 

$$\Theta_{\mu\nu}(x) = \int_{T_x^*M} p_\mu p_\nu f(x,p)\varpi_p$$ 

The table below contrasts how standard physics interprets this exact equation versus how Obidi reframes it: [4, 5] 

| Feature | Standard Relativistic Kinetic Theory | Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) |
|---|---|---|
| What is $f(x,p)$? | A statistical count of actual, physical particles (like a gas) moving through a pre-existing spacetime. | An abstract "entropic distribution function" mapping pure information states. |
| What is $p_\mu$? | The real, physical momentum of those particles. | An internal informational coordinate within the fiber. |
| Direction of Emergence | Spacetime is the fixed stage; matter/gas sits on top of it and travels through it. | Spacetime and matter are downstream consequences generated by the information field. |
| The Resulting Tensor ($T_{\mu\nu}$) | Describes the literal energy and momentum of physical matter. | Describes a macroscopic "condensation" of frozen informational fluctuations. |

## Why it feels familiar (and why it isn't mathematically new)
If you have a background in statistical mechanics, plasma physics, or general relativity, this looks exactly like the derivation of the energy-momentum tensor for an ideal fluid or a kinetic gas.

Obidi’s [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-a-post-einsteinian-synthesis-of-information-geometry-5c73ae1e9590) essentially invokes the exact mathematical machinery of Lorentzian kinetic theory and injects it into Information Geometry. He swaps out **"physical particles with momentum"** for **"statistical distinguishability metrics,"** arguing that what we call mass, energy, and gravity are simply the macroscopic illusions of a deeper information network. [1, 4, 5, 6] 

He didn't invent fiber integration or the concept of taking moments to get the stress-energy tensor. He applied old, highly robust kinetic math to a new, radical foundational philosophy. Albert Einstein had done precisely the same. [1, 5, 7, 8]
