# What is the Obidi-Haller Action (OHA) of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)?


What-is-the-Obidi-Haller-Action-(OHA)-of-the-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md

The Obidi-Haller Action (OHA) is a specialized action functional within the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://theory-of-entropicity-toe.pages.dev/), a radical theoretical physics framework formulated by John Onimisi Obidi. It defines the entropic dynamics of a system by establishing a formal identity between a particle's classical physical action and its thermodynamic entropy. The OHA mathematically demonstrates that the Principle of Least Action in classical mechanics is a direct consequence of the Second Law of Thermodynamics. This implies that physical systems follow specific trajectories because they are maximizing or extremizing their entropy. [1, 2, 3, 4] 
## ⚖️ The Haller-Obidi Correspondence
The foundation of the OHA relies on the Haller-Obidi Correspondence (HOC). This concept bridges two distinct eras of entropy-action research: [3, 5] 

* John L. Haller Jr. (2015): He proposed an entropy-action identity showing that a particle's self-information (entropy) is directly proportional to its classical action over its worldline.
* John Onimisi Obidi (2025/2026): He generalized Haller's single-particle mechanics into a universal field theory. [3, 6] 

The correspondence reveals that the classical path of a particle is simply the single-particle projection of a much broader, continuous entropic field. [6, 7] 
## 🧪 Mathematical Formulation
In field theory, this relationship is expressed by defining the entropic Lagrangian ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{ent}}$): [3] 
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{ent}} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}(u^\mu \partial_\mu S)$$ 
Integrating this entropic Lagrangian over time yields the Obidi-Haller Action (OHA). [3, 8] 

* The Mechanism: The entropy production rate acts as an informational "cost".
* The Dynamic: Rapid entropy production reduces the variational weight of a trajectory.
* The Limit: A photon produces zero entropy, maximizing its effective Lagrangian. [8] 

## 🌌 Role in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)
Under the broader [Theory of Entropicity](https://osf.io/d7pky/overview), space, time, and gravity are not fundamental primitives. Instead, they emerge from a primary, universal entropic field S(x). The OHA serves as a vital mathematical link in this framework: [2, 9, 10] 

* Monistic Substrate: It treats entropy as the fundamental substance ("matter") and the source of geometric curvature.
* Emergent Gravitation: Varying the overarching Obidi Action yields the Master Entropic Equation (MEE) and entropic Einstein equations, reducing directly to general relativity in classical limits.
* The Arrow of Time: It embeds thermodynamic irreversibility directly into the core variational principles of physical motion. [3, 11]
