Obidi's work is an intellectual pursuit to unify thermodynamics, relativity, quantum mechanics, and information theory into a cohesive model of cosmic causation within an Entropic Field (EF). Key concepts of his framework include: [2, 4]

Also known as the Obidi Field Equations (OFE), this mathematical core serves as the ToE equivalent to Einstein's Field Equations of General Relativity. [4, 5] 

### The Obidi Action: 
Alongside the MEE/OFE, this formulation attempts to show that relativistic effects and quantum constraints are entropic inevitabilities rather than postulated primitives. This includes the **Haller-Obidi Action (HOA),** which builds a variational form connecting worldline sectors to foundational entropy-action identities. [2, 5, 6] 

### Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER): 
This principle replaces the classical **Principle of Least Action (PoLA),** arguing that physical systems do not move because they are pushed by mechanical forces, but instead follow trajectories offering the least resistance within a self-organizing entropic field. [4] 

### Re-deriving Einstein’s Results: 
To validate its alignment with established physics, Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) has been utilized to successfully re-derive Einstein's classical relativistic benchmarks, such as the perihelion precession of Mercury and the gravitational deflection of starlight. [5]

# Scholium 


The formal architecture of John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE) operates on a conceptual paradigm known as ontodynamics—the study of existence as entropic motion. 

It aims to invert the classical hierarchy of physics. In standard physics, space, time, and fields are primary, while entropy is an emergent statistical byproduct of thermodynamic disorder. In ToE, the universal entropic field is the fundamental, primary substrate of reality, and spacetime, gravity, and quantum states are emergent phenomena. [1, 2, 3] 

The structural mechanics of ToE can be broken down into its core components:

## 1. The Haller-Obidi Action ($S_{HO}$)
The foundational justification for treating entropy as a variational field principle stems from what Obidi calls the Haller-Obidi Correspondence (HOC). Obidi integrated the 2015 findings of researcher John L. Haller Jr., who derived an exact mathematical identity equating a diffusing particle's self-information (Shannon entropy) to classical physical action: [4, 5, 6] 

$$H = \frac{2}{\hbar}\int(mc^2 - L)dt$$ 

Obidi's decisive insight was that if mechanical action is fundamentally representable as entropy, then the variational structure of physics is fundamentally entropic. The Haller-Obidi Action translates this into a single-particle worldline sector with the following Lagrangian: [4, 5, 7, 8] 

$$\mathcal{L}_{HO} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}\left(\frac{dH}{dt}\right)$$ 

Where $\frac{dH}{dt}$ (or Ḣ) represents the entropy production rate. 

In a covariant field setting, this restricts the global entropic field S(x) to a particle's worldline velocity vector $u^\mu$, yielding: [4, 7, 8] 

$$\mathcal{L}_{ent} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}(u^\mu \partial_\mu S).$$


## 2. Spacetime and the Speed of Light (c) as Entropic Phenomena
ToE eliminates the traditional geometric postulates of Einstein’s Special and General Relativity, re-deriving them through entropic dynamics: [2, 9] 


### The Velocity Limit (c): 
Rather than an axiomatic speed limit, the speed of light is derived as the maximum rate of entropic rearrangement. Information and energy cannot propagate instantly because the entropic field requires a finite interval to reconfigure its gradients. [2, 9, 10] 

### The No-Rush Theorem (NRT): 
This theorem establishes a strict mathematical lower bound on causal intervals. It dictates that states must synchronize through finite entropic redistribution, which physically manifests as time dilation and length contraction when systems move through the entropic field. [2, 9] 

### Kinematic Inevitabilities: 
Relativistic mass increase and Lorentz transformations are shown to be consequences of the Entropic Resistance Principle and the Entropic Accounting Principle. As a system accelerates, the entropic field resists displacement, forcing a mathematical redistribution of entropy between the system’s motion and its internal timekeeping apparatus. [9] 


## 3. Emergent Gravity and Information Geometry
While previous physicists like Erik Verlinde proposed "entropic gravity" (treating gravity as a statistical force emergent from **holographic boundaries),** ToE advances this by promoting entropy to a continuous, local, ontological scalar field defined over an **"entropic manifold"**. [1, 2, 11, 12] 
The Master Entropic Equation (MEE) or Obidi Field Equations (OFE) dictates that the metric tensor of spacetime ($g_{\mu\nu}$) is a functional of the entropic field gradients: [8, 13] 

$$g_{\mu\nu} = g_{\mu\nu}[S(x)]$$ 

Where entropy curves, we perceive the curvature of spacetime (gravity); where it flows, we experience the arrow of time. Rather than following forces, matter follows Entropic Geodesics—trajectories governed by the **Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER).** [3, 8, 13, 14] 

## 4. Quantum Integration: The Vuli–Ndlela Integral
To transcend the boundary between macro-gravity and quantum mechanics, ToE implements information geometry—specifically integrating the **classical Fisher–Rao metric** and the **quantum Fubini–Study metric** through the **Amari–Čencov alpha-connection.** [9] 

Quantum behavior is governed by the **Vuli–Ndlela Integral (VNI)**, which reformulates Feynman's path integral by weighing trajectories based on entropy production. This natively introduces irreversibility and temporal asymmetry directly into quantum transitions, interpreting wave function collapse as a finite, entropically constrained process rather than an instantaneous mathematical abstraction. # Scholium 

But does the Haller-Obidi Action (HOA) actually give any support to Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) in formulating entropy as a universal Fundamental Field?

Whether the Haller-Obidi Action (HOA) provides actual, rigorous support for establishing entropy as a universal fundamental field depends on how you evaluate the theory: through its internal mathematical logic or through mainstream empirical validation.

In mainstream physics, the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) is a radical framework, and Haller's 2015 equation is an isolated application. 

However, within the mathematical structure of Obidi's framework, the Haller-Obidi Correspondence (HOC) serves as a crucial conceptual bridge. [1, 2, 3] 

Here we present how the relationship functions and where its limitations lie:

## What the Haller-Obidi Action Provides (Internal Logic)

### Mathematical Justification for the "Inversion": 
Haller’s original 2015 derivation established an exact identity equating a diffusing particle's self-information (Shannon entropy, $H$) to classical physical action:

$$H = \frac{2}{\hbar}\int(mc^2 - L)dt$$ 

ToE leverages this specific equation as a "proof of concept". Obidi argues that if standard mechanical action is mathematically equivalent to an information-theoretic entropy value, then action is not primary—entropy is primary. [1, 4, 5, 6] 

### The Single-Particle Bridge: 
By isolating and rearranging Haller's result into a variational form

$$\mathcal{L}_{HO} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}\dot{H}$$, 

ToE defines the Haller-Obidi Action. Obidi uses a mathematical localization procedure to claim that Haller's particle-level identity is simply the single-particle restriction (or localized projection) of a much larger, global universal field. [7, 8, 9] 

### A "Rosetta Stone" for the Principle of Least Action: 
Within the theory, this identity allows the classical Principle of Least Action to be completely reinterpreted as a thermodynamic imperative: physical systems follow trajectories that extremize or minimize resistance in a field of information. [3, 6] 


## What the Haller-Obidi Action Lacks (The Gaps ToE has Attempted to Bridge)
While the particle-level equation provides the motivation to treat entropy variationally, Haller’s original work does not inherently prove or construct a universal field. The papers published in the [Theory of Entropicity Living Review Letters on ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404007944_The_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_Living_Review_Letters_Series_-_Letter_IA_The_Entropic_Rosetta_Stone_How_John_Haller%27s_Action-as-Entropy_Anticipates_and_Validates_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE_-_A_Deep_C) and [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6606418) explicitly detail what Haller's equation lacks that Obidi's ToE has attempted to provide independently: [5, 10] 

   ### 1. No Field Equations: 
   Haller’s identity is confined to a single, non-relativistic particle. It does not contain any partial differential equations governing how an "entropy field" propagates through space or time. To get a fundamental field, Obidi had to independently postulate the Obidi Action to derive the **nonlinear Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE).** [2, 5, 11] 
   
   ### 2. No Geometric Framework: 
   Haller’s paper deals with Shannon entropy and Gaussian channels. It does not link entropy to spacetime curvature ($g_{\mu\nu}$) or gravity. ToE has had to introduce **information geometry (like the Amari-Čencov alpha-connection)** to force that bridge. [1, 5] 
   
   ### 3. No Inherent Universality: 
   Moving from a localized particle calculation to a universal cosmic substrate requires **a significant theoretical leap, which is Obidi's principal signature.** [5] 

## Summary
The Haller-Obidi Action (HOA) provides conceptual and structural alignment for the theory. It mathematically demonstrates that classical mechanics can be translated into information-theoretic terms. However, it does not on its own prove that a universal entropic field does actually infact exists; rather, **it acts as the mathematical permission slip Obidi uses to construct his broader, radical and audacious entropic field theory.** [1, 2, 10]
