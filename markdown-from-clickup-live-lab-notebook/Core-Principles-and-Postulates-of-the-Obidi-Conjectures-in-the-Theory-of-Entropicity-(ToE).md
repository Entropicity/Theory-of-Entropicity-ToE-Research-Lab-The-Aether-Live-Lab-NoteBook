## 🌌 A Radical Reversal of Physics
The Obidi Conjectures propose a bold inversion of traditional physics: Entropy is not a statistical after‑effect — it is the fundamental, dynamical field from which space, time, matter, geometry, and physical law emerge.

Obidi frames this revolution through three core Conjectures supported by a rigorous Correspondence Principle to ensure scientific continuity.

## 🧩 1️⃣ The First Conjecture — Ontology
Entropy is the primary substrate of existence. Matter and energy are emergent expressions of deeper entropic structure. This is the foundational Ontological Conjecture:

Entropy First. Everything Else Follows.

## ⚙️ 2️⃣ The Second Conjecture — Dynamics
All physical laws and interactions arise from the Entropic Field (EF). Gravity, electromagnetism, quantum behavior — all become emergent responses of entropic dynamics.

## 🔸 Why the Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP) Is Necessary
The OCP ensures that ToE reproduces established physics in the correct limits. It enforces:

### • Empirical continuity 
— ToE must preserve the successes of GR, QM, EM. 

### • Theoretical discipline 
— constrains entropic formulations to match known laws. 

### • Scientific legitimacy 
— mirrors the classical correspondence principles used by Bohr, Einstein, Dirac.

Because the Second Conjecture is sweeping in ambition, the OCP prevents contradictions with well‑tested physics.

## 🧭 3️⃣ The Third Conjecture — Geometry
Spacetime is not fundamental. It emerges from a deeper entropic‑informational manifold.

This geometric conjecture is essential: The Second Conjecture + OCP alone cannot guarantee spacetime’s existence. Traditional physics assumes spacetime without explaining it. Obidi provides the missing mechanism — a geometric emergence from entropy itself.

## 🧠 A Unified, Post‑Einsteinian Framework
Together, the Obidi Conjectures challenge the fundamentality of Einstein’s spacetime and the quantum measurement problem, offering a radical but unified foundation for modern physics.

They map a complete logical structure:

### 🔹 Ontology

### 🔹 Dynamics
