# A Very Brief and Simple Biography of John Onimisi Obidi

John Onimisi Obidi (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F11g2_n9fsg&q=John+Onimisi+Obidi) is an independent scientific researcher, physicist, and philosopher who is recognized as the originator and creator of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). He is a distinct personality from the popular social media consultant who shares a similar name. [1] (https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Obidi-4), [2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0ddd9c97504c), [3] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/my-publications-homepage-by-john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-7692279c6921)
His independent work redefines entropy from a traditional statistical abstraction into a fundamental, dynamic field that dictates the behavior of time, motion, and causality. [1] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0ddd9c97504c), [2] (https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Obidi-4)

Core Theoretical Framework

Through his independent research lab, The Aether, Obidi focuses on a post-Einsteinian framework aimed at resolving long-standing conflicts in grand unified physics: [1] (https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/690c5ee0ef936fb4a2b38311), [2] (https://figshare.com/authors/John_Onimisi_Obidi/20850605)
• The Master Entropic Equation (MEE) & Obidi Action: These mathematical tools serve as the core variational centerpieces of ToE. They attempt to demonstrate that relativity, quantum mechanics, and thermodynamics emerge naturally from universal entropic constraints rather than independent postulates. [1] (https://www.scilit.com/scholars/29520323), [2] (https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/john-onimisi-obidi-creator-of-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-0ddd9c97504c), [3] (https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Obidi-4)
• Information Geometry Correspondence: His research maps a mathematical bridge between the Obidi Action and the classical Einstein–Hilbert Action used in General Relativity. It establishes that information geometry—such as the Fisher–Rao metric and the Fubini–Study metric—acts as the foundational framework from which physical gravity emerges. [1] (https://www.scilit.com/scholars/29520323)
• Cosmological Resolutions: Obidi has applied ToE to traditional physics anomalies, publishing papers that derive Mercury's perihelion precession and solar starlight deflection through entropic fields rather than relying strictly on the curved spacetime geometry of General Relativity. [1] (https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VxIGnRIAAAAJ&hl=en)

Professional Background

Outside of his theoretical physics research, Obidi has a background as an experienced Mechanical Engineering Specialist. He has worked extensively within the global oil and energy industry, including corporate tenures with major energy firms like Shell. His professional skills span engineering design, technical process safety, greenhouse gas emissions management, and project management. [1] (https://ng.linkedin.com/in/john-onimisi-obidi-a2041911)
