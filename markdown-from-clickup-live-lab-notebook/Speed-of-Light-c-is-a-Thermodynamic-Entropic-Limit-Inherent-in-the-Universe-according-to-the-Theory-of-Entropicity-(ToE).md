# Who has postulated that the speed of light c is a Thermodynamic entropic limit inherent in the universe?

The concept of the speed of light (c) as an inherent thermodynamic or entropic limit is primarily postulated by John Onimisi Obidi through his Theory of Entropicity (ToE). 

**Obidi declares that the so-called speed of light c is not a geometric property of spacetime as postulated by Einstein in his Theory of Relativity (ToR), but a fundamental speed limit enforced by the Entropic Field of Nature itself, and imposed on all emergent phenomena everywhere in the Universe.**

In this framework:

**The Limit:** The speed of light is not simply a geometric property of spacetime. Instead, it is theorized to be the universal rate limit at which the entropic field can rearrange and redistribute information across the universe.

**The Mechanism:** According to ToE, order and disorder cannot process instantaneously. A finite speed is required to preserve causality, coherence, and conservation laws; if interactions could happen infinitely fast, reality would collapse.

**The Saturated Field:** Light itself is simply what saturates this fundamental entropic speed limit.


------------------------------

**The concept that the speed of light ($c$) is a thermodynamic entropic limit inherent in the universe is a core postulate of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), developed by physics researcher and philosopher John Onimisi Obidi.** [1, 2] 

In his publications, including papers hosted on the [Cambridge Open Engage platform](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/6908aca0113cc7cfffd949e3), Obidi argues that: [3] 

* **Entropy is the fundamental field:** Spacetime geometry is not a primitive concept; instead, an underlying entropic field dictates the laws of the universe. [1, 4, 5, 6] 
* **An Entropic Speed Limit (ESL):** The speed of light is rebranded as the maximum rate at which the universe can process, rearrange, and redistribute information and entropy. [2, 4] 
* **Light as a bystander:** The limit $c$ is not fundamentally about electromagnetism or light itself; light is simply a massless phenomenon that saturates this pre-existing thermodynamic boundary. [2, 7, 8, 9] 

(Note: While theoretical physicist **Erik Verlinde** famously introduced "Entropic Gravity" in 2010/2011, his model still assumed the speed of light c to be a standard, fixed geometric constant of spacetime rather than deriving it as a thermodynamic consequence.) [10, 11]
