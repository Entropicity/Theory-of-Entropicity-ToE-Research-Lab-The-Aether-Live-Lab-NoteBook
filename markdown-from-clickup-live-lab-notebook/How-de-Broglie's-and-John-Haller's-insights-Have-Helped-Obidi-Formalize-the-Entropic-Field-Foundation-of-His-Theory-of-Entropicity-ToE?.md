## How Obidi Transformed Their Insights into a Universal Field
Obidi did not have to guess at the foundational mechanics because de Broglie and Haller had already mapped its localized footprint. His role was to recognize that their isolated calculations were actually a **"worldline sector"** of a continuous, cosmic field. [1, 3] 


### The Localized Proof (The Haller–Obidi Lagrangian): 
To integrate Haller’s single-particle work into a field theory, Obidi rearranged Haller's entropy–action identity into an explicit, variational particle form called the Haller–Obidi Lagrangian:

$$\mathcal{L}_{HO} = mc^2 - \frac{\hbar}{2}\left(\frac{dH}{dt}\right)$$ 

This captured how an isolated particle behaves under informational constraints. [3, 4] 

### The Covariant Field Scale-Up: 
Obidi then generalized this 1D worldline trajectory into a full, multi-dimensional tensor field equation. He realized that if you restrict the universal entropic field $S(x)$ down to the path of a single particle, it reduces directly into the **Haller–Obidi** structure. [3]
