Both are theoretical researchers focused on rebuilding physics using information geometry and entropy, but they approach the concept from different structural levels. [1, 2] 

## Theoretical Frameworks

* **David Sigtermans** created the [Total Entropic Quantity (TEQ) framework](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202412.1265/v1/download). His work focuses on entropy geometry, showing how physical structures, quantum wave-particle duality, and horizon thermodynamics emerge from curved entropy boundaries. [2, 3, 4] 
* **John Onimisi Obidi** created the [Theory of Entropicity (ToE)](https://independent.academia.edu/JOHNOBIDI). His framework goes a step further by treating entropy not just as a geometric property, but as the fundamental, continuous physical field (S(x)) from which spacetime, matter, gravity, and the universe itself emerge. [5, 6, 7] 

## Mathematical Scope

* **TEQ (Sigtermans)** relies on finite entropy curvature, resolution boundaries, and entropy-weighted path integrals to explain quantum mechanics and vacuum energy. [2, 3, 8] 
* **ToE (Obidi)** acts as a mathematical [superset of TEQ](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/analysis-of-john-onimisi-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-and-david-sigtermans-total-entropic-70784046bbbd). Obidi’s Obidi Action Principle (OAP) and entropic manifold absorb Sigtermans' concepts—treating TEQ's resolution boundaries and curvature mechanics as specific, localized boundary cases or subsets within a larger variational field theory. [1, 2, 9] 

## Philosophical and Applied Focus

* **Sigtermans** applies his model heavily to information theory and quantum optics (such as explaining the Lorentzian kernel as an epistemic envelope of uncertainty) while working professionally in data-driven diagnostics. [3, 10, 11] 
* **Obidi** extends his framework into a full philosophical domain called Ontodynamics (the study of existence as entropic motion). He utilizes ToE to completely replace standard relativity postulates, deriving the speed of light (c) as a dynamic "No-Rush" limit of how fast his entropic field can rearrange itself. [6, 12, 13, 14]
