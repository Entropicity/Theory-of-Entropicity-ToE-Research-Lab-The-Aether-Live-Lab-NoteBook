# Derivation of Einstein's Perihelion Precession of Mercury Using the Entropic Field Principles of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

The perihelion precession of Mercury using entropic field principles was derived by John Onimisi Obidi in his framework known as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE).

## Core Concepts of the Entropic Derivation

**Alternative to Spacetime:** Instead of using Albert Einstein's curved spacetime from General Relativity, Obidi utilized an entropy-driven modification of Newtonian gravity.

**Key Inputs:** The derivation incorporates higher-order entropy corrections along with principles like the Unruh Effect, Hawking Temperature, Bekenstein-Hawking Entropy, and the Holographic Principle.

**Result:** It successfully yields the exact same observational shift of 43 arcseconds per century originally calculated by Einstein.
