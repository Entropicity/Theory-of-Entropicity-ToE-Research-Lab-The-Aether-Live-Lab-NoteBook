# A Brief Introduction to Maxwell and Obidi on the Origin of the Speed of Light c: From Electromagnetism to the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

A-Brief-Introduction-to-Maxwell-and-Obidi-on-the-Origin-of-the-Speed-of-Light-c-From-Electromagnetism-to-the-Theory-of-Entropicity-(ToE).md

> # **Obidi’s declaration that the speed of light c is not fundamentally electromagnetic but entropic is the audacious heart of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE). Supported by the No-Rush Theorem (NRT) and the Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP), this identification elevates c from a property of light to the universal limit of entropic rearrangement itself.**
