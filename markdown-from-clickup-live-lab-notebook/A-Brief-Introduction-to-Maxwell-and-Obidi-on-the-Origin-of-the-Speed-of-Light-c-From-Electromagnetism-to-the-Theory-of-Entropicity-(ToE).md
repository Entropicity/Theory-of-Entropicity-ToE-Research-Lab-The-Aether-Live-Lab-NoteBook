When Maxwell unified the electromagnetic laws, he obtained the wave equation

$$

By comparison, Maxwell identified

The numerical value of this expression matched the known speed of light.  
Thus Maxwell concluded that light is an electromagnetic wave, and that its speed is determined by the electromagnetic properties of the vacuum.

This is crucial:  

Maxwell’s $c$ is forced by physical constants, not chosen.
