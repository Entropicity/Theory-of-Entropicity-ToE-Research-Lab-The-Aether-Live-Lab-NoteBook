# Conjectures of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE): Obidi's First, Second, and Third Conjectures 

## Overview of Obidi’s Conjectures

Obidi’s Conjectures form the axiomatic core of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), a bold framework proposing that **entropy is the fundamental ontological field** from which all physical reality emerges. Unlike traditional views that treat entropy as a derived or statistical quantity, ToE elevates it to the status of the primary substrate of existence—more fundamental than matter, energy, forces, or even spacetime itself. The theory is structured around three interlocking conjectures that define the ontology, dynamics, and geometry of the universe.

## Obidi’s First Conjecture: The Fundamental Entropy Field

The **First Conjecture** asserts that **entropy is the fundamental universal field of nature**. This is not a reinterpretation but a radical ontological claim: entropy is the primary entity from which all physical structures and phenomena arise. In this view, everything else—matter, energy, forces, and spacetime—is secondary and emergent. The entropic field S(x) permeates all of reality, and its dynamics underlie all observable processes. This conjecture repositions entropy to the conceptual role once held by matter in classical physics, quantum fields in field theory, and spacetime geometry in general relativity.

## Obidi’s Second Conjecture: Universal Derivability of Physical Laws

Building on the ontological foundation, the **Second Conjecture** states that **all physical laws and interactions are derivable from the Entropic Field**. This transforms ToE from a philosophical stance into a scientific program: if entropy is fundamental, then gravity, quantum mechanics, gauge interactions, thermodynamics, and cosmology must all be recoverable from entropic dynamics. This derivability is constrained by the **Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP)**, which requires that any entropic formulation must reproduce established physical theories in their appropriate limits. For example, Einstein’s field equations and the anomalous precession of Mercury’s orbit have been shown to emerge from entropy-based corrections without invoking spacetime curvature.

## Obidi’s Third Conjecture: Emergent Spacetime from Entropic Information

The **Third Conjecture** addresses the nature of spacetime, proposing that **physical spacetime emerges from a deeper entropic-informational manifold**. Geometry, curvature, and the spacetime metric are not primitive but macroscopic manifestations of underlying entropic-informational relations. Specifically, the Fisher–Rao and Fubini–Study geometries “live beneath” each point of everyday spacetime, and what we perceive as spacetime is the emergent geometry extruded from these informational structures. When the informational object varies from point to point, its gradients and curvature become the metric, connection, and Einstein tensor of physical spacetime. Thus, spacetime is not a neutral stage but a macroscopic shadow of entropic information organization.

## Key Implications and Novel Concepts

Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) introduces several novel concepts that reshape our understanding of physics and reality. 

The **No-Rush Theorem (NRT)** states that no physical process can occur in zero time, enforcing a minimum duration for causal influence and providing a mechanism for the speed-of-light limit. 

The **Entropic Time Limit (ETL)** posits a fundamental time quantum, challenging absolute simultaneity. 

Additionally, **Entropic Observability** and **Entropic Existentiality** suggest that observation and existence depend on entropy thresholds, offering an objective mechanism for wavefunction collapse without requiring conscious observers. 

The **Obidi Curvature Invariant (OCI)** defines the minimal entropic separation required for physical distinguishability, linking information geometry to measurable physical constants.
