# Motivation Behind Obidi's Radical Proposition of Entropy as a Fundamental Universal Field of Nature 

Motivation-Behind-Obidi's-Radical-Proposition-of-Entropy-as-a-Fundamental-Universal-Field-of-Nature.md 

The motivation behind John Onimisi Obidi’s radical proposition—known as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE)—stems from a deep philosophical dissatisfaction with the current fragmentation of modern physics and a desire to find a single, unified "operating system" for reality. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/foundations-of-obidis-theory-of-entropicity-toe-conceptual-mathematical-and-physical-pillars-929690e65c55), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-activity-7507415818830450688-ZrX3)

His work is driven by several core conceptual and philosophical motivators:

## 1. Refusal to Accept Physics' Core Duality

Modern physics is deeply fractured: [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-activity-7507415818830450688-ZrX3)

• General Relativity (GR) treats the macroscopic universe as a smooth, deterministic geometric stage.

• Quantum Mechanics (QM) treats the microscopic universe through discrete, non-local probabilities. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-activity-7507415818830450688-ZrX3)

Obidi’s primary motivation is to resolve this contradiction by eliminating both spacetime geometry and quantum probability as fundamental axioms. Instead of trying to force quantum mechanics into a pre-existing geometric stage (the traditional goal of quantum gravity), ToE seeks a deeper, singular substrate from which both smooth geometry and quantum behavior emerge naturally as secondary side-effects. [1](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/theory-of-entropicity-toe_obidis-frantic-efforts-to-understand-activity-7507415818830450688-ZrX3)

## 2. Inverting the Classical Hierarchy (Ontological Primacy)

In standard physics, entropy is merely an epistemic or statistical shorthand—a calculation human observers make about a system's microstates (like molecules in a gas). [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-overview-of-the-logical-motivation-and-mathematical-construction-of-obidis-theory-of-3ff24a844944), [2](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/798989506715208b13ba4c33bc1ef74e)

Obidi’s radical proposition is motivated by **"ontological courage"**: flipping the script to state that entropy is a real, active physical field (S(x)) perverting the universe. Instead of geometry constraining how entropy behaves, entropy dynamically generates geometry. He was motivated by a **"nonvolitional convergence of reasoning"** in mathematical tools—realizing that every modern theory attempting to derive spacetime from something deeper ultimately relies on the mathematical framework of information theory and entropy. [1](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/58738), [2](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/antonio-anna-mele_very-happy-to-see-our-paper-just-published-activity-7445479245599891456-ZmJc), [3](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [4](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6599558.pdf?abstractid=6599558&mirid=1), [5](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-overview-of-the-logical-motivation-and-mathematical-construction-of-obidis-theory-of-3ff24a844944)

## 3. Replacing the "Observer" with "Distinguishability"

In standard physics (especially quantum mechanics and relativity), the "observer" occupies a highly privileged, problematic role. Obidi wanted to dethrone the observer and anchor reality in an objective, physical framework. [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-dethrones-the-observer-and-the-observers-privileged-role-in-e8a4575c842e)

In his Foundational Philosophy of ToE, he argues that space and time only exist because of statistical distinguishability. If two points or states cannot be distinguished informationally, they do not have separate coordinates. Therefore, the metric of spacetime is built purely out of the gradients of an entropic field. [1](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404911185_The_Foundational_Philosophy_Behind_John_Onimisi_Obidi's_Formulation_of_the_Theory_of_Entropicity_ToE), [2](https://gist.github.com/Entropicity/798989506715208b13ba4c33bc1ef74e), [3](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-overview-of-the-logical-motivation-and-mathematical-construction-of-obidis-theory-of-3ff24a844944), [4](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/6599558.pdf?abstractid=6599558&mirid=1)

## 4. Deriving Physical Constraints from Information Limits

Rather than stating that the speed of light (c) or time dilation are arbitrary rules baked into the universe, Obidi was motivated to explain why these constraints exist. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/antonio-anna-mele_very-happy-to-see-our-paper-just-published-activity-7445479245599891456-ZmJc), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-dethrones-the-observer-and-the-observers-privileged-role-in-e8a4575c842e)

• The "No-Rush" Principle: ToE reinterprets the speed of light as the maximum entropic reconfiguration rate of the universe.

• Relativistic Effects: Physical phenomena like time dilation and length contraction are motivated not as artifacts of moving frames of reference, but as physical capacity constraints. When an object moves, the entropic field must allocate "bandwidth" to maintain that motion, leaving less internal entropic capacity for atomic processes (causing time to slow down). [1](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/an-introduction-to-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-its-conceptual-foundations-and-far-reaching-3f6a9a8be616), [2](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-dethrones-the-observer-and-the-observers-privileged-role-in-e8a4575c842e)

Ultimately, Obidi's goal is to strip physics down to one primitive element—arguing that the universe does not simply contain information and entropy; the universe is a dynamic entropic field.
