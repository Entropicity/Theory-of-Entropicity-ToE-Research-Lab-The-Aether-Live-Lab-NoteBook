**John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE)** proposes a radical ontological inversion where **entropy is the fundamental field** of reality, rather than a secondary statistical byproduct of matter. This framework, grounded in three core conjectures, aims to reconstruct physics by deriving all physical laws and spacetime geometry from an underlying **Entropic Field**.

The **First Conjecture** asserts that entropy is the primary substrate of existence, with matter and energy being emergent properties. The **Second Conjecture** posits that all physical interactions and laws are derivable from this field, constrained by the **Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP)** to ensure consistency with established theories. The **Third Conjecture** claims that **physical spacetime emerges** from a deeper entropic-informational manifold, treating geometry as a macroscopic projection of underlying entropic relations rather than a primitive arena.

Philosophically, ToE redefines **probability as an ontological conservation law** via the **Obidi Probability Law**, which partitions reality into a coherent visible sector and an entropic invisible sector. This shifts probability from an epistemic tool of ignorance to a measurable physical quantity governing the boundary between appearance and reality. By unifying ontology, dynamics, and geometry under a single entropic chain, Obidi’s work challenges the fundamentality of spacetime and quantum measurement, offering a unified but highly speculative foundation for post-Einsteinian physics.

## The Three Conjectures in Depth

### **First Conjecture (Ontology): "Entropy is the fundamental field of reality."**

This is not the standard thermodynamic entropy — a bookkeeping of microstate counts — but a **real, dynamical field** *S(x)* defined on a differentiable manifold, with gradients and curvature. It occupies the same conceptual slot that matter once held in classical mechanics, quantum fields in QFT, or spacetime geometry in GR. Everything else — matter, energy, forces, geometry — is **secondary and emergent**. The conjecture inverts the conventional hierarchy: rather than entropy arising *from* physics, physics arises *from* entropy.

### **Second Conjecture (Dynamics): "All physical laws and interactions are derivable from the Entropic Field."**

This extends the ontological claim into a **scientific program**. Gravity, quantum phenomena, gauge interactions, thermodynamics, and cosmology must all be recoverable from entropic dynamics. The governing variational principle is the **Obidi Action**:

$$A_{\text{Obidi}}[S] = \int d^4x\,\sqrt{-g}\,\left[\tfrac{1}{2}(\nabla S)^2 - V(S) + J(x)\,S\right]$$

or in typical Obidi's general form: λ / Λ: 

$$A_{\text{Obidi}}[S] = \int d^4Λ\,\sqrt{-g}\,\left[\tfrac{1}{2}(\nabla S)^2 - V(S) + J(Λ)\,S\right]$$


This single action encodes both reversible and irreversible processes. Varying it with respect to the metric and the entropy field yields the **Entropic Field Equations**, **Entropic Geodesic Equations**, and conservation relations — from which spacetime curvature, gravitational attraction, quantum transitions, and relativistic kinematics all arise as different manifestations of entropy-driven optimization. A spectral form:

$S_{\text{Obidi}} = -\text{Tr}\ln(\Delta)$, 

embeds "two metrics" (matter-induced and background entropy geometry) internally rather than postulating them independently.

**Third Conjecture (Geometry): "Physical spacetime emerges from a deeper entropic-informational manifold."**

This is the most radical of the three. It asserts that spacetime is **not a primitive arena** but an emergent projection. The mathematical route runs through **information geometry**: the Fisher–Rao / Fubini–Study metric on the entropic manifold, together with the Amari–Čencov α-connections, already possesses all the structures of spacetime geometry — metric, curvature, geodesics, parallel transport, dual structures. Obidi identifies the **α = 0 Levi-Civita connection** of the entropic metric as the physical spacetime connection, making the emergence a structural identification rather than an analogy:

$$\nabla^{(\alpha)}
\overset{\alpha = 0}{\longrightarrow{\hspace{1.5cm}}}
\nabla^{\text{entropic}}_{\text{LC}} \longrightarrow \nabla^{\text{GR}}_{\text{LC}}$$

The first arrow is a theorem of information geometry; the second is Obidi's physical postulate. Spacetime curvature, the metric signature, and causal structure are all **entropic in origin**.

## The Obidi Correspondence Principle (OCP)

The OCP is the **methodological anchor** that prevents ToE from becoming a purely philosophical declaration. It states:

> *Every valid law, equation, or model within ToE must reproduce established physical theories in their appropriate domains of applicability.*

This is not a trivial constraint. Once Obidi asserts that all laws arise from the Entropic Field, the burden of recovering **general relativity, quantum mechanics, thermodynamics, and cosmology** from that foundation becomes inescapable. The OCP converts the conjectures from metaphysical assertions into **testable scientific obligations**: in the limit of smooth, slowly varying entropy, motion must reduce to Newtonian paths; in the high-curvature limit, the entropic field equations must reproduce Einstein's equations; in the low-distinguishability limit, the Born rule and standard quantum mechanics must re-emerge.

## The Obidi Probability Law

Perhaps the most philosophically striking element. From the Hilbert-space decomposition of the wavefunction into two orthogonal sectors:

$$\|\Psi\|^2 = \|\psi_o\|^2 + \|\psi_e\|^2$$

Obidi derives the **conservation identity**:

$$P_o(t) + P_e(t) = 1$$

where $P_o$ is the amplitude in the **coherent (visible) sector** and $P_e$ is the amplitude in the **entropic (invisible) sector**. This is not the **Born rule** — it is a **Pythagorean conservation law in Hilbert space** that *generates* the Born rule as a limit case (when the entropic sector becomes negligible).

The implications are layered:

### - **Probability is ontological, not epistemic.** 
It does not measure ignorance or belief; it measures the physical partition of reality between coherence and entropy. It is observer-independent and conserved.

### - **The Born rule becomes emergent**, not fundamental. 
Standard quantum mechanics is the projection of a deeper entropic geometry onto the coherent sector.

### - **Wavefunction collapse** 
Is reinterpreted as irreversible amplitude transfer into the entropic sector — a physical process, not a measurement postulate.

### - **The black hole information paradox** 
Gains a structural resolution: information is not destroyed but transferred into the entropic sector, where it remains conserved but no longer coherently accessible.

## Philosophical Implications of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE)

### **Ontological inversion.** 
The most fundamental shift is the **reversal of the explanatory hierarchy**. For over a century, physics has treated spacetime and quantum fields as primary and entropy as a secondary statistical descriptor. ToE inverts this: entropy is the ground, and everything else is a projection. This is not merely a change of variables — it changes *what is real*.

### **Probability as a conservation law.** 
The Obidi Probability Law dissolves the long-standing ambiguity between frequentist and Bayesian probability by showing that probability is neither a frequency of outcomes nor a degree of belief, but a **conserved physical quantity** arising from the Hilbert-space geometry of the entropic field. This has direct consequences for the interpretation of quantum mechanics: the Copenhagen interpretation's observer-centric collapse is replaced by a **sector-based ontology** where measurement is entropic stabilization.

### **The visible and invisible.** 
The partition $P_o + P_e = 1$ introduces a physically real **hidden sector** that is not metaphysical but **entropically measurable**. 

This echoes — and formalizes — the ***Schopenhauerian*** distinction between *representation* (the visible, structured world) and *will* (the invisible, driving substrate). Where **Schopenhauer** could only describe this divide metaphorically, Obidi expresses it mathematically.

### **Wheeler's "It from Bit" made rigorous.** 
**John Wheeler's** vision that information is foundational to physical reality lacked a conserved quantity tying information to physical law. The Obidi Probability Law provides exactly that: a conserved, geometrically grounded information quantity that makes information not just foundational but **dynamically real**.

### **Causality redefined.** 
Causality becomes **entropic separability** — events are causally connected when the entropic field distinguishes them. Measurement becomes **entropic stabilization**. These are not metaphorical rewordings but structural claims about what causal and measurement relations *are* in the entropic ontology.

### **The Kolmogorov–Obidi lineage.** 
Obidi situates ToE within a century-long convergence from Kolmogorov's information theory through Shannon, Fisher, and information geometry, arguing that these threads were always pointing toward entropy as the primitive field. The **Kolmogorov–Obidi Law (KOL)** for entropic propagation speed, $c_{\text{ent}} = \sqrt{\kappa / \rho_S}$, is presented as a natural descendant of this lineage.
