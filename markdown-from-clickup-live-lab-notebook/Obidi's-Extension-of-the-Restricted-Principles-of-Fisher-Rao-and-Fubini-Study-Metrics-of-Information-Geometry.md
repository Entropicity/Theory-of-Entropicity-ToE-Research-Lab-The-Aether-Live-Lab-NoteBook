# Obidi's Extension of the Restricted Principles of Fisher-Rao and Fubini-Study Metrics of Information Geometry

Yes, that is an accurate assessment of the core mathematical mechanics underpinning **John Onimisi Obidi’s Theory of Entropicity (ToE).** [1, 2, 3] 

By treating the physical coordinates of spacetime events as completely integrated with information geometry, Obidi fundamentally extends classical and quantum statistical frameworks into a dynamical, pseudo-Riemannian context. [3, 4, 5] 

The exact breakdown of how ToE achieves this extension, moving from restricted statistical models to Lorentzian spacetime metric content, follows a specific structural progression: [3] 

## 1. Breaking the "Restricted" Boundaries
Traditionally, the Fisher–Rao metric (which measures the distinguishability of classical probability distributions) and the Fubini–Study metric (which measures the distinguishability of quantum pure states) are strictly Riemannian and kinematic. They live in an abstract parameter or Hilbert state space, devoid of physical time, gravity, or Lorentzian signatures ($+,-,-,-$). They are passive mathematical tools used to compare statistical data. [3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9] 

Obidi removes these restrictions by making information geometry dynamical. In ToE, the entropic field $S(x)$ is treated as the foundational substrate of the universe. 

Physical events and their coordinates do not just happen on a background grid; they are localized manifestations of the entropic field's changing probability states. [1, 3, 9, 10] 

## 2. The Hybrid Metric-Affine Integration
To unify classical and quantum probability states into physical spacetime, Obidi constructs what he terms a Hybrid Metric-Affine Space. He introduces a generalized induced entropic metric $g_{\mu\nu}^{(\text{ent})}$ that explicitly synthesizes these restricted metrics: [1, 7] 
$$g_{\mu\nu}^{(\text{ent})} = F_{\mu\nu}[S] + \alpha A_{\mu\nu}[S] + \beta Q_{\mu\nu}[S]$$ 

* $F_{\mu\nu}[S]$: Extends the classical Fisher–Rao curvature.
* $Q_{\mu\nu}[S]$: Integrates the quantum Fubini–Study geometric contributions.
* $A_{\mu\nu}[S]$: Deforms the space using Amari–Čencov $\alpha$-connections, allowing the probability distributions to smoothly vary and co-evolve. [7, 11] 

## 3. Emergence of the Lorentzian Spacetime Content
The ultimate leap in ToE is mapping this integrated information-geometric structure onto a four-dimensional Lorentzian manifold. Obidi uses controlled entropy-gradient disformal transformations—driven by the Obidi Action—to shift the signature from a purely statistical Riemannian space to a physical, pseudo-Riemannian Lorentzian space. [1, 4, 5] 

Through this mathematical map, the distances between physical events are no longer defined by a pre-existing spatial grid. Instead, physical spacetime intervals represent the literal "statistical distance" (distinguishability) between varying configurations of the universe's underlying entropic field. Einsteinian gravity and the curvature of the spacetime metric are derived as macroscopic, emergent approximations of this information-geometric workflow. [1, 4, 5, 10]
