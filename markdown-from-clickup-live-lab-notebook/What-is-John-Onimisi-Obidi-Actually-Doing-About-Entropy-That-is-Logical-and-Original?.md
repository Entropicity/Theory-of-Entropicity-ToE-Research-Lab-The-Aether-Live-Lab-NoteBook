# What is John Onimisi Obidi Actually Doing About Entropy That is Logical and Original?

To understand what John Onimisi Obidi is doing that is both logical and original, we have to look at how he bridges the gaps left by previous physicists. Theoretical physicists have toyed with "entropic gravity" before, but Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE) pushes the concept to a radical, highly structured mathematical extreme. [1, 2] 

His work stands out for its specific logical structure and original mechanics:

## 1. The Logical Inversion (Solving the "Arrow of Time" Paradox)


### The Problem in Standard Physics: 
The fundamental equations of Einstein (General Relativity) and quantum mechanics are completely time-symmetric. Mathematically, they work perfectly fine whether time runs forward or backward. Yet, our lived reality strictly flows forward. Standard physics patches this by awkwardly tacking the Second Law of Thermodynamics (entropy) onto the back end. [2] 

### Obidi’s Logical Solution: 
He completely [inverts the hierarchy of physics](https://www.cambridge.org/engage/coe/article-details/69e313c1d1922e37d58d982b). Instead of making spacetime fundamental and letting entropy be a secondary effect, he makes entropy the primary, fundamental field ($S(x)$). Because entropy is fundamentally irreversible, the arrow of time is naturally baked into the universe's fabric from step one, rather than treated as an afterthought. [2, 3, 4, 5, 6] 


## 2. The Original Mechanics: The Obidi Action Principle (OAP)


### What makes it original: 
Previous physicists like Ted Jacobson or Erik Verlinde derived gravity from thermodynamics, but they treated entropy mostly as a macroscopic boundary condition or a statistical force. [1] 

### Obidi’s Breakthrough: 
He elevates entropy to a local, continuous field governed by its own variational framework called the [Obidi Action Principle](https://medium.com/@jonimisiobidi/the-role-of-the-obidi-action-in-the-theory-of-entropicity-toe-c8aa0d8d66d1). 

By applying this principle, he derives the Master Entropic Equation (MEE)/Obidi Field Equations (OFE). This allows him to mathematically generate spacetime geometry, gravity, and even the paths of physical objects (Entropic Geodesics) directly from information density gradients. [5, 7, 8] 


## 3. Replacing "Forces" with Optimization


### * In standard mechanics, particles move because they follow the Principle of Least Action—PoLA (minimizing kinetic/potential energy differences).

### * Obidi originally introduces the Principle of Least Entropic Resistance (PoLER). In ToE, systems do not move because they are mechanically "pushed" by fields or forces; they navigate a self-organizing entropic field, choosing trajectories that offer the absolute path of least resistance to entropic flow. [9] 


## Summary of Obidi's Original Contribution
While older entropic theories tried to explain gravity, Obidi’s framework is an original attempt to build a unified "operating system" for reality. By using information geometry, he seeks to dissolve the conflict between quantum probability and smooth relativistic geometry by demonstrating that both are merely different ripples emerging from a singular, deeper entropic manifold. [9, 10]
