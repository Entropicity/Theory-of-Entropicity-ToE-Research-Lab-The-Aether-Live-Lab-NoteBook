# The Obidi Postulate

The-Obidi-Postulate.md

The Obidi Postulate (or the single fundamental postulate of the Theory of Entropicity) is the foundational premise introduced by researcher **John Onimisi Obidi.** It states that entropy is the absolute, primary field and causal substrate of the universe, rather than a secondary statistical byproduct of physical systems.

Within his framework, known as the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), this concept completely reverses the traditional hierarchy of physics:

## Traditional View: 
Spacetime and matter exist fundamentally → They interact → Information is gathered → Entropy is calculated as a measure of disorder.

## Obidi's Postulate: 
Entropy (S(x)) exists fundamentally → It generates differences and contrasts (distinguishability) → Distinguishability constructs information geometry → Spacetime, matter, and gravity emerge as macroscopic illusions from this entropic field.


## Core Implications of the Postulate

### 1. The Nature of a Point: 
Under this postulate, a physical point in spacetime is not an empty geometric location. It is defined as an entropic event—a specific point of distinguishability created entirely by the underlying entropic manifold.


### 2. Derivation of Relativity: 
Obidi argues that the core tenets of Einstein’s Theory of Relativity—including the universal speed limit (c)—are not arbitrary starting constants. Instead, the speed of light is reinterpreted as the maximum informational processing speed (or refresh rate) at which the entropic field can causally reorganize itself.



### 3. Gravity as Pressure: 
Gravity is removed as a fundamental force or a native warping of spacetime. Instead, it is treated as a macroscopic entropic pressure pushing toward statistical equilibrium.



***Note: While the Theory of Entropicity attempts to reconcile general relativity and quantum mechanics by replacing traditional spacetime with information geometry, it remains an independent, audacious theoretical model undergoing rigorous mathematical development and is yet to be widely accepted within mainstream physics.***

The Obidi Postulate (often referred to as the fundamental postulate of the Theory of Entropicity or ToE) states that no physical interaction in the universe can be instantaneous, because every process requires a finite time interval proportional to an irreducible entropic cost. [1] 

Formulated by theoretical researcher John Onimisi Obidi, this speculative framework in modern theoretical physics suggests that entropy is not merely a statistical byproduct of disorder, but rather the primary, foundational field of reality from which space, time, and gravity emerge. [2] 


## Core Concepts of the Postulate

### Reversal of Physical Hierarchy: 
Traditional physics views space and time as the primary stage where matter and entropy evolve. The Obidi framework reverses this order, positioning entropy as ontologically prior and fundamental, with spacetime acting as an emergent macroscopic projection. [2, 3] 

### Entropic Latency: 
Because every state change or information transfer must "pay" a minimal cost in entropy, true instantaneous actions (such as idealized quantum collapses) are forbidden. Every interaction has a built-in temporal delay or "latency". [1] 

### Emergence of the Speed of Light (c): 
Rather than treating the speed of light as a fundamental geometric limit (as in Einstein's relativity), the Obidi framework derives c as the maximum throughput or rate at which the universal entropic field can reorganize itself and transfer information. [1, 4] 


Note: The Theory of Entropicity and its foundational postulates represent an emerging, non-mainstream theoretical proposal still undergoing peer scrutiny and mathematical development rather than a consensus view in standard physics. [5, 6]
