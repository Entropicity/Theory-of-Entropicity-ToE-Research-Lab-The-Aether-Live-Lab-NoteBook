In this markdown Volume, I preserve the 
Correspondence between Mathematical Physicist **Daniel Moses Alemoh** and **John Onimisi Obidi** on the Conceptual, Philosophical and Mathematical Foundations of Obidi's Theory of Entropicity (ToE). 

**This is the basis and foundation of the now famous Alemoh-Obidi-Correspondence (AOC) of the Theory of Entropicity (ToE), published as Letter IC (Monograph, Volume I) in the Theory of Entropicity (ToE) Living Review Letters Series (ToE LRLS).**
