
# John Onimisi Obidi to Isah Salami on the physical and the spiritual, and on the Nature of God as a monistic and wholistic dual: On the Physical and Spiritual and the Unity of God—Physical and Spiritual Principles and the Nature of God 

On-the-Physical-and-Spiritual-and-the-Unity-of-God-Physical-and-Spiritual-Principles-and-the-Nature-of-God.md 

I always believe that as we have physical principles, so do we have spiritual principles...

The issue and problem and challenge is that  we often don't know the spiritual principles and we claim we know them.....

As far as I am concerned, physical principles and spiritual principles are both scientific....once we are really able to discover and understand spiritual principles, we can apply them with certainty in the same way we are now able to apply physical principles to our benefits in all the sciences.... for God is our ultimate scientist and engineer! God is both physical and spiritual!
